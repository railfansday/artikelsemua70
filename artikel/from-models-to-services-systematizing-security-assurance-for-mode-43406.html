From Models to Services: Systematizing Security Assurance for Model-as-a-Service

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 27 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (167 words, 📖 Standard)

It has been revealed that arXiv:2611.34623v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Large language models are mainly delivered as cloud services, in which the assets of different principals are coupled within a single service pipeline, while the related security research is scattered across several fields with differing threat models. This paper systematizes this research from a service perspective. We decompose Model-as-a-Service (MaaS) into a five-layer stack and five trust boundaries, define six adversary classes and six security goals, use them to code 120 studies, and evaluate 50 defenses under a unified set of criteria. The results show that 44.2% of the studies concentrate on behavioral integrity, while service integrity, availability and cost, and accountability together account for only 13.3%. Defenses are evaluated on production services at less than half the rate of attacks, and none of the cryptographic or hardware mechanisms that protect against the provider can be adopted unilaterally by customers. Finally, we propose a research agenda by comparison with current standards and regulations.
