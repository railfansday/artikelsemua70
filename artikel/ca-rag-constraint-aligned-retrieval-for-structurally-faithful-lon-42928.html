CA-RAG: Constraint-Aligned Retrieval for Structurally Faithful Long-Form Generation

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 27 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (142 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

arXiv:2611.33497v1 Announce Type: latest Abstract: Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) typically optimises evidence selection for semantic relevance, whereas compliance-sensitive long-form generation also requires evidence supporting explicit structural obligations. Semantically relevant retrieval may therefore omit information needed for mandatory downstream clauses. We propose Constraint-Aligned RAG (CA-RAG), a retrieval-conditioning framework that augments section-specific queries with ontology-derived structural constraints and policy-grounded knowledge-graph relations before evidence selection. Retrieval-only evaluation with manually annotated evidence highlights consistent improvements across retrieval budgets. At $k=6$, CA-RAG increases Precision@$k$ from $0.5268$ to $0.5692$, pooled Recall@$k$ from $0.5402$ to $0.5950$, and F1@$k$ from $0.5389$ to $0.5672$ over standard RAG. Generation experiments further show improvements in compliance and reliability in several model--domain settings, while generation-only constraints achieve higher clause coverage but lower overall compliance and reliability. These results indicate that structurally aligned retrieval can improve evidence sufficiency in compliance-sensitive long-form generation.
