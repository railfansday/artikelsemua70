Industry News: AWS launches credit splitting and transfers

🌐 Source: Amazon • 📅 17 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 2 min read (302 words, 📖 Standard)

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

AWS now supports credit splitting and transfers, enabling customers to divide any AWS credit into multiple smaller credits and transfer them to other linked accounts within their payer account. Previously, AWS applied credits starting with the owning account, then shared any excess with other accounts based on their usage, making it difficult for customers with large credits to direct precise amounts to specific teams or business units. FinOps teams can now split credits by dollar amounts, percentages, or even splits, and transfer each portion to the team that earned it.

According to recent reports, credit splitting and transfers eliminate hours of monthly manual chargeback work for FinOps teams and provide predictable credit budgets per team or business unit. Each split credit inherits the same expiration date and service eligibility as the original, and split credits retain sharing settings by default so unused balances flow to other eligible accounts. Accounts receive first-priority consumption for transferred credits.

Recent findings suggest that credit splitting and transfers compose naturally with existing credit features, including credit-level sharing and credit pausing. After a split, customers have full control over each resulting credit. For example, customers can split and share one credit across all intended accounts so multiple teams benefit, while restricting another split credit to a single account. They can also pause a credit earmarked for a future project, then resume it when needed.

It has been revealed that you can split any credit type via the AWS Billing Console, CLI, or API. Partially consumed credits can be split, with the split applying to remaining balance only. Each credit family supports up to 100 splits, with a $100 minimum per split credit. Splits are permanent with no undo operation.

Credit splitting and transfers are available in all AWS commercial regions at no additional cost.
