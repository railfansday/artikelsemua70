The MoneyHelper Pensions Dashboard, a government-backed tool that will let savers view all their pensions in one place, will go live to the public in summer 2027, with pension schemes now under pressure to make sure the data behind it is accurate before launch.

Pensions minister Torsten Bell MP shared the timeline at a town hall event run by the Money and Pensions Service (MaPS) on 19 November 2026.

He told delegates that the first dashboard is now under a year away. He also confirmed that the Government still intends to give the industry six months’ warning of the exact public launch date.

The announcement follows the 31 October 2026 deadline for pension providers and schemes to join the dashboards ecosystem.

According to recent reports, more than 2,400 schemes and providers are now linked to the infrastructure, which covers upwards of 75 million records alongside millions of State Pension records. MaPS chief executive Oliver Morley used the event to thank the industry for meeting this connection target.

Sources indicate that consumer testing has reached its second phase. The number of consumers is being raised step by step so that the MoneyHelper dashboard can be refined as feedback comes in. Delegates at the town hall heard the newest findings from this testing.

MaPS also commissioned Ipsos to study how people use dashboards, and the research was presented at the event. People responded very positively. Some 75% said they were satisfied with the experience overall, and over 80% said they would return to the dashboard.

Once live, the service will give people a single view of their pension savings for the first time. The aim is to help them plan for retirement and manage their wider finances with better information.

The town hall gathered representatives from industry, government and regulators to review progress on the programme.

Government pensions minister Torsten Bell MP noted, “Pensions Dashboard are on track to transform how people understand their pensions. Being able to see your pensions in one place will give millions of savers something too many have missed out on for too long: a clear picture of their finances that helps them proposal ahead with confidence.

“Thanks to hard yards that have been put in across the industry, we can now look ahead to launch. Behind every pension is someone thinking about their future, and we want them to feel confident their pots are easy to find, secure and actually working to provide them with an income in retirement.”.

According to recent reports, money and Pensions Service CEO Oliver Morley added, “The milestones we have now reached with pensions dashboards are a testament to the dedication shown right across industry, government and regulators. Delivering dashboards is a partnership, and our town hall event celebrates what has been achieved.

“We are now building momentum towards the launch of the MoneyHelper Pensions Dashboard in Summer 2027. This is a once-in-a-generation opportunity to help people better understand and connect with their pensions.

“By continuing to work together, ensuring people can access high-quality pensions information and striving to deliver the best possible user experience, we will deliver a tool that helps people feel more informed and in control of their retirement planning.”.

The Pensions Regulator executive director for market oversight Ben Gunnee added, “Having millions of member records connected to pensions dashboards is a significant step towards members being able to find their pensions information in one place. But this is not the finish line.

“As we look ahead to launch, schemes should take the necessary steps to ensure the information members see is accurate, complete and up-to-date, and be ready to support those who engage with them, so members can reap the benefits of dashboards.”.
