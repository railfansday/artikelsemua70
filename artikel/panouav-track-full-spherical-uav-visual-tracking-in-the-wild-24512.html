PanoUAV-Track: Full-Spherical UAV Visual Tracking in the Wild

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 23 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (174 words, 📖 Standard)

arXiv:2611.27383v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Existing UAV tracking benchmarks predominantly rely on perspective cameras with limited fields of view. Omnidirectional cameras enable full-spherical observation, yet tracking from moving UAV platforms remains largely unexplored. Unlike ground-level panoramic tracking, UAV-mounted omnidirectional cameras observe targets across both azimuth and elevation, introducing geometric challenges under equirectangular projection (ERP). To investigate this setting, we capture approximately \textbf{40 hours of real-world 8K panoramic UAV video} and construct \textbf{PanoUAV-Track} through temporal clip extraction, target annotation, and the integration of simulated sequences. The resulting benchmark comprises 1,895 sequences, 507,961 frames, and 2,872 annotated objects, covering real-world daytime and nighttime scenes alongside simulation, and supporting both visual and language-based target specification. Through extensive evaluations, we reveal a clear \textbf{perspective-to-spherical} generalization gap and identify tracking degradation associated with latitude-dependent distortion and ERP seam discontinuity. To address these challenges, we propose \textbf{PanoUAV-Tracker (PUTrack)}, a geometry-aware tracking framework combining latitude-adaptive search and seam-aware periodic sampling. PUTrack improves zero-shot and in-domain tracking, particularly for seam-crossing targets. Code and benchmark resources will be available\footnote{\textcolor{tabpink}{https://libingyu01.github.io/PanoUAV-Track/}}.
