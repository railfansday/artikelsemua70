Learning to Assimilate: Amortized Control for Autoregressive Diffusion

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 23 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (149 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

arXiv:2510.06637v4 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: Autoregressive diffusion models (ARDMs) offer expressive priors for forecasting chaotic dynamics. However, maintaining consistency with sparse observations over long rollouts can require substantial test-time computation. We introduce Control-Augmented Data Assimilation (CADA), which trains a lightweight controller on top of a frozen ARDM to steer predictions toward future observations. Using a KL-regularized stochastic optimal control objective, the controller learns from short autoregressive rollouts to anticipate observations while staying close to the pretrained dynamics. At inference, it injects corrections at each denoising step without per-forecast optimization. Across two chaotic PDE benchmarks, CADA improves long-horizon accuracy, stability, and physical fidelity under sparse observations. On compact ERA5, it outperforms diffusion-based baselines in most regimes with over $10\times$ faster inference than conditional diffusion baselines. Applied to frozen GenCast, CADA also reduces temperature errors across 12--48-hour forecasts with spatially sparse observations. Code is available at https://github.com/mandt-lab/cada.
