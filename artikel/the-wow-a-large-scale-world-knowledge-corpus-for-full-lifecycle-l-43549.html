The WoW: A Large-Scale World Knowledge Corpus for Full-Lifecycle LLM Training

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 27 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (131 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

In a recent development, arXiv:2611.13487v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: Existing Chinese educational corpora often suffer from fragmented coverage and a lack of systematic organization. In this paper, we propose WoW (\textbf{Wo}rld Kno\textbf{w}ledge), a large-scale dataset designed for the full-cycle training of foundation models. The construction of this dataset is driven by an Educational Knowledge Graph, which serves as a taxonomic backbone for the systematic collection and completion of missing educational modules across all levels. Compared to previous versions, this iteration provides enhanced systematicity, superior quality, and a larger scale, while incorporating multimodal signals and supporting every stage of model development. Experiments demonstrate that WoW significantly improves model performance across diverse benchmarks. To facilitate community research, we further provide an interactive platform for graph-based data selection and retrieval.
