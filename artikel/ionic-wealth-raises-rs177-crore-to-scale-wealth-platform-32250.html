Ionic Wealth, a technology-driven wealth management platform owned by Angel One, has secured Rs177 crore ($19m) in a strategic funding round to power its next stage of expansion.

The round was backed by Angel One alongside a group of marquee families, and brings the total amount raised by the tech giant to around Rs466 crore ($50m).

All investors from the earlier round returned for this one. The investor base includes prominent business families, Bollywood personalities, FinTech entrepreneurs, global CXOs, board advisors and media houses.

The investment follows a period of rapid growth. Within two years, Ionic Wealth has pushed its assets under management (AUM) beyond Rs16,000 crore. It now serves more than 2,900 high-net-worth and ultra-high-net-worth clients across over 12 cities, with a team of more than 100 private bankers.

Ionic Wealth plans to use the fresh capital to strengthen its portfolio-led proposition and global capabilities, bring AI into its WealthTech experience, grow its UHNI business and widen its presence both in India and overseas.

Recent findings suggest that the company described the funds as patient capital, which will let it build on its early bets. These include using technology to scale its HNI offering, advancing global investing through GIFT City, opening up fractional access through the Accredited Investor framework and SIFs, and creating a distinct UHNI offering for India’s leading families.

It has been revealed that operating under the Ionic Wealth brand, Angel One Wealth Limited is a wholly owned subsidiary of Angel One Limited, one of India’s major FinTech platforms with a user base of over 40 million. Ionic Wealth is an omnichannel WealthTech platform that aims to rethink wealth management for India’s emerging HNIs and UHNIs.

According to recent reports, it gives clients access to exclusive investment opportunities across asset classes, a round-the-clock digital experience through its app, and domain specialists who build strategic plans suited to each client’s wealth journey. The firm uses technology to generate sharper insights, boost team productivity and support scale.
