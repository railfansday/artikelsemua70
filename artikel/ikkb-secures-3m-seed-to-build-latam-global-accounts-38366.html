📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

IKKB, a financial management platform for companies operating across borders, has closed a $3m seed round to secure regulatory licences and develop upcoming products for its multi-currency business account.

Recent findings suggest that the investment was led by Canary. According to the announcement, the capital will help IKKB obtain the licences it needs to operate as a regulated institution in each of its markets. It will also fund further products built on top of the core account. The firm already has licensing applications under way in Brazil, the US and Canada.

Canary noted it first met co-founders Edward Konig and Rafael Katalan in August 2025 and tracked their progress for over a year before investing. The investor said the pair learned swiftly, made difficult decisions with long-term goals in mind and achieved a great deal on limited funding. Canary believes a growing number of companies now operate internationally from launch, while the financial infrastructure supporting them remains slow, costly and fragmented.

Sources indicate that IKKB’s offering has grown considerably in a short time. Just over a year ago, its product had a single function, converting PIX payments into US dollars. It has since become a multi-currency account for businesses, holding balances in BRL, USD, EUR and MXN. The account connects to local payment rails in Brazil, the US, Europe and Mexico, and offers corporate cards in BRL and USD. The team developed the platform internally on modest capital and prioritised regulatory work from the outset.

According to recent reports, the firm is focused on giving Latin American businesses a global account. Firms can manage international payments, foreign exchange and treasury from a single platform. The service operates on stablecoin rails and applies AI to provide additional services alongside the account. IKKB offers accounts, cards, currency exchange, payments and treasury management.

IKKB was founded to reduce the friction and expense of moving money across borders for both businesses and individuals. Its mission is to make international transfers as straightforward as moving funds between accounts, offering transparent FX and payments across all rails without the hidden spreads and bureaucracy associated with traditional banks.
