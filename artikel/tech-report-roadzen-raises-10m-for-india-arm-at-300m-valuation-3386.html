Tech Report: Roadzen raises $10m for India arm at $300m valuation

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Roadzen, which applies AI to insurance and mobility, has secured roughly $10m (₹94 crore) for its Indian subsidiary, Roadzen AI Limited, which is now valued at about $300m post-money on a stand-alone basis.

The round is being raised entirely as common equity. It carries no warrants or preferred rights, and the latest shares rank equally with those held by the existing shareholder, Roadzen. Because the capital goes into the subsidiary, shareholders in the Nasdaq-listed parent will not be diluted. Family offices and capital markets investors anchored the deal. Several of them had backed the company’s earlier India subsidiary round and raised their commitments this time.

As part of the deal, Roadzen is bringing its worldwide operating businesses together under Roadzen AI Limited, which was previously called Roadzen Technologies Limited. Roadzen Inc. will remain the Nasdaq-listed parent and will keep about 89% of the subsidiary.

The money will go towards the group’s fastest-growing priorities in insurance and mobility. It will also shore up the balance sheet and support the company’s aim of reaching an annual revenue run-rate of $100m this fiscal year.

It has been revealed that in the US, Roadzen plans to add sales capacity to pursue several growth opportunities expected in the near term. In India, its AI-powered claims-to-repair business VehicleCare is expected to increase revenue more than tenfold this year. DrivebuddyAI, which Roadzen describes as the only AIS-184-validated driver-safety platform in the country, is expanding as new safety rules take effect.

According to recent reports, roadzen operates at the intersection of insurance and mobility and uses AI across its products. Its platform helps insurers, automakers, and fleets better predict and prevent risk, automate claims, and improve insurance experiences.
