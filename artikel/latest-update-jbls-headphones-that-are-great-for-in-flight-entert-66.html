In a recent development, if you’re looking to upgrade your headphones before the big wave of Black Friday deals arrives, you can buy JBL’s Tour One M3 for a fresh low price of $149 ($300 off) at Amazon, Best Buy, and directly from JBL. The deal is so good that my colleague John Higgins, The Verge’s audio and TV reviewer, popped in on his day off to tell us just how much of a steal they are at this price.

Sources indicate that JBL’s Tour One M3 stand out largely because they come with the Smart Tx wireless audio transmitter. Frequent fliers will likely appreciate the accessory, which plugs into a plane’s in-flight entertainment system via a 3.5mm cable, allowing you to listen to audio wirelessly. It also works with other devices that lack Bluetooth support but have a headphone jack. The small touchscreen-equipped transmitter also lets you easily adjust the headphones’ noise cancellation, EQ settings, and volume. It can be used to answer calls, too.

Fancy tricks aside, the headphones get the essentials right. According to John, they offer excellent passive noise isolation, and their active noise cancellation is good enough to block out low-frequency sounds, like airplane engines. He also praises their overall sound quality, though he notes that JBL’s default tuning slightly affects midrange clarity, something you can easily fix by adjusting the EQ. They also support high-resolution lossless audio over the included USB-C or 3.5mm cable, plus spatial audio with head tracking. JBL notes they can last up to 70 hours on a single charge with noise cancellation off — enough to get you through multiple long-haul flights — while the USB-C-chargeable transmitter offers up to 18 hours of battery life.
