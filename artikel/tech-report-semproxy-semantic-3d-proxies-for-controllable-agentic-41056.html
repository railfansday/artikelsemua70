Tech Report: SemProxy: Semantic 3D Proxies for Controllable Agentic 3D Modeling

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 27 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (170 words, 📖 Standard)

arXiv:2611.34414v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Coding agents are increasingly capable of generating 3D models, yet enabling users to effectively control the modeling process remains challenging. Existing workflows rely primarily on text prompts and reference images, which often struggle to convey precise spatial intentions and require iterative corrections. We introduce SemProxy, a lightweight intermediate representation that bridges intuitive human interaction and agentic 3D modeling. Instantiated as labeled 3D bounding boxes, semantic 3D proxies allow users to directly specify object parts and spatial arrangements while leaving geometric details to coding agents. These proxies can be serialized into structured text and rendered as images, making them directly accessible to existing coding agents without additional training or modifications. We further develop ProxyStudio, a user interface that integrates text, image references, and interactive 3D proxy editing into agentic modeling workflows. We demonstrate the versatility of SemProxy across object- and scene-level modeling tasks with different coding agents, and evaluate its effectiveness in supporting controllable human-agent collaborative 3D modeling through a user study.
