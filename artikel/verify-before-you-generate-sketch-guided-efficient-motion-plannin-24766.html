Verify Before You Generate: Sketch-Guided Efficient Motion-Planning for Video Generation

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 23 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (238 words, 📖 Standard)

arXiv:2511.17450v2 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: Recent video generation approaches increasingly rely on planning intermediate control signals like object trajectories to improve temporal coherence and motion fidelity. However, these methods mostly employ single-shot plans that are typically limited to simple motion instructions, leading to suboptimal motion quality, or iterative refinement which requires multiple calls to the video generator and incurs high computational cost. To address this trade-off, we propose SketchVerify, a training-free framework whose key insight is to verify motion plans using lightweight video sketches before invoking the expensive video generator. This lightweight planning space enables efficient comparison of multiple candidate trajectories and bounded iterative refinement before a single final video-generation pass. Given a prompt and a reference image, SketchVerify samples multiple candidate trajectories and renders each as a lightweight video sketch by compositing the moving objects over a static background. A vision-language verifier then ranks these sketches according to instruction alignment and visual motion consistency, allowing unsuitable trajectories to be rejected before full video synthesis. The motion plan is iteratively refined until a satisfactory one is identified, then passed to a trajectory-conditioned generator for final synthesis. Experiments on WORLDMODELBENCH and PHYWORLDBENCH demonstrate consistent improvements in instruction following and temporally coherent, visually plausible motion. Using the same ATI-14B generator, SketchVerify improves the aggregate WorldModelBench score from 7.71 to 8.71. Compared with full-video iterative refinement, it achieves a 13.1x planning-stage speedup and a 3.6x end-to-end speedup.
