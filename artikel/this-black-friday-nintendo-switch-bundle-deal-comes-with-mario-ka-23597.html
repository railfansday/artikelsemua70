Industry observers note that savings on the Nintendo Switch 2 are pretty tough to come by, so you definitely won’t want to miss this Black Friday offer if you’re looking to pick up Nintendo’s latest console this holiday season. With this bundle deal from Nintendo available at Amazon, you get the Switch 2, a digital copy of Mario Kart World, and three free months of Nintendo Online for $499.99, which is $50 off the usual price.

According to recent reports, according to ZDNET gaming expert Taylor Clemons, the Switch 2 is the best handheld console on the market right now. It’s serious fun on the massive screen or on the go, and offers some nice improvements over its predecessor, like a larger display and significantly more storage. Plus, the recent price hikes make discounts like this a welcome sight.

That being remarked, it’s key to note that you’re not actually saving any cash on the console itself with this offer. But considering that the digital edition of Mario Kart World lists for $80 on its own, this is still a great opportunity to lock in some savings if you already know it’s a game that you’ll want in your library.

Plus, the included Nintendo Online membership means you’ll be able to race against your friends around the globe. It also comes with plenty of other benefits like a collection of classic Nintendo titles, cloud storage, exclusive discounts, and more.

Even though it’s been over a year since it first hit shelves, deals and discounts on the Switch 2 are pretty rare. $50 off this bundle means you can pick up a free game and Nintendo Online membership with the console, earning it a rating of 3/5 on our system.

Based on Nintendo’s announcement, this deal will run through Dec. 5. But considering how popular this console is, there’s a good chance it will sell out well before then. If you don’t want to miss out on these savings, we’d recommend you get your order in as soon as possible.
