VeRA: Verified Reasoning Data Augmentation at Scale

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 23 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (282 words, 📖 Standard)

It has been revealed that arXiv:2602.13217v3 Announce Type: replace Abstract: Static benchmarks wear out: their problems circulate widely and can enter training data, and their scores saturate as models improve. Renewing a benchmark requires fresh problems with reliable answers, which are expensive to write by hand at competition level. We introduce VeRA (Verified Reasoning Data Augmentation), in which a language model automatically compiles each benchmark problem into an executable specification: a question template, a generator of valid inputs, and a program that computes the answer. Once its programs pass automatic checks, a specification yields many fresh, code-labeled problems at almost no cost, and people are needed only to audit. VeRA-E (equivalent) draws new instances of the same computation. VeRA-H (hardened) modifies the computation to create harder problems where scores saturate; because code computes every answer, the language model that writes them never has to solve them. VeRA-H Pro keeps the hardest of up to five such candidates, as ranked by a judge. Across 16 frontier models, fresh instances of AIME 2024 problems lower accuracy from 84.46% to 70.25%, and every model drops. GSM8K and the less-circulated Beyond-AIME, compiled by the same pipeline, stay essentially unchanged, so the drop reflects familiarity with specific problems, not an artifact of rewriting. On the near-saturated AIME 2024 II problems, VeRA-H Pro lowers accuracy from 84.91% to 58.57%. Expert audits back the labels, with all 220 audited fresh instances accepted and 95.1% of hardened candidates usable after repair. Compiling all 1,319 GSM8K problems cost about USD 100. VeRA thus makes benchmarks renewable by construction: wherever code can compute the answer, a benchmark can be refreshed whenever its problems leak or models catch up.
