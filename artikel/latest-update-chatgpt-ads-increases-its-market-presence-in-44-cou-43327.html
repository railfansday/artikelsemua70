📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Aleph selected as ChatGPT Ads’ exclusive reseller in 44 countries to offer local invoicing, in-person support and local market expertise to brands and agencies.

Starting today, brands and agencies in 44 countries can contract, pay and get local support for ChatGPT Ads through Aleph, OpenAI’s exclusive reseller. Aleph offers local advertisers in these countries an additional buying option alongside our existing global technology partners⁠ and our self-service ChatGPT Ads Manager⁠(opens in a new window).

The reseller agreement covers advertisers based in the following 44 markets:.

Today’s announcement does not impact any advertisers ability to target any of these countries in a campaign. Advertisers globally can continue to create and run campaigns targeting any country where ChatGPT Ads are available⁠(opens in a newly introduced window).

Advertisers and agencies in these markets who are interested in buying ChatGPT Ads from a local entity or local salesforce, can contact Aleph’s local teams⁠(opens in a upcoming window) to learn more. Aleph offers local contracting and invoicing in local currency, payment terms, onboarding, campaign support and training.

According to recent reports, advertisers and agencies interested in buying ads directly can continue to buy through Ads Manager. Advertisers and agencies interested in buying through global technology partners in these markets can continue to do so.

Over one hundred thousand marketers have advertised on ChatGPT, and we continue to improve the platform based on what we learn from people and businesses. Today’s announcement represents additional local ways to purchase ChatGPT Ads across these markets.

According to recent reports, we’ve made significant progress in ten months, but we are still early in building the full potential of ChatGPT Ads. As we expand into latest markets, and develop new formats, optimization tools, and measurement solutions, we’ll remain focused on broadening access to powerful AI, protecting the trust people place in ChatGPT, and helping businesses achieve their goals.

It has been revealed that *OpenAI models and ChatGPT are not available to owners in mainland China or Hong Kong, so ChatGPT Ads are not available in-market. Businesses based in these markets can advertise to ChatGPT users in markets where Ads are available through authorized advertising partners, subject to applicable eligibility requirements.
