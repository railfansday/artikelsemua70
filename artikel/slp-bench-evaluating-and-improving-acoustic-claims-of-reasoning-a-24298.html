SLP-Bench: Evaluating and Improving Acoustic Claims of Reasoning Audio Language Models on Clinical Speech

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 23 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (211 words, 📖 Standard)

According to recent reports, arXiv:2611.27810v1 Announce Type: fresh Abstract: Audio language models (ALMs) can answer clinical questions from speech, outperforming baselines built on handcrafted acoustic features. Reasoning ALMs generate a chain of thought that refers to properties of the speaker's voice before predicting. Each statement the model makes about the voice needs to be checked against clinicians' perceptual ratings of the same recording. On clinical speech, existing evaluations score only the final answer. We introduce Speech Language Pathologist Bench (SLP-Bench), a benchmark grading each acoustic claim in the chain of thought against clinicians' perceptual ratings of the same recording. It covers five clinician-rated datasets and 25 perceptual dimensions, and assesses claims for factuality, stability under resampling, and consistency across reworded and different questions. Across ten open-weight reasoning ALMs evaluated zero-shot, claim balanced accuracy ranges from 0.52 to 0.58, claims change under resampling and rewording, and the most consistent models are not the most factual. Fine-tuning a 7B ALM with GRPO on a reward for claims that agree with clinicians raises its claim balanced accuracy from 0.54 to 0.60 and its mean kappa from 0.39 to 0.61, while preserving answer accuracy on per-dimension questions. SLP-Bench is available upon request, subject to approval by the providers of each dataset.
