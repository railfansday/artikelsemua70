Evernote⁠(opens in a new window) helps people capture, organize, and retrieve the knowledge they build over time. For longtime users, that collection of notes and ideas becomes a “second brain,” growing more useful as they add to it.

“The challenge is helping new owners see enough value to become paying customers. We need to show people what Evernote can do for them and give them a clear reason to start using it.”.

Sources indicate that that challenge is also a question of relevance. Many people already know Evernote by name, but may not know how the product has evolved. As it develops AI capabilities, the manufacturer wants to show prospective people how its approach to capturing and organizing knowledge fits the way they work today.

ChatGPT Ads offered a way to introduce that evolving product to people already using AI to create and work with information.

In a recent development, evernote’s starting point was a practical need: keeping information useful after it has been created. People working with conversations, notes, and transcriptions may want a place to organize that material and find it again. The team saw a natural role for Evernote in that workflow.

Its initial ads highlighted Evernote as a place to save and organize notes and transcriptions, drawing on messaging from strong-performing search campaigns. That gave the team a practical starting point for testing how those use cases resonated in ChatGPT.

Ads directed people to Evernote’s website, including its homepage, with conversion campaigns optimized for new-account registrations. Using its own web analytics and subscription records, Evernote could follow that initial interest through to a free trial and a first subscription payment.

Evernote built its core campaigns—its primary group of ChatGPT Ads campaigns—around a single product feature. In the first month, around 25% of desktop visits from those campaigns resulted in a upcoming account, giving the team an early indication that people were willing to try the product.2.

Evernote is treating those first-month results as encouraging and directional while it tests whether the performance can hold as the channel grows.

Industry observers note that evernote’s next step is to make the connection between its product and AI workflows more tangible. The team looks to explore additional use cases and develop creative and dedicated landing pages that show people how Evernote can fit into their own work.

According to recent reports, “We saw a natural fit between people using ChatGPT and what Evernote offers. What stood out in the early results was how well upcoming people converted to paid subscriptions. For us, that was an encouraging sign that we were reaching people who found real value in the product.”.
