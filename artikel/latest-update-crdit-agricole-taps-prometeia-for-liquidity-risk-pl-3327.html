📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Prometeia, a provider of asset and liability management (ALM) and liquidity risk technology for Tier 1 banks across EMEA, has been chosen by Crédit Agricole to supply and roll out a liquidity risk management platform spanning the entire banking group.

In a recent development, under the agreement, Prometeia will act as Crédit Agricole’s strategic partner on the programme, which will cover both regulatory reporting and financial steering. Crédit Agricole ranks as Europe’s second-largest banking group and the ninth-largest globally when measured by total assets.

In a recent development, the group will use Prometeia’s integrated liquidity risk analysis platform to calculate a range of managerial and regulatory liquidity measures. These include the Liquidity Coverage Ratio (LCR), the Net Stable Funding Ratio (NSFR) and the Additional Liquidity Monitoring Metrics (ALMM).

The system will automatically bring together data drawn from various perimeters across the group, supplying the reports submitted to the European Central Bank and to France’s Autorité de contrôle prudentiel et de résolution (ACPR) through one unified analytical framework.

It has been revealed that the deal comes shortly after Prometeia introduced a branch in Paris, which it describes as a strategic hub designed to serve its expanding customer base in one of Europe’s most active financial markets. The Paris opening builds on the firm’s Luxembourg branch, launched in 2025, and a representative office in Frankfurt that opened in early 2026. Together, these moves form part of the group’s strategy to establish itself as a go-to partner across Europe’s major financial centres.

It has been revealed that prometeia head of risk management area Andrea Partesotti said, “Being selected by Crédit Agricole for a programme of this magnitude is a strong recognition of our growing presence in the market of ALM solutions for Tier1 banking groups. What makes this initiative distinctive is the combination of scale, analytical complexity and integration challenges.”.
