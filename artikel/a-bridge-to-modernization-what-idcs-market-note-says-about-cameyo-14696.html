This IDC Market Note contains an analysis on how Cameyo by Google, a virtual app delivery (VAD) solution, helps organizations deliver any legacy application over the web.

Organizations are increasingly adopting generative and agentic AI as they look for ways to unlock more business value. Yet for many IT leaders, business-critical legacy desktop apps stand in the way—too essential to retire, too complex and costly to rewrite—yet unequipped for the security challenges and productivity opportunities of the AI era.

That doesn’t have to block your modernization strategy. By delivering Windows and legacy applications directly inside the Chrome browser, Cameyo by Google connects legacy workflows to Chrome Enterprise, ChromeOS, and Gemini—with zero replatforming required.

In a recent IDC Market Note, “Cameyo by Google: A Modern Bridge Technology Platform in Digital Workspace Transformation,” lead analyst Filippo Vanara examines how Cameyo reduces deployment complexity while enhancing security and lowering costs. Here are five of our key takeaways from the IDC Market Note:.

Enterprise modernization barriers make rewriting or replatforming legacy applications unfeasible for many organizations. The IDC report notes that "Cameyo is positioned as a strategic bridge between legacy application environments and modern, AI-driven digital workspaces." By connecting legacy systems to modern, AI-enhanced digital workspaces, Cameyo enables organizations to safeguard their existing technology investments.

It has been revealed that 2. Instant AI and security upgrades with Gemini and Chrome Enterprise.

In a recent development, moving legacy apps into the browser immediately surrounds them with modern capabilities they were never built to support. With Cameyo, legacy workflows run right alongside built-in enterprise AI like Gemini in Chrome—giving employees safe, managed AI tools instead of pushing them toward unmanaged "shadow AI." At the same time, apps can inherit Chrome’s advanced threat protections and Data Loss Prevention (DLP) policies through Chrome Enterprise Premium, which the IDC Market Note highlights "This enables organizations to extend AI capabilities and modern security controls to legacy workflows.".

3. Protecting your organizations from cyberattacks.

In a recent development, traditional VDI utilizes open VPNs and public server ports that leave organizations vulnerable to cyberattacks. According to the IDC Market Note, "As part of its value proposition, security is central to Cameyo. In fact, the platform adopts a zero trust architecture, isolating users, sessions, and resources from the corporate network.” Additionally, "the use of non-persistent infrastructure and a simplified architecture reduces the overall attack surface.".

4. Slashing operational and virtualization costs.

Industry observers note that according to IDC's 2025 EMEA CloudOps Survey (n = 509) findings, 89% of organizations in Europe estimated that they are right now wasting their public cloud spending. When companies use Cameyo as opposed to complex virtual desktops, they consistently realize reduced costs. According to the IDC Market Note, "Cost is another core value proposition for Cameyo as it emphasizes its potential to deliver significant cost savings compared with traditional VDI and DaaS solutions: up to 54% reduction in total cost of ownership (TCO) when transitioning from legacy environments.".

Industry observers note that 5. Phased modernization across endpoints.

In a recent development, according to the IDC Market Note, IDC believes Cameyo represents an important evolution within the broader VCC market, specifically reflecting a shift from desktop-centric virtualization to application-centric delivery models. In this context, the platform functions as a bridge technology, enabling enterprises to modernize incrementally rather than requiring full application replatforming. This aligns with IDC's view that most organizations will pursue phased modernization strategies, maintaining legacy software while gradually transitioning to cloud-native architectures.” By delivering legacy applications as Progressive Web Apps (PWAs), Cameyo helps businesses modernize without being tethered to a specific device or operating system. When paired with ChromeOS Flex, Cameyo can ensure seamless access to those legacy apps on older devices, extending existing hardware lifespans and reducing capital expenditures.

Adopting AI doesn't mean leaving legacy applications stranded. True workplace modernization integrates existing, business-critical software into a secure, unified AI ecosystem—without requiring complex app rewrites or costly migrations.
