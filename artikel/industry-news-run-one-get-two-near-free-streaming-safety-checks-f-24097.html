Industry News: Run One, Get Two: Near-Free Streaming Safety Checks for Local LLMs

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 23 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (136 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Recent findings suggest that arXiv:2611.26975v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Local LLM assistants decode for one user at a time, so decoding is bound by memory bandwidth and leaves compute idle. Streaming safety checks today trade accuracy against cost. A dedicated guard model is accurate but nearly doubles the latency per token, while a hidden-state probe is cheap but less accurate. We present ToRA, an adapter that runs a second model as an extra row in the generator's decode step, sharing its weights and KV cache. Dual-row kernels read each weight once for both rows. On Qwen3-0.6B, ToRA matches or beats a dedicated streaming guard, with higher F1 on 12 of 17 benchmarks. It detects 11.8 points more unsafe responses than the probe at a similar cost, under 4% added latency per token.
