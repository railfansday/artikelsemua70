When we look at the best deals for Black Friday, we’re not just looking at prices — we’re looking for products we’ve tested and recommend that are actually worth buying. ZDNET’s Lab Awards series takes the rigorous testing from our Louisville, Kentucky-based lab and combines it with our decades of hands-on testing expertise to give you the data you need to make informed shopping decisions on everything from robot vacuums to phones to VPNs.

Also: Black Friday Live Blog 2026.

Many of the products we’ve tested in the lab are flagship devices from substantial-name brands, so they don’t come cheap. But during the Black Friday shopping weekend, several of our 2026 Lab Award winners are on sale with significant discounts. Here are five of the best deals we’ve found.

It has been revealed that in our lab testing, the AirPods Pro 3 had the highest overall average in reducing full-spectrum noise at 35.81 dB. They were the best at reducing both mid (36.16 dB) and treble (30.17 dB) frequency bands.

Also: We put 8 pairs of earbuds to the test in our lab – this pair won best ANC.

We tested the earbuds by setting up a human‑like, soft, silicone-molded “ear” as well as high-quality couplers (microphones) to act as the ears in our testing.

In a recent development, based on our test results for how long each power bank took to charge to 100%, the Cuktech 15 Air was the fastest, charging in only 54 minutes. It also took only about 26 minutes to charge to 50%, which is great for those who want a quick top-up.

Also: We tested 10 power banks in our lab – and this one provided the fastest charge.

In addition to our lab testing, in our hands-on testing, ZDNET contributor Adrian Kingsley0Hughes noted that the Cuktech 15 Air supports pass-through charging, so it can charge itself while powering other devices.

In Standard Dynamic (SDR) mode — the traditional video format that is enabled by default on most TVs — the LG C6 had the most accurate color reproduction.

Also: We tested 7 TVs for color accuracy and we found two winners.

In a recent development, the picture quality is top-notch with the LG C6, offering some of the boldest and brightest colors we’ve seen in an OLED at our lab. The refreshed panel provides better contrast by controlling color bleed and backlight blooming, reducing the risk of a halo effect around objects set against a dark background.

Based on our lab testing, this Wi-Fi router maintained the strongest signal across two out of the three D&I bands we tested in the  2.4 GHz band. Its dBm range stayed in the “good” rating for medium D&I (-38.4 dBm) and high D&I (-46.9 dBm), meaning it had a strong signal that makes it reliable for streaming, video calls, and browsing.

Also: We tested 15 Wi-Fi 7 routers in our lab, and this one had the widest coverage.

With tri-band Wi-Fi delivering speeds up to 19Gbps, it has the bandwidth to handle 4K streaming, online gaming, large file transfers, and video conferencing simultaneously without bottlenecking.

Recent findings suggest that across the board, there was one clear winner among the laptops tested in the 5.0 GHz band: the Lenovo ThinkPad X1. It scored highest across all three D&I bands, and had a weighted average of 702.36 Mbps.

Also: We tested 7 business laptops for the fastest Wi-Fi speed – the winner surprised us.

The lab team’s goal was to assess and compare the raw throughput capability of each laptop’s Wi-Fi hardware across distances and physical interference — basically, to tell you which business laptop can run the fastest over Wi-Fi.
