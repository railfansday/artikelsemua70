FutureBench: A Renewable Framework for Evaluating Knowledge-Context Conflict in LLMs

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 20 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (164 words, 📖 Standard)

Recent findings suggest that arXiv:2611.23601v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Asking a model to check retrieved text can also tell it which source to trust. We introduce FutureBench, a renewable framework for evaluating knowledge-context conflict using timestamped questions, controlled passage triplets, and no-context probes. Across 3,629 questions and six models, Verify (a prompt combining checking with passage priority) raises false-answer adoption in all five models tested on pre-cutoff events, mainly by displacing answers that were correct under Baseline, which allows both passage evidence and model knowledge. On post-cutoff events, only o4-mini increases adoption (+13.7 percentage points). A four-prompt comparison on 902 post-cutoff questions finds that passage priority without checking raises adoption, while moving to Verify lowers it in all five models. Adversarial-Aware, which warns of misleading passages and prioritizes model knowledge, restores 82.5-98.9% of displaced pre-cutoff answers with under one point of change in true-passage accuracy. FutureBench supports repeated evaluation on fixed cohorts and extension to new events as models change.
