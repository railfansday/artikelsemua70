Improving fine-grained expressivity of graph neural networks

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 27 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (141 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Industry observers note that arXiv:2611.20020v2 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: This thesis investigates the expressive power of neural networks for structured data, focusing on graph neural networks and symmetry-aware architectures. We present four main contributions. First, we characterize the generic expressive power of geometric message-passing neural networks on sparse graphs and introduce EGenNet, an architecture achieving the corresponding guarantees. Second, we use representation theory to characterize linear layers equivariant to multiple permutations, including wreath-product symmetries. Third, we study permutation-invariant set representations that preserve containment, establish dimensional bounds and impossibility results, and introduce MASNet. Finally, we propose FSW-GNN, a message-passing architecture combining strong expressivity and bi-Lipschitz stability guarantees with strong empirical performance on long-range graph-learning tasks. Together, these results provide a broader understanding of how neural architectures can preserve the geometric, algebraic, order-theoretic, and metric structure of their inputs.
