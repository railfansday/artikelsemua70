Stafford Building Society goes live with MV Shield

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

The Stafford Building Society, a UK mutual lender, has deployed MV Shield, the financial crime compliance platform from Mutual Vision that runs on Napier AI technology.

The move brings the Society’s screening, monitoring and case management into a single system.

The rollout moves customer screening, transaction monitoring, customer risk assessment and know-your-customer (KYC) checks onto one unified platform.

Since the launch, initial signs show a drop in false positives, a lighter manual workload and quicker resolution of cases. Each decision is logged in a complete audit trail, which helps the Society prepare for audits and engage with regulators.

The Society chose MV Shield largely because it was built with mutuals in mind. It pairs Napier AI’s detection capabilities, which align with Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) expectations, with Mutual Vision’s experience of working with building societies for more than 25 years, along with locally based support.

Industry observers note that because screening, monitoring and case management sit together in a no-code environment, staff can assess alerts from one page and change rules and thresholds without outside help. They can also trial adjustments in a Sandbox before putting them into production, so they no longer need to raise tickets with the vendor.

MV Shield forms part of Mutual Vision’s updated technology offering for the mutual sector. It was created for the small compliance teams common at smaller societies. It gives them the kind of detection tools normally used by much larger organisations, in a format they can set up, test and manage on their own.

The Stafford Building Society CRO & MLRO Michelle Pledger said, “We are a small team with the same risk exposure as a much bigger one. What sold us was control: screening, monitoring and workflow in one platform, and the ability to change rules and test them ourselves, the same day a risk emerges.”.

It has been revealed that napier AI CEO Greg Watson remarked, “Money laundering cost the UK economy £156.7bn ($206bn) last year and continues to rise, as found by the Napier AI / AML Index 26-27. That means the scale of the challenge of financial crime compliance teams is growing too.

“We are delighted to partner with Mutual Vision to bring best-in-class AML solutions to mutuals, with embedded AI that helps small teams tackle financial crime efficiently without compromising on compliance.”.
