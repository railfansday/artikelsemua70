Industry observers note that today, AWS announces that AWS Quick, the AI workspace for business people, now supports Japanese, German, Vietnamese, and Chinese (Simplified and Traditional). AWS Quick now displays its interface in these languages and responds in the language each user chooses. Teams that work primarily in these languages can now use AWS Quick without switching to English.

For example, a finance team in Japan can ask questions about their business data in Japanese and get answers in Japanese, and a marketing team in Germany can create research reports in German. Language support covers chat, research reports, and apps in the AWS Quick web app, the desktop app for macOS and Windows, and the mobile app for iOS and Android.

Support for these languages is available in all AWS Regions where AWS Quick is available.

It has been revealed that to get started, open Settings in AWS Quick, choose Appearance and Language, and select your language. To learn more, see the AWS Quick product page.
