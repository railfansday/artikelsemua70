📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Metano has come out of stealth with $12m in seed capital as enterprises struggle to keep autonomous software under control across their technology estates.

Sierra Ventures headed the round, which also drew backing from Correlation Ventures, High Alpha and 360 ONE.

The business is led by co-founders Neelima Rustagi, who serves as CEO, and Ratnesh Pandey, its CTO, with the leadership team bringing security backgrounds from ServiceNow, Zscaler, Palo Alto Networks, FireEye and Microsoft.

According to recent reports, the launch lands at a moment when companies are rolling out AI agents on employee devices, in coding tools, across cloud systems and inside SaaS applications. Each fresh deployment widens a scattered attack surface.

Current security products tend to guard a single layer of infrastructure, leaving teams without the end-to-end view and behavioural insight required to protect agents that move between those layers on their own.

Industry observers note that AI agents also differ from traditional software because they act and decide on the fly, frequently with valid credentials and sanctioned tools. Their conduct can shift depending on the prompts they receive, the outside material they consume and the conditions they run in, so dangers may stay hidden during early testing and only surface later.

Sources indicate that metano tackles this by merging three functions into a single control layer. First, it locates both sanctioned and shadow AI agents, along with MCP servers, skills and software packages, and maps the permissions and exposure attached to each.

Second, it interprets agent conduct through Hive, a live context graph that links tasks, identities, tools, data access and actions over the course of a session, allowing analysts to spot anomalies and judge whether an agent is acting in line with its purpose.

Third, it applies guardrails directly where agents run, including activity that never passes through conventional network gateways, so centrally set policies can halt harmful actions in the moment. The platform is designed to scale alongside a manufacturer’s maturity in deploying agents.

The firm’s detection work draws on MARS Labs, its in-house AI security research unit, which studies new threats such as malicious skills, tainted software dependencies and weaknesses in MCP connections.

Metano is now generally available, and security teams can be running it in under 30 minutes. Its detections are aligned with MITRE ATLAS and the OWASP Top 10 for Agentic Applications. The company has passed a SOC 2 Type 2 examination, is certified to ISO 27001:2022 and appears on the Okta integration networks.

A free Eval Edition gives organisations 30 days of live agent scanning to uncover agent activity, risky settings and potential exposure within their own systems.

Sources indicate that metano co-founder and CEO Neelima Rustagi stated, “Traditional security tools weren’t designed for AI agents that can make decisions and change their behavior as they operate. We built Metano to understand agent behavior in real time, connecting intent to action, and stopping threats before autonomy turns into risk.”.
