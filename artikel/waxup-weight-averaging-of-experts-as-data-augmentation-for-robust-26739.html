WaXup: Weight Averaging of Experts as Data Augmentation for Robust Introspection

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 23 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (177 words, 📖 Standard)

arXiv:2611.27353v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: As the capabilities of large language models keep improving, traditional mechanistic interpretability methods struggle to keep pace. In contrast, trainable introspection methods emerge as a promising alternative; they repurpose the model itself as a self-diagnostic tool, trained to verbalize what it has learned during finetuning in natural language. While training on datasets of finetuned experts achieves encouraging results, it currently lacks robustness and generalization. Building on the data augmentation framework of MiXup, we introduce WaXup, a method that augments the introspection training set through linear interpolation in the weight space. This is motivated by the linear mode connectivity property: weight averaged experts interpolate the behaviors of the initial experts. We evaluate WaXup on three benchmarks: hidden-behavior models, a meta-dataset of real-world NLP tasks and AuditBench, a challenging alignment auditing suite of models adversarially trained to conceal their behaviors. WaXup improves the accuracy of generated descriptions as well as robustness to various distribution shifts. We hope to improve the reliability of trainable introspection methods towards better and safer models.
