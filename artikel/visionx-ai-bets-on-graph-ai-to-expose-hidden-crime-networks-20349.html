VisionX AI bets on graph AI to expose hidden crime networks

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

VisionX AI, a Singapore-based AI-native RegTech firm operating as a standalone company within Alpha Ladder Group, has debuted AEGIS, an in-house Large Graph Model (LGM) designed to detect and investigate financial crime, along with TraceX, the first product to run on it.

It has been revealed that AEGIS takes a different approach from tools that assess payments one at a time. It uses a Graph Transformer architecture and was pretrained on graph data drawn from financial transactions, so it learns how wallets, accounts and entities link together and how money travels between them.

According to recent reports, visionX AI pairs this proprietary model with LLMs from established providers in a hybrid set-up. AEGIS supplies the network intelligence, while the LLMs manage conversational interaction, reasoning and AI-supported investigative work. The combination aims to help banks and investigators surface concealed relationships, unusual fund movements and the wider criminal groups operating behind single transactions.

The launch comes as illicit finance grows more networked. In July 2026, the Financial Action Task Force (FATF) cautioned that criminal groups take advantage of the cross-border reach of virtual assets to defraud victims, dodge sanctions and launder money. Its findings showed that illicit funds pass through stablecoins, cross-chain bridges, decentralised exchanges and mixers, where they blend with legitimate activity and become more difficult to follow and freeze.

TraceX, an AI-driven platform for blockchain tracing and investigation, is being developed for financial crime investigators and law enforcement bodies. It is intended to follow funds across linked blockchain addresses, flag suspicious patterns that connect separate cases, and produce investigative leads backed by traceable evidence and human oversight.

It has been revealed that reverse tracing, MegaMap network analysis, pattern recognition and case association are on the roadmap. Investigators retain decision-making authority, using TraceX’s suggestions as guidance, and every decision is logged.

According to recent reports, visionX began life as an incubated project inside MetaComp, tackling compliance and financial crime risks where traditional finance meets digital assets. It has since grown into a combined financial crime and compliance platform that underpins compliance intelligence for MetaComp’s StableX Agentic Network, which debuted as the StableX Network in November 2025.

Its offering includes VisionX KYT, which pools blockchain risk data from several independent analytics providers covering 1 billion wallets across 25 chains, and Web2.5 TxM, which merges identity, behavioural and network signals to monitor transactions across both traditional and digital asset markets.

It has been revealed that spinning VisionX AI out as an independent business allows it to sell to a wider pool of financial institutions, digital asset firms and public-sector bodies. The organization plans to apply its graph intelligence technology to financial crime detection, fraud and scam prevention, compliance risk management and investigations.

The AEGIS name, short for Attention-based Entity and Graph Intelligence System, references the shield of Zeus in Greek myth that was lent to Athena, reflecting the technology’s protective purpose.

VisionX AI executive chairwoman Tin Pei Ling, who is also co-president of Alpha Ladder Group and MetaComp, mentioned, “Financial crime operates through networks of people, entities and financial activities. To fight it, we need AI that understands the relationships between transactions. AEGIS is built to see this network. It learns how money moves between accounts and wallets, so it can spot the patterns that link activity which looks unrelated. Our ambition is to build AI-native financial crime tools that help institutions stay ahead of emerging threats.”.

Sources indicate that visionX AI CEO Summer Yu said, “Individual cases often look unrelated, even when the same criminal network sits behind them. TraceX brings AEGIS into practical blockchain investigations, so investigators can identify patterns, connect cases and uncover those networks. AI highlights investigators where to look next, and their judgement and the evidence still decide the case.”.
