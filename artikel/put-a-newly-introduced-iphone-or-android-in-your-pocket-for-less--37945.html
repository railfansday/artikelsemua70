Industry observers note that cyber Monday is the Monday after Black Friday. This year, it falls on Nov. 30.

Holiday sales, especially at the end of the year, are usually a great time to buy all kinds of tech. Smaller holidays in the middle of the year yield shallow discounts, but retailers bank on Black Friday sales, and you can typically find more valuable price slashes. However, some phones have gone up in price by $100-$200, so keep that in mind when shopping.

Apple traditionally doesn’t discount products like iPhones at any point during the year. However, you can sometimes you can find iPhones and other Apple gear on sale at major retailers that carry Apple products, such as Amazon, Walmart, Best Buy, and more.

Generally, Amazon offers deals on unlocked devices that can be purchased outright and then activated with any carrier. This means you’re typically paying more up-front than if you were to contract a upcoming device via a carrier deal, so in most cases, the best Black Friday phone deals for the average mobile consumer will be available at carriers like T-Mobile, AT&T, Verizon, and more.
