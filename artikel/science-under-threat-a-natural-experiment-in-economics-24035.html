Science under Threat? A Natural Experiment in Economics

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 23 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (109 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

arXiv:2608.14164v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: Academic freedom is under strain worldwide. To study how scholars respond to (perceived) political pressure, we exploit a natural experiment in the United States: the publication in early 2025 of a "stop list" of words flagged by the government. We find that economists at universities relying more on federal funding reduce gender-, race-, and environment-related content with flagged words relative to other US and UK economists. Regarding mechanisms, our analyses suggest that self-censorship may explain this reduction better than direct grant cancellation. Estimates are similar when excluding papers acknowledging federal funding or controlling for universities' institutional measures and financial distress.
