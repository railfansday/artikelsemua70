Measuring AI progress on the tasks that matter in everyday life.

It has been revealed that openAI’s mission is to ensure that artificial general intelligence benefits all of humanity. For the more than one billion people who currently use ChatGPT every week, we want to build AI that can help with the practical challenges of everyday life, like finding childcare, accessing government services, creating a budget, preparing for school, modifying recipes, ordering groceries, designing workout plans, and more.

When someone is looking for help making their life easier, they need more than advice or a chat response. To be a truly helpful personal assistant, ChatGPT should be able to take actions that actually get things done.

To measure model performance over time on everyday tasks, we built an evaluation of tasks covering areas where customers wanted the most help from AI and personal agents. We used this evaluation to track and drive improvement in model performance on everyday tasks over the past year.

According to recent reports, to identify the tasks people value most, we started with two large studies that track how people spend time in both the U.S. and internationally: the American Time Use Survey⁠(opens in a new window) and the Multinational Time Use Study⁠(opens in a new window). Together, these datasets provide a detailed catalog of everyday life tasks that people do across different demographics, times of day, and seasons. To understand where people would most value AI assistance, we expanded on these existing surveys with a new demographically representative survey of 3,995 respondents. For each task, we asked a variety of questions to identify the areas where everyday people most wanted help from a personal assistant. The resulting tasks span the most notable areas that people want help with, including caregiving, health, education, household administration, personal finances, shopping, and accessing government services.

Recent findings suggest that over the past year, we’ve trained our models to become much faster, more capable, and more aligned on browser use, computer use, and connectors tasks. Enabling these capabilities in ChatGPT also expands the types of tasks the model can help with, helping move ChatGPT beyond a chat tool and towards a truly agentic personal assistant.

It has been revealed that to ensure AGI benefits all of humanity, we need to measure and improve model progress on what matters in the lives of everyday people. That means going beyond academic assessments of model knowledge and actually measuring how well AI models can help people navigate decisions, manage everyday responsibilities, and get meaningful things done. We are hopeful that LifeVal represents a step towards that goal.
