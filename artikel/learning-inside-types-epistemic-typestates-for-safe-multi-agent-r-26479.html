Learning Inside Types: Epistemic Typestates for Safe Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 23 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (233 words, 📖 Standard)

arXiv:2611.27631v1 Announce Type: upcoming Abstract: Type disciplines make programs safe by construction: components checked against each other's types compose into programs that cannot go wrong in the ways the types exclude. Types suit reinforcement learning: a type constrains what an agent may choose, not how it chooses, so the guarantee holds for every policy a learner produces. We introduce epistemic typestates, a type discipline for safe multi-agent reinforcement learning. An agent's type is a protocol whose states record its beliefs, the worlds it cannot rule out from its own observations and actions, and permits only actions that are safe in every world of the belief, whatever the others do within their public types. Agents learn inside their types, which are built and checked one agent at a time and run without communication. We prove type soundness and team safety, give a synthesis that is sound and complete, build types from guards when worlds are infinite, and show that shields are a special case. All proofs are mechanised in Rocq. On nine benchmarks, no typed learner causes a safety violation, and task performance does not differ significantly from that of two recent multi-agent shielding methods. Where a contract-based method fails at five and ten agents, types for ten agents are built in under a second. In the two continuous benchmarks, Lagrangian learners cause thousands of violations in every training run.
