Industry News: Abstract What Varies, Reuse Longer Patterns: Efficient Offline Chain-of-Thought Compression

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 23 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (150 words, 📖 Standard)

Sources indicate that arXiv:2611.27710v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Offline chain-of-thought (CoT) compression creates shorter supervision for efficient reasoning, but per-example compressors repeatedly process similar expressions across traces, wasting compressor budget. Methods that reuse identical text across examples miss longer shared expressions when names, numbers, or formulas change from one question to another. We introduce CRAFT, which abstracts problem-specific details into slots to uncover partially lexicalized expression templates (PLETs) and clusters wording variants into families. Compressing each family representative once yields shared deletion or rewriting edits, which are applied with each occurrence's own details to construct compact, ordinary-text training targets. Compared with per-example rewriting, CRAFT Rewrite reduces weighted compressor budgets by a median 90.7% with a median accuracy loss of 1.21 percentage points, while generating shorter reasoning. Against cross-sample encoding, it shortens reasoning across all eight model-dataset settings by 25.3% on average and improves accuracy in five.
