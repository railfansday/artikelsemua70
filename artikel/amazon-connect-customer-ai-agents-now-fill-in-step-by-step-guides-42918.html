Amazon Connect Customer AI agents now fill in step-by-step guides

AI agents in Amazon Connect Customer can now populate fields of a step-by-step guide for contact center agents during live customer interactions. With step-by-step guides, managers can design forms and other user interfaces for common workflows, such as rebooking a trip or submitting a claim. Now, AI agents can fill in a guide when it first loads and suggest updates during live conversations, saving time and improving data accuracy.

According to recent reports, when contact center agents speak to customers, they spend valuable time recording the information they need to solve the customer's problem in a step-by-step guide. For example, when a traveler calls an airline to change her flight, the agent might record her booking reference, dates of travel, and seat preferences. Now, Connect Customer's AI agents can automatically suggest and populate information like this, based on the context of the conversation. Human agents can review each suggestion, accept or reject suggestions individually or in bulk, and make any edits before submitting. The agent can focus on the customer and the problem at hand rather than on manual data entry.

You can use AI agents with step-by-step guides in the following AWS regions: US East (N. Virginia), US West (Oregon), Asia Pacific (Seoul), Asia Pacific (Singapore), Asia Pacific (Sydney), Asia Pacific (Tokyo), Canada (Central), Europe (Frankfurt), and Europe (London).

To learn more and get started, see step-by-step guides in the Amazon Connect Customer administrator guide.
