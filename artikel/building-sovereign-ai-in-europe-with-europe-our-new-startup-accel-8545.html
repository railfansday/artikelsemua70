Building Sovereign AI in Europe, With Europe: Our New Startup Accelerator

Today we’re announcing an open call for applications for the Google for Startups Accelerator: Sovereign AI. Available in Europe and Israel, this initiative supports startups that are building cutting-edge AI solutions for European regulatory environments. Artificial intelligence represents an estimated €1.2 trillion economic opportunity for Europe. To realize this potential, European enterprises and public institutions need to adopt advanced AI while also meeting crucial data governance requirements, including the EU AI Act, NIS2, DORA, and national frameworks such as SecNumCloud and BSI's cloud security and sovereignty principles. We want ambitious founders to build in Europe, with solutions made in Europe. For startups, digital sovereignty cannot mean settling for second-rate tools or getting locked into a single rigid stack. It means co-engineering with Europe's tech ecosystem so founders can scale on their own terms from day one: for instance combining frontier Gemini models, open-weight models like Gemma and Mistral, and verifiable data residency and cryptographic controls delivered with local European sovereign partners. How it works This equity-free, 15-week hybrid program includes virtual and in-person sessions connecting founders with Google engineers, regulatory experts, and local partners. Selected startups receive hands-on architectural mentorship, 1:1 pairing with Google Cloud engineers, and advisory support to prepare for EU AI Act Regulatory Sandboxes. Participants will gain direct support to deploy across Google Cloud’s tiered sovereign portfolio: Sovereign cloud infrastructure: From regional data controls and external key management (data boundary) to partner-operated clouds (such as S3NS in France and Thales in Germany) and fully air-gapped Google Distributed Cloud environments. Auditable, private AI: Fine-tuning and deploying open-weight models like the Gemma family (including MedGemma) in sovereign environments for verifiable control over data and operations. Ecosystem and investors access: Direct engagement with European sovereign cloud operators, system integrators, and institutional investors to compress enterprise sales cycles. Who should apply We are selecting a small, high-touch cohort of late-seed to post Series A startups headquartered across Europe and Israel with strong traction that use AI to handle sensitive, regulated workloads. We’re specifically seeking startups focused on: Government and public service technologies including sovereign document processing, citizen service portals, municipal operations, smart utilities, and civic emergency grids (defense applications excluded). Healthcare and life sciences including clinical trial analytics, electronic health records (EHR/EHDS), diagnostic copilots, and hospital operations that meet compliance requirements for GDPR and the European Health Data Space. Regulated finance including credit risk modeling, anti-money laundering pipelines, fraud detection, credit underwriting pipelines, and claims processing. Cybersecurity and data trust including enterprise threat detection and response, supply chain security, vulnerability scanning, and civilian IT infrastructure protection. If you’re a founder building sovereign AI to power Europe’s digital autonomy, we want to help you scale your impact. You can apply on the Google for Startups Accelerator page. The deadline for applications is January 15th, 2027.
