📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Superscript, an award-winning digital insurance broker, has joined forces with Lloyds Banking Group to offer business insurance to the bank’s small business clients, opening up digital cover to its one million UK business customers.

Through the tie-up, SMEs across the country can access digital-first insurance via the Lloyds Bank and Bank of Scotland brands. Using Superscript’s technology, customers can obtain a quote and take out a policy within minutes through the online channels of both banks.

The service lets businesses pick from a selection of covers and receive proof of insurance straight away. Policyholders can also adjust their cover partway through the term without being charged administration fees.

Superscript operates as a digital insurance broker, using its technology to provide business insurance.

Industry observers note that superscript managing director of SME insurance Tori Butler remarked, “Running a small business comes with enough admin without insurance adding another layer of complexity. Business owners need straightforward ways to understand their insurance options, choose the cover that works for them and get on with running their business.

It has been revealed that “That’s what makes our partnership with Lloyds so exciting. By bringing Superscript’s digital insurance experience together with the reach and trusted relationships of Lloyds, we can make insurance simpler and more accessible for more businesses across the UK.”.

Lloyds Banking Group managing director of insurance Jeremy Ward said, “Making insurance simpler isn’t just about getting customers through a journey faster. It’s about understanding where things aren’t working for them and being able to improve them promptly. That’s what excites me about this partnership. By combining Superscript’s digital capability with our understanding of customers, we can learn from how businesses use the service and keep making the experience better, helping more businesses get the protection they need with less complexity.”.
