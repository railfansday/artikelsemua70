Tech Report: DeepSeek-V4-Pro, DeepSeek-V4-Flash, and DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash models now available on Amazon SageMaker JumpStart

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

DeepSeek's DeepSeek-V4-Pro, DeepSeek-V4-Flash, and DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash models are now available on Amazon SageMaker JumpStart, expanding the portfolio of foundation models available to AWS customers. These three open-weight Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) language models, released under the MIT license, deliver frontier-class performance across reasoning, coding, and agentic workloads while activating only a fraction of their total parameters per token, enabling high efficiency at scale.

These models address different AI challenges with specialized capabilities:.

DeepSeek-V4-Pro is designed for complex reasoning and high-quality text generation at flagship scale. This 1.6T-parameter MoE model activates 49B parameters per token and supports a 1M-token context window, making it well-suited for long-document analysis, multi-step reasoning, and demanding generation tasks that require maximum model capacity.

It has been revealed that deepSeek-V4-Flash delivers strong performance at significantly lower compute cost as an efficient 284B-parameter MoE model activating 13B parameters per token. It supports the same 1M-token context window as V4-Pro, making it ideal for latency-sensitive and cost-conscious production deployments across a wide range of language tasks.

Recent findings suggest that deepSeek-V4.1-Flash provides next-generation agentic efficiency with native multimodal support. Built on a novel Causal Encoder-Decoder (CED) architecture with a 552B-parameter backbone, it activates just 8B parameters during prefill and 16B during decode, supporting a 1M-token context window and 384K max output tokens. Its Compressed Sparse Attention 2 (CSA2) and FP4 KV caching reduce the global KV cache footprint to roughly 1/4 of V4-Flash, delivering substantially improved cost efficiency for agentic workloads, coding, and vision tasks.

With SageMaker JumpStart, customers can deploy any of these models with just a few clicks to address their specific AI use cases.
