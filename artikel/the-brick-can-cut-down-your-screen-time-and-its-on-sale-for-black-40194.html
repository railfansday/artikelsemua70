The Brick is a magnetic square that works with an app to let you choose which apps you’d like to block. When you tap your phone to the Brick, those apps are blocked (or “bricked”) using NFC technology until you physically tap the Brick again.

According to recent reports, you can set up schedules to block apps at specific times of day and modes to block certain types of apps. Brick gives you five free “unbricks” you can use in an emergency, when you aren’t near the physical Brick — so you can’t just unlock them without tapping whenever you want, which is usually my problem with the app-blocking apps I’ve used before.

Sources indicate that ZDNET editor Nina Raemont has been using the Brick for a year now. “I highly recommend Brick if you struggle to check your phone regularly or waste time on social media,” she wrote in her review. “During the first full week I used it, my screen time decreased by 7%. Its positive reinforcements, rather than admonishments when you’ve gone over your allotted screen time, incrementally build the case that you can, indeed, go without these apps.”.
