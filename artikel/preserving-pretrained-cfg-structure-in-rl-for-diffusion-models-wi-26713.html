Preserving Pretrained CFG Structure in RL for Diffusion Models with Conditional Contrast Regularization

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 23 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (211 words, 📖 Standard)

Recent findings suggest that arXiv:2611.27288v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Reinforcement Learning (RL) has become an vital component in the post-training of diffusion models. In practice, however, standard RL often improves the target reward without reliably preserving holistic generation quality, leading to unstable reward-quality trade-offs that we view as a form of reward hacking. Since Classifier-Free Guidance (CFG) is known to improve visual clarity, prompt alignment, and perceptual quality in generation, we investigate whether its underlying structure can serve as a useful prior during RL. We present a unified geometric view that interprets both CFG and RL as correction vectors in a common prediction space, and identify the projection of the RL-induced correction onto the pretrained CFG correction as a useful diagnostic of quality-related structural preservation. Geometric analysis and rotation intervention further show that the pretrained CFG direction carries information useful for generation quality that reward-driven RL updates do not necessarily preserve. Based on this insight, we propose Conditional Contrast Regularization (CCR), which uses the frozen reference model's conditional contrast as a local structural prior during RL. Experiments across multiple reward settings and backbones show that CCR more consistently preserves generation quality while maintaining competitive reward, yielding a better reward-quality trade-off than standard RL and generic regularization methods.
