Rational Ensemble Logic for Cohort Discovery with Real-World Electrophysiology Data

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 20 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (229 words, 📖 Standard)

Recent findings suggest that arXiv:2611.23708v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Biomedical knowledge representation should make the passage from observations to conclusions transparent, rigorous, understandable, explainable, and reproducible (TRUER). We introduce Rational Ensemble Logic (QEL) for specifying temporal phenotypes in real-world electrophysiology data (RWED), with a meaning independent of the implementation software. QEL combines three parameterized modal operators (exact displacement, bounded existence, and bounded universality) over rational time with Boolean connectives and first-order quantification. Its Labeled Interval Ensemble (LINE) semantics represents annotated records as finite interval ensembles. We relate the operators to morphological operations and develop endpoint-array algorithms and finite rational evidence sets for model checking. We prove closure and semantic correctness of the interval operations, obtain finite evidence sets by quantifier elimination, and establish polynomial data complexity for fixed formulas. On 54 manually constructed records, a prototype agreed with the assigned outcomes for base and strict post-ictal generalized EEG suppression (PGES) queries. A separate feasibility experiment over 1,976 patients identified 37 qualifying patients and 45 annotation patterns in 16 seconds. The released prototype uses endpoint candidates and numerical tolerances. QEL supports the TRUER principle through explicit phenotype definitions, precise semantics, and provably correct evaluation procedures, with rational witnesses for existential queries. These provide a logical foundation for formal specification and correctness of algorithms that are independent of specific clinical interpretation, data annotation quality, and software implementation.
