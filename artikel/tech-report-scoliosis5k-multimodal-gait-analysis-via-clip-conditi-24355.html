Tech Report: Scoliosis5K: Multimodal Gait Analysis via Clip-Conditioned RGB-Silhouette Fusion

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 23 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (213 words, 📖 Standard)

arXiv:2611.26849v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Color walking video records body boundary motion alongside clothing folds, surface appearance, and recording-environment cues. To examine what an explicit binary silhouette contributes to participant-level classification of scoliosis-related gait when a classifier already observes the paired color sequence, we construct Scoliosis5K, a multi-site collection of 4,998 corridor-walking sequences across 3,010 participant identifiers in which RGB person crops, binary silhouettes, and two-dimensional poses share each recording identifier and frame index. Our architecture uses each patch's pixel deviation from its clip mean to weight two low-rank query and value updates inside a pretrained RGB encoder, fusing the resulting video representation with a silhouette transformer sequence and regional kernel ridge scores extracted from one native-resolution RGB frame per window. Across 988 participants in five participant-disjoint cross-validation folds and three training seeds, the complete system reaches 83.77% accuracy and 79.91% macro-averaged F1, compared with 83.27% accuracy and 79.56% macro-F1 when the video RGB encoder remains frozen under identical inputs. Zeroing the silhouette vector at fixed weights shifts overall accuracy by only 0.10 percentage points while raising positive-category recall from 68.52% to 77.78% and lowering recall for both remaining categories. Macro-averaged AUROC is 0.950 across the cohort and 0.879 among participant pairs matched on acquisition site and recording-count group.
