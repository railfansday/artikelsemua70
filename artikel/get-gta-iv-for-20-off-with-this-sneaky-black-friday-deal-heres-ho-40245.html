If you’ve (somehow) been holding out on buying Grand Theft Auto VI, waiting for a sale, you’re in luck this Black Friday: While the game itself isn’t discounted (and probably won’t be for quite some time), you can get $100 worth of PlayStation 5 gift cards from Costco for just $79.99 — saving you $20, essentially getting you 20% off GTA VI Ultimate Edition if you choose to spend it on the game.

It has been revealed that to get this PS5 gift card deal, you do need to have a Costco membership (or know someone who is who will let you borrow their login). The gift cards come in a pack of four $25 cards. Costco usually offers them for a $10 discount, at $89.99, but this Black Friday deal drops it down another $10 off.
