📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Valeria, a Barcelona-headquartered payroll and workforce management platform built on artificial intelligence, has secured €5.5m in seed funding to expand its technology for businesses that see frequent staff changes.

It has been revealed that the round was led by BlackFin, with returning backers VentureFriends and Fortino also participating. GoHub Ventures came on board as a new investor in the company.

The seed raise follows a €1.7m pre-seed round completed in February 2026, meaning Valeria has secured over €7m in total during 2026.

The company plans to use the fresh capital to speed up work on its proprietary payroll engine and bring it to market more widely. It also intends to push beyond its current focus on logistics and delivery, moving into other sectors with high staff turnover, including hospitality, retail and cleaning services. Valeria’s ambition is to become the leading player in Spain’s workforce management market, which is estimated to be worth over €1bn.

Recent findings suggest that according to the tech giant, payroll processing in Spain remains heavily dependent on manual tasks handled by a scattered mix of providers, an approach that struggles in industries such as delivery, logistics and hospitality where headcounts shift weekly. Valeria pairs its in-house payroll calculation engine and AI with a dedicated team of employment law specialists, who track irregularities and provide clients with legal assurance.

Unlike most HR software, which depends on outside providers, Valeria’s system links through APIs directly to the Spanish Social Security system, the State Public Employment Service (SEPE) and the Spanish Tax Agency. Its engine handles every step automatically, covering recruitment, contract creation, sick leave notices, wage garnishments and workplace accident reporting. It also addresses the collective agreement rules applying to the courier, delivery and fleet management industries.

Set up in 2024 by CEO Pau Laporte, COO Carlos Saiz and CTO Sergio Morales, Valeria now serves over 100 businesses and 20,000 workers. The organization has expanded to a headcount of more than 40 across payroll, product and engineering since establishing its Barcelona base. Its customer roster includes Glovo, Uber Eats, Goiko, Honest Greens and Desigual.

Industry observers note that valeria CEO and co-founder Pau Laporte said, “Our focus is not on rigid corporate structures, but on businesses that need flexibility and the ability to respond immediately. Valeria’s system is available 24 hours a day, seven days a week, allowing businesses to operate whenever they need to, without having to wait for manual processes or intermediaries.”.

Recent findings suggest that goHub Ventures partner Pablo Perea mentioned, “We met Pau, Carlos, Sergio, and the rest of the team more than a year ago, and watching them work since then is what has convinced us: they complement each other like few teams we have seen, and they iterate at incredible speed. In high-turnover sectors, labor management still relies on manual processes, and Valeria solves that problem with an AI-native solution that brings savings and agility to both employers and workers. We are delighted to support them as they become the benchmark in Spain and replicate the model in international markets.”.
