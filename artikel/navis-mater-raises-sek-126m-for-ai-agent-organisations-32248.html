Navis Mater, a Stockholm-based ownership and development group with interests in AI research and cybersecurity, has raised SEK 12.6m ($12.3m) through a upcoming share concern to advance organisational models that combine people and AI agents.

The funding was provided by three backers. Michael Hjorth and Robert Chirico Willstedt contributed a combined SEK 7.6m via their vehicles Loriac Ltd and Rowil AB, while founder Emanuel Lipschütz put in a further SEK 5m through Spiky Investments AB.

According to recent reports, navis Mater intends to direct the money towards research and development in two areas: its Governed Agentic Organization (GAO) concept and sovereign AI. It will also use the capital to build out its portfolio companies and hire upcoming staff. The sovereign AI work is intended to help European firms gain more authority over their AI systems, data and technical dependencies, regardless of which model provider or infrastructure they rely on.

Recent findings suggest that under the GAO approach, human staff and AI agents operate side by side within a structure built on defined mandates, human accountability, independent checks and escalation procedures. The aim is to let agents take on greater autonomy while organisations retain oversight. Navis Mater runs its own reference implementation of the model and has released a report on the subject.

Sources indicate that founded in Stockholm in 2025, Navis Mater is a Swedish group that carries out research into GAO and AI. Through companies it owns either fully or partially, it is active in cyber and information security, digital resilience and modern IT networking. Its portfolio includes Cyber Defencely, CyberResilient, NetSymphony and AceBit.

Navis Mater founder and CEO Emanuel Lipschütz mentioned, “We are building Navis Mater with a long-term vision to develop fresh European technology leaders. When people and AI agents work together, it changes how organizations work. The capital gives us the power to develop both GAO and our companies. Michael and Robert also bring valuable experience.”.

It has been revealed that robert Chirico Willstedt remarked, “The development of organizations where AI agents can perform increasingly large parts of the work under secure and verifiable control, together with the need for digital resilience against growing cyber threats, represent two enormous business opportunities. The Navis Mater group companies have leading expertise and scalable solutions in both.”.
