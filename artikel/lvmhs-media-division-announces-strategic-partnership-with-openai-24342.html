It has been revealed that paris, 16 November 2026—LVMH Group’s Media Division and OpenAI announce the signing of a strategic agreement to support new uses of artificial intelligence across all the division’s publications and make their journalism more accessible through ChatGPT. The agreement includes the deployment of OpenAI tools and ChatGPT Enterprise to teams across the media division, enabling them to develop AI applications tailored to their day-to-day work.

Their shared ambition is to empower teams across the media division to experiment with and develop practical uses of AI that support journalism and high-quality reporting.

Sources indicate that artificial intelligence is a powerful tool that, when put to work in newsrooms, enables journalists to focus on the core of their profession: investigating, analysing and writing. Teams will be able to explore applications such as document research, document comparison and preliminary translation. The goal is to enable them to devote more time to investigative reporting, analysis and reporting in the field.

In addition, in an increasingly crowded information landscape, personalisation has become a major priority. Artificial intelligence is an essential tool for developing and delivering products and services that meet the needs of customers, readers and advertisers alike, helping to attract and retain them and grow revenue across the publications.

In a recent development, this partnership is a company-wide initiative that also enhances the presence of content from LVMH Group’s Media Division in ChatGPT, providing summaries and excerpts in response to relevant user questions, identifying their sources and including direct links to the original articles. The partnership will extend across all the division’s publications and support the evolution of roles throughout the organisation: streamlining operations, improving efficiency, and encouraging innovation and creativity to prepare today’s publications for tomorrow. The rollout will be supported by training tailored to everyone’s use cases.

It has been revealed that LVMH Group’s media division, Groupe Les Echos-Le Parisien, brings together 17 media brands covering news, finance, culture, heritage, arts and music. With 1,800 employees, including nearly 700 journalists, it reaches more than 27 million people in France every month.

The Group’s media brands: Boursier.com⁠(opens in a newly introduced window), Capital Finance, Challenges, Connaissance des Arts, Historia, Investir, La Lettre de l’Expansion, L’Agefi, Le Parisien, Aujourd’hui en France, Les Echos, L’Opinion, medici.tv⁠(opens in a new window), Mezzo, Mieux Vivre Votre Argent, Paris Match, Radio Classique.

The Group’s exhibitions and publishing houses: Citadelles & Mazenod, Fine Arts Paris, In Fine, Salon du Dessin.
