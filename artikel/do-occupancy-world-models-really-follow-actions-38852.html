Do Occupancy World Models Really Follow Actions?

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 26 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (141 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Sources indicate that arXiv:2611.32029v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Occupancy world models predict future 3D scenes conditioned on ego actions, yet their predictions may not faithfully follow the specified trajectory under counterfactual actions. Because driving datasets provide sparse coverage of alternative trajectories for each scene, existing models learn the spatial effect of ego motion implicitly from observed scene--trajectory combinations, which may entangle action effects with scene--trajectory correlations. We propose ReCast, an ego-trajectory-guided transformer that grounds future prediction in action-induced geometric correspondence across ego-centric frames. ReCast decouples historical encoding from future decoding and aligns deformable interactions using relative ego transformations, while retaining flexibility for non-rigid scene evolution. We also introduce a counterfactual action-faithfulness benchmark covering in-distribution, tail, and out-of-distribution trajectories. On Occ3D-nuScenes, ReCast achieves competitive standard forecasting performance while improving counterfactual forecasting, with particularly clear gains under out-of-distribution trajectories.
