Weatherbys Private Bank, a long-standing specialist in private banking and high-net-worth lending, has entered a strategic partnership with Firenze, a UK credit infrastructure and Lombard lending platform, with the aim of making portfolio-backed borrowing easier to obtain and less expensive for clients.

Under the agreement, the private bank’s experience in serving wealthy clients is combined with Firenze’s digital technology. Weatherbys is currently the only private banking partner working with Firenze, a position the bank states will let it extend Lombard facilities to a broader client base while offering greater flexibility and more competitive terms.

Lombard lending lets borrowers raise money using their existing investments as collateral. The market for this type of credit has changed considerably in recent years as technology has been applied to it.

Firenze’s system relies on live portfolio monitoring and dynamic risk management to make the lending process quicker and more efficient, and assets do not need to be transferred to a different custodian.

Firenze supplies the technology and infrastructure that allows banks and wealth managers to provide portfolio-backed loans to their clients or to improve the Lombard products they already offer. David Newman founded the business, and it is his second venture. Firenze has secured around £10m in equity funding from investors including AlbionVC and Outward VC.

It has been revealed that weatherbys head of private clients Oliver Barnett stated, “Lombard lending has long been a valuable solution for clients seeking liquidity whilst remaining invested. Yet the complexity, infrastructure and operating models necessary for a private bank to offer this product have often limited its availability or resulted in higher pricing.

“This has particularly been the case when a client wishes to against portfolios managed elsewhere without changing their investment manager or custodian. With tech-led innovation reshaping this space, we identified Firenze early in its development as a trailblazer capable of leading the way in embracing new market opportunities.

“The Firenze team has now built a platform that combines cutting-edge technology with a streamlined operating model, helping deliver fast, secure and reliable lending decisions.”.

Firenze CEO David Newman said, “Firenze’s proposition is designed to meet the needs of seeking a digital-first borrowing experience.

“Meanwhile, we know Weatherbys’ strong reputation for delivering a highly personalised private banking service to clients who expect the very best in relationship management and financial advice.

“By working together, we’re now further opening up access to Lombard lending across a new range of clients and advisers whilst, crucially, retaining the flexibility and service that are vital to both.”.

Sources indicate that firenze has had a busy year, having recently partnered with AJ Bell Investcentre. The deal allows advisers using AJ Bell Investcentre to provide their clients with tech-enabled portfolio lending.

Earlier in the year, the FinTech organization secured £6m in a funding round led by AlbionVC. The capital was earmarked for its growth intends, which include launching its capabilities across more partners, expanding its products, and launching in new jurisdictions.
