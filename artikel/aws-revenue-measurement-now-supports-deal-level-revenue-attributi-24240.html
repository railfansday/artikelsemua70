AWS Revenue Measurement now supports deal-level revenue attribution for AWS Marketplace offers and opportunities

According to recent reports, today, AWS introduces deal-level revenue attribution for AWS Revenue Measurement (previously Partner Revenue Measurement) capabilities. AWS Partners can now see how the AWS revenue measured for their products maps to specific AWS Marketplace offers and/or APN Customer Engagements (ACE) opportunities. Until now, partners who implemented AWS Revenue Measurement via Resource Tagging, User Agent String or AWS Marketplace Metering could view aggregated, product-level attributed revenue. With this launch, partners can view attributed revenue by offers and opportunities on the Attributed Revenue dashboard in AWS Partner Central for components hosted in partners' own AWS accounts. Two newly introduced capabilities make this possible: Revenue Attribution IDs and Marketplace Revenue Share.

A Revenue Attribution ID is a unique identifier that allows partners to allocate a product's measured AWS revenue to specific offers and opportunities. Partners create a Revenue Attribution ID, associate it with their deals, and enter a monthly attributed revenue allocation percentage for each deal. Partners with SaaS products on AWS Marketplace can automate allocation to offers with Marketplace Revenue Share. Each month, partners enter the percentage of the product's attributed revenue contributed by AWS Marketplace deals in the Revenue Attribution page, and AWS then calculates the attributed revenue for each active offer. Partners can use Revenue Attribution IDs alongside Marketplace Revenue Share to adjust a calculated offer amount or attribute revenue to opportunities for future months. Both capabilities build on AWS Revenue Measurement, so partners can extend their current product-level implementations to gain deal-level insights.

Industry observers note that partners can manage Revenue Attribution IDs and Marketplace Revenue Share in AWS Partner Central or through the AWS Revenue Measurement and Marketplace Revenue Share APIs. AWS Revenue Measurement is generally available in all commercial Regions. To learn more, review the onboarding guide.
