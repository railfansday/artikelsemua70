Black Friday falls one day after Thanksgiving. This year, it’s on Nov. 27, 2026.

Cyber Monday happens on the Monday after Black Friday. This year, Cyber Monday is on Nov. 30, 2026.

We typically see some of the lowest prices of the year on laptops during the Black Friday/Cyber Monday shopping window, so yes, we’d recommend shopping these sales if you’re in the market for a upcoming laptop.

The best Black Friday laptop deal depends on a few factors: Your budget, if you want a PC or a Mac, and what features you need. My pick for the best Black Friday laptop deal so far is the MacBook Pro M5, which won our Editors’ Choice award and is seeing a rare discount.

Black Friday and Cyber Monday typically see comparable deals on laptops. If you see a model you’re looking for hit a good price on Black Friday, we’d recommend buying it then — it’s unlikely that prices will drop lower on Cyber Monday, and stock could run out.
