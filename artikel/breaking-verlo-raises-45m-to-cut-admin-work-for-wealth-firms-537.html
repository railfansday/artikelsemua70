📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Verlo, a provider of an AI operating system built for wealth management firms, has secured $4.5m CAD in seed funding to help Registered Investment Advisors (RIAs) cut back on time-consuming administrative tasks.

In a recent development, staircase Ventures led the investment, while Pelorus VC came back to back the manufacturer again. They were joined by several angel investors, among them founders and senior executives from Verafin, the Newfoundland financial crime software business that Nasdaq bought in 2020 for $2.7bn.

The firm says its technology gives RIAs and wealth managers the groundwork they need to reclaim thousands of hours otherwise lost to admin, allowing them to expand their client base without taking on extra operational costs.

Verlo intends to put the fresh capital towards enhancing its platform, growing its headcount in St. John’s and speeding up its expansion in the US.

Industry observers note that the business describes itself as a compliant AI operating system for the wealth sector. It turns every client record and interaction into material that AI agents can search and draw upon, enabling RIAs to automate administrative work, build their books of business and oversee AI use across the whole firm, while saving time and avoiding added overhead. To achieve this, Verlo brings together and reconciles information from a firm’s custodian, CRM, document storage, calendar and email into one trusted dataset for its agents.

Verlo CEO and founder Brent Pretty remarked, “Our customers are independent advisors and wealth management firms who are weighed down by tedious admin. Verlo is the AI operating system that frees up their time to focus on growth, not paperwork.”.

He added, “Legacy wealth management tools are beginning to introduce AI, but they feel more like add-ons to existing workflows rather than integrated technology that will fundamentally transform how their firm operates. They keep data siloed, which sets AI agents up for failure. Any answers they give will be useless because the source can’t be trusted.”.

Industry observers note that staircase Ventures managing partner Janet Bannister stated, “Verlo has the product, team and traction to win. When we met Brent, we were immediately impressed by his tenacity, vision, bias for action, and understanding of the nuances of building great products in heavily regulated industries. The team is dedicated to helping wealth management firms stay focused on their clients by reducing their administrative overhead.”.
