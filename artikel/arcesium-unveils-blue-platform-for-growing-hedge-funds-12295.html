Arcesium, a global technology provider serving the investment sector, has rolled out Arcesium Blue, an integrated, AI-ready platform that gives expanding hedge funds a complete front-to-back setup from the moment they go live.

In a recent development, blue pairs cloud-native software with premium managed services. It is aimed at funds running long/short equity, macro, simple credit or single-strategy books. Its purpose is to spare managers from building complicated operational infrastructure themselves, so they can concentrate on generating returns while they grow.

The platform covers real-time portfolio management, order and execution management, compliance and risk, and post-trade operations, linking each stage of the investment lifecycle in one connected system. Newly debuted and rapidly expanding funds often run into disjointed technology and configuration problems. Blue tackles this with a unified stack backed by experienced, institutional-grade professionals who provide managed services.

According to recent reports, on the front-office side, portfolio managers can monitor P&L in real time, simulate trades and run stress tests. The order management system lets users allocate across asset classes from a single order entry screen. Day-to-day tasks such as reporting, accounting, reconciliations and data management take place within the platform and are overseen by Arcesium’s operations specialists. Compliance and risk processes rely on tight data controls and a full audit trail, so firms work with verified data at every stage.

Industry observers note that the Aquata data platform sits at the core of Blue. It offers ready-made connections to external parties and systems, sophisticated data management tools and support for AI-native workflows, which keeps the front office and operational systems aligned on a single set of figures. Owners also get full access to Arcesium Intelligence, an embedded AI environment that works with any model and includes hundreds of preconfigured agents and applications designed to speed up decision-making.

Because Blue uses a focused scope and standardised configurations, it can be deployed swiftly through one provider. As funds mature and take on more sophisticated strategies, they can move into the broader Arcesium ecosystem for a tailored setup without rebuilding their systems.

Arcesium CEO Gaurav Suri mentioned, “Hedge funds require confidence and trust in the technologies and services that fuel their daily operations. This peace of mind allows managers to focus on what they do best without the constraints of fragmented infrastructure. Blue enables funds to focus on growth, with a full front-to-back offering, AI-readiness, and seasoned experts who take investment operations off their plates.

“Clients can take an idea from order to allocation, and through the full trade lifecycle, in one workflow — all powered by AI for efficiency and insight.

In a recent development, horizon Asset COO Stephen McDermott said, “As we continue to extend into new asset classes and strategies, Arcesium brings the institutional pedigree to handle the added complexity. Arcesium Blue gives us a unified front-to-back platform, so we can focus on expanding our strategies, backed by an operating model built to scale alongside them. We are delighted to be a flagship client for Arcesium Blue, and we look forward to growing together.”.
