Breaking: SGD with Large Learning Rates: General Analysis & Improved Rates

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 26 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (179 words, 📖 Standard)

In a recent development, arXiv:2611.32762v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Stochastic Gradient Descent lies at the core of modern machine learning. However its classical analysis typically restricts the stepsize to be below a curvature-dependent stability threshold. Recent developments in deterministic optimization have challenged this paradigm, showing that suitably chosen large stepsizes can not only preserve convergence, but also accelerate Gradient Descent (GD). Whether this phenomenon persists in the presence of stochastic gradient noise has remained an open question (Wu et al., 2024). In this work, we answer this question affirmatively for the cross-entropy loss with linear predictors and two-layer neural networks, establishing an accelerated $\mathcal{O}(T^{-2})$ convergence rate. At the core of our analysis is a fine-grained characterization of the stochastic large-stepsize dynamics. This sharper control of the trajectory further yields tight convergence guarantees within the stable regime, improving existing bounds even in the deterministic setting. Taken together, our results show that the acceleration mechanism induced by large stepsizes survives stochasticity and extends beyond linear models. Numerical experiments demonstrate the benefits of large learning rates in practice.
