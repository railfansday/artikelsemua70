DevDay 2026 is our biggest yet, with more than 20 major announcements across ChatGPT, Codex, our models, and entirely newly introduced forms of working with AI.

We believe AI can help bring about a fresh renaissance of creativity and discovery. It should give people more time for what matters to them, more freedom to pursue their ideas, and the ability to do things they didn’t think were possible.

Industry observers note that today, we introduced agents that can take on ongoing responsibilities and fresh ways for people and AI to work together. We also expanded our commitment to an open ecosystem by opening up ChatGPT as a shared surface where humans and agents can collaborate and where developers can directly launch new native experiences to our collective 1.2B weekly users.
