Industry News: AWS Marketplace now supports VAT credit notes for deemed supply refunds

🌐 Source: Amazon • 📅 13 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (200 words, 📖 Standard)
💡 Key Takeaways
  • According to recent reports, AWS Marketplace sellers can now submit Value Added Tax (VAT) credit notes for refunded deemed supply transactions through a self-service experience in AWS Partner Central.
  • According to recent reports, sellers can view eligible refund line items in the VAT on deemed supply section, select the refunds covered by a credit note, upload a PDF or the applicable electronic invoice format, and track the submission status.
  • These capabilities are available for eligible AWS Marketplace transactions, including Channel Partner Private Offer (CPPO) transactions, in supported EU member states, Norway, and the United Kingdom.

According to recent reports, AWS Marketplace sellers can now submit Value Added Tax (VAT) credit notes for refunded deemed supply transactions through a self-service experience in AWS Partner Central. Previously, VAT was disbursed to sellers for eligible transactions, and when a buyer was later refunded, sellers had to contact AWS to submit the corresponding credit note. This launch simplifies post-refund VAT documentation for sellers.

According to recent reports, sellers can view eligible refund line items in the VAT on deemed supply section, select the refunds covered by a credit note, upload a PDF or the applicable electronic invoice format, and track the submission status. Sellers can consolidate multiple eligible refunds into a single credit note where applicable and review the associated financial adjustment in the Seller Insights reporting.

These capabilities are available for eligible AWS Marketplace transactions, including Channel Partner Private Offer (CPPO) transactions, in supported EU member states, Norway, and the United Kingdom. Country-specific invoicing and electronic-invoicing requirements continue to apply.

To get started, sign in to AWS Partner Central and go to Settings → Tax information → VAT on deemed supply. To learn more, visit the AWS Marketplace Seller Guide or Deemed Supply FAQs.
