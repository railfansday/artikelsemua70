In a recent development, after the first RTX Spark laptops launched earlier this year, Nvidia’s RTX Spark N1X chip is also now available in desktops from Asus, Dell, and MSI. More RTX Spark desktops are coming later from Acer, Gigabyte, and HP, while Microsoft’s Surface RTX Spark Dev Box is slated to start shipping this month. The new mini PCs include a 6144-core Nvidia Blackwell RTX GPU, 20-core Nvidia Grace CPU, and up to 128GB of memory. That high-end hardware comes with a price to match — Asus’s ProArt GR1X starts at $4,499 for a configuration with 64GB of memory and 1TB of storage.

Sources indicate that like the RTX Spark laptops that compete with the MacBook Pro and the DGX Spark desktop Nvidia launched last year, the new mini PCs are Mac Mini or Mac Studio alternatives for developers working with local AI models, as well as RTX-accelerated software. That includes the AI-powered features for tools like Adobe Premiere and Photoshop, and new DLSS 4.5 Ray Reconstruction support in Blender 5.3.

They can run games too, and Windows on Arm game support is improving, but at these prices, that’s not a realistic reason to pick one up yet. Nvidia claims they can act as a “dedicated Windows PC for local models and always-on agents,” and deliver “up to 1 petaflop of AI performance.” They also feature native CUDA support and access to Linux development tools through Windows Subsystem for Linux.
