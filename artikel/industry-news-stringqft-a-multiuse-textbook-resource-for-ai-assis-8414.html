Industry News: StringQFT: A Multiuse Textbook Resource for AI-Assisted Theoretical Physics

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (139 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

arXiv:2611.23225v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: We present StringQFT as a textbook collection for string constructions of quantum field theory and as infrastructure for AI-assisted theoretical physics. The collection comprises 18 volumes, 190 chapters and 3,795 pages, covering brane probes, brane webs, geometric engineering, F-theory constructions, wrapped M5-branes and exact worldsheet constructions. Its open-book benchmark inventory contains 1,073 items, including 499 with automatic finite-answer checking. For retrieval-augmented generation, it provides 1,371 section records linked to textbook sources and PDF locations, together with a local lexical retriever. Training-data preparation is illustrated by 21 private, source-linked examples. For agents, it exposes 19 calculation tools with specified inputs and finite domains, connecting tool use to textbook derivations. We illustrate these uses through specific questions, retrieved passages and finite calculations. The textbook collection and accompanying resources are available at https://github.com/xingyang-yu/StringQFT.
