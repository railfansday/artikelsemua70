📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Luminor has joined forces with FinTech firm Plumery to speed up the rollout of a range of digital banking services in the Baltic region.

Recent findings suggest that through the agreement, Luminor will draw on Plumery’s headless, API-first technology to build and launch digital banking experiences, combining it with its own applications, customer journeys and integrations. This lets the bank bring fresh features to market more quickly while keeping full ownership over how its digital offering is shaped and connected.

The first product to emerge from the tie-up is Luminor Bloom, which runs on the bank’s newly built cloud-based banking platform. Plumery supplies the digital banking interface behind Luminor Bloom, and Luminor layers this with its in-house technology and capabilities to shape the end-to-end customer experience. Because the architecture is modular, the bank can reshape its digital services as the needs of its customers change over time.

Luminor CIO Enkelejd Zotaj said, “We are building a scalable cloud banking platform, and this cooperation helps us shorten the path from ideas to value for our customers.”.

Industry observers note that plumery CEO Ben Goldin noted, “Digital banking transformation should not require banks to compromise on control. Luminor’s approach highlights how banks can combine proven digital banking technology with their own technology, engineering and market expertise to build experiences around their customers’ needs. This provides a flexible foundation that allows Luminor to continue evolving its digital services as customer expectations change.”.
