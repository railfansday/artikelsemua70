Latest Update: Sixfold brings AI underwriting to German MGA exxis

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

exxis, the underwriting agency of insurance broker Ecclesia Group, has become the first managing general agent (MGA) in Germany to run AI underwriting in live production with InsurTech firm Sixfold.

The Sixfold platform is the first AI underwriting tool exxis has adopted. It now covers the agency’s cyber, property and accident business across several carriers. The system is ready for the EU AI Act and works in both German and English.

Recent findings suggest that because exxis underwrites on behalf of multiple carriers throughout the EU, bringing in AI meant satisfying a broad set of conditions. These included readiness for the EU AI Act and DORA, single-tenant hosting with data held in Europe, SOC 2 Type 2 certification, and German-language support in addition to English.

Sources indicate that sixfold fulfilled these conditions while also keeping the guidelines and risk appetite of each carrier distinct for every line of business. As a result, the guidance shown to an underwriter always matches the carrier whose risk they are handling.

When underwriters open a case, it arrives pre-assembled with the carrier’s appetite, relevant sources, highlighted gaps and a draft narrative, allowing them to concentrate on evaluating the risk instead of compiling the file. Each line of business came with its own difficulties. Cyber involved large volumes of questionnaires, property required loss ratios to be worked out manually, and accident carried a documentation burden out of proportion to the premium involved.

According to recent reports, in under two months, the cyber, property and accident lines were running on Sixfold across multiple carriers, and the agency’s underwriters now rely on it daily. exxis is already working with Sixfold to identify further parts of its underwriting process that AI could support.

In a recent development, sixfold describes itself as the AI brain behind better underwriting. It serves L&H and P&C carriers, MGAs and reinsurers in North America, South America, Europe and Australia, with clients including Guardian and Fresh York Life.

Sources indicate that the platform analyses submissions in the manner of a seasoned underwriter, offering recommendations before a person opens the file. As it comes pre-trained with underwriting expertise and connects to existing tools, it is designed to deliver value immediately.

exxis managing director Tim Brozio said, “Bringing AI into underwriting for the first time is a decision you make once, and you have to be able to defend it.

Recent findings suggest that “We write for several insurers within the regulatory framework, so the technology has to meet the requirements and reflect how each line and each carrier actually underwrites. Sixfold delivered on both, and our underwriters started using it on day one.”.

Recent findings suggest that sixfold founder and CEO Alex Schemelkin stated, “When a carrier gives an MGA authority to bind its business, it’s trusting the MGA to underwrite the way the carrier would. exxis gets capacity from several insurers, and Sixfold’s underwriting AI evaluates every submission in the context of each carrier’s appetite. That consistency is what earns exxis the confidence of its carriers and customers.”.
