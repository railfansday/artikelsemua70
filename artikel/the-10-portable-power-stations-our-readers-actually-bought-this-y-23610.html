Setting up a power station for backup power gives you a profound sense of satisfaction. I imagine it’s how preppers feel when they add to their doomsday hoard. The comfort in knowing your home will remain powered during an outage is unlike any other, especially if you have kids or live in an area that experiences a lot of blackouts.

I’ve reached the stage in my life where I’m a significant fan of a steady backup system. Because of this, I enjoy testing power stations of all sizes, and I’m lucky enough to review these devices for a living.

The EcoFlow Delta Pro Ultra X is one of the most powerful power stations that still retains some portability — though that’s saying a lot for 350 pounds. The DPUX can power your whole home with a 12kW to 36kW output and scalable 12 to 180kWh capacity. I have my DPUX as part of a backup power system for my house and keep a DIY solar setup plugged into it with ground-mounted panels.

The Delta Pro Ultra was my first whole-home power station, thanks to its portability and modular design. Each battery has 6,144Wh capacity, and you can stack up to five batteries under one inverter.

It has been revealed that designed to serve as a cohesive smart-home backup system, the EcoFlow Delta Pro Ultra works seamlessly with the Smart Home Panel 2. With this setup, you can easily control your circuits from your phone, simply by using the EcoFlow mobile app. You can turn circuits on and off, monitor energy consumption, and customize your backup system’s behavior during an outage, such as prioritizing certain circuits over others.

The Explorer 5000 Plus is designed to work with your home circuits, much like the EcoFlow DPUX with the Smart Home Panel 3, and supports both 120V and 240V.
