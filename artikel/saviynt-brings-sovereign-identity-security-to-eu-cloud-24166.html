Saviynt brings sovereign identity security to EU cloud

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Saviynt, an identity security specialist focused on AI, has made its cloud-native Identity Security platform available within the European Union through PREMI3NS, the Trusted Cloud service from S3NS that holds qualification from France’s National Cybersecurity Agency (ANSSI).

The launch places Saviynt’s platform inside a locally run cloud environment built for European public bodies, operators of critical infrastructure and regulated businesses. Thales will act as a trusted partner, running and supporting the SaaS service through staff based in the EU.

Recent findings suggest that the move comes as digital sovereignty rises up the agenda for European governments and companies. Organisations across the region want firmer oversight of their sensitive data, technology and infrastructure, alongside greater resilience and less exposure to strategic dependencies on outside providers.

To meet these demands, Saviynt is running its identity security tools on S3NS infrastructure that carries SecNumCloud qualification, with Thales handling operations as a sovereign trusted third party.

Only EU residents employed by S3NS operate and support the service, which sits on PREMI3NS in an environment kept apart from Saviynt’s worldwide commercial cloud. This separation keeps data, infrastructure and day-to-day operations under local control.

Saviynt provides AI-enabled, cloud-native identity security for organisations handling highly sensitive workloads. The new EU service adds to its existing data residency and private cloud options.

Because the offering runs independently of Saviynt’s global commercial cloud, customers can keep essential identity security functions running while cutting their reliance on external infrastructure and services.

According to recent reports, saviynt vice president of product management Niraj Gopal noted, “Saviynt does not believe in a one-size-fits-all approach to sovereignty. Our EU Sovereign Identity Security Cloud complements our data residency and private cloud offerings, extending our portfolio with a modern, cloud-native, AI-enabled SaaS identity security solution. It is designed to help government agencies, regulated enterprises, and critical infrastructure providers protect their critical assets and systems while meeting stringent regulatory and sovereignty requirements.”.

Sources indicate that saviynt field CIO Simon Gooch added, “European organisations should not have to choose between sovereignty and innovation.” He added, “With this offering, we are extending our cloud-native identity security platform to organisations that require complete sovereignty over their identity environments. By combining local control with operational independence, we are helping European organisations protect critical access, strengthen resilience and modernise identity security without compromising their sovereignty requirements.”.
