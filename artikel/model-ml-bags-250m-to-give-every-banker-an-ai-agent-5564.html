Model ML bags $250m to give every banker an AI agent

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Model ML, a London-based personal AI platform for financial and professional services, has secured $250m in a Series B round that values the company at $1.5bn.

Recent findings suggest that general Atlantic and QED Investors co-led the investment. Evolution Equity Partners, Airtree, Forestay Capital, Y Combinator, FT Partners, Latitude, Inception Fund and 13Books Capital also took part. The deal lifts the total capital raised by Model ML to $350m.

The raise comes after a period of rapid expansion. Over the third quarter of 2026, annual recurring revenue at the firm increased twelvefold compared with the same period a year earlier, while daily active people rose 26-fold. Model ML plans to put the newly introduced capital towards growing its research and engineering headcount in London and New York, with the aim of advancing its product and handling larger customer rollouts. The manufacturer also opened offices this year in Paris, Madrid, Tokyo, Singapore and Dubai so it can support clients across their various locations.

Sources indicate that set up by brothers and serial founders Chaz and Arnie Englander, Model ML provides bankers, consultants and investors with an individual AI agent. The agent learns about each user’s clients, active transactions and preferred ways of working, operates on the firm’s own systems and data, runs continuously and tailors itself further with each use.

Industry observers note that rather than waiting to be prompted, it identifies what is relevant, discards distractions and acts. If a company a banker follows makes the news, the agent assesses the significance, considers the consequences and produces analysis in the firm’s house formats within minutes instead of days.

In a recent development, the platform supports a range of deployment options and is frequently installed inside clients’ own infrastructure, meaning their data stays in their environment. Client data is never used for AI model training, helping institutions protect their competitive advantage. The firm holds ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certification and has completed a SOC 2 Type II audit.

In a recent development, model ML does not depend on a single AI vendor. It tests models against genuine financial tasks and assigns each job to whichever model offers the best balance of quality, speed and cost. An increasing proportion of its workload is handled by its own fine-tuned open-source models, which on those tasks cost 2% of what frontier models do, are typically five times quicker and equal or exceed frontier-level accuracy.

The new round builds on a $75m Series A led by FT Partners in November 2025, which also drew backing from Y Combinator, LocalGlobe, Latitude, 13Books Capital and QED Investors.

It has been revealed that model ML co-founder and CEO Chaz Englander said, “AI should do more than give you another answer or notification. It should understand your work and get things done.

“That means knowing what matters, doing the work in the background and bringing you something finished. We’re building a personal AI agent for every banker, consultant and investor, working 24/7 inside the systems their firm already trusts.”.

It has been revealed that general Atlantic managing director and global head of financial services Paul Stamas said, “Model ML is purpose-built for financial and professional services at a time when these institutions are moving quickly from AI experimentation to everyday use. We see a significant opportunity for the platform to become core to how these firms operate, with customers already trusting it with increasingly complex work.

“Model ML’s pace of customer adoption highlights that momentum is inflecting and reflects the team’s highly focused approach to innovation and execution.”.
