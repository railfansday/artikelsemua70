Integral Multipliers and Arithmetic Obstructions for Two-Hidden-Layer ReLU Maxima

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 23 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (182 words, 📖 Standard)

Sources indicate that arXiv:2611.27018v1 Announce Type: newly introduced Abstract: We study exact representations of maximum functions by ReLU networks with two hidden layers and integer parameters. The least positive output multiplier is two for the six-input maximum, and either two or six for the seven-input maximum. We obtain these bounds from integral cubical fillings and a coefficient-preserving passage from polytope identities to networks. For the coordinate-facet complex of the six-simplex, we compute a coning index of six over the integers and three after inverting two, allowing arbitrary integral directions within each facet. A class in the homology of the unrestricted kernel complex characterizes the remaining seven-input problem; its vanishing is equivalent to an integral filling of a fixed target. We also construct a finite character that separates rational from three-local representability in a dictionary containing two known constructions and all pairs of edge-disjoint three-edge forests. This obstruction permits nonsymmetric coefficients and is confined to the specified dictionary. Control of lower-dimensional remainders yields a sufficient criterion in the top-dimensional quotient. Exact certificates and verification programs accompany the finite calculations.
