Graph Signal Generation via Graph-Aware Diffusion Models

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 23 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (217 words, 📖 Standard)

arXiv:2611.27826v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Many signals are defined on the nodes of a graph, such as traffic flow at road sensors or user purchases of related products. They are often partly observed, and learning their distribution enables imputing missing values and forecasting future ones. Diffusion models learn such distributions, but their forward noise is independent across nodes and does not depend on the graph. We introduce graph-aware diffusion (GAD), whose forward process is a heat diffusion on the graph driven by Gaussian noise. In the graph Fourier basis, the forward transition is a scalar Gaussian channel at each frequency. With a shared diffusion rate, high graph frequencies are attenuated faster than low ones. We propose Mode-Adaptive SNR Scheduling (MASS), which assigns each graph frequency its own diffusion rate and keeps all of them at the same SNR. For graph-stationary signals, MASS equalizes the normalized error of the optimal linear denoiser across frequencies. We bound reverse-SDE stiffness as a function of the (shifted) Laplacian's condition number, with a logarithmic upper bound for MASS and a linear lower bound for shared rates. GAD with MASS improves recommendation accuracy by 5-19%, reduces traffic-imputation RMSE by up to 7.8% with half as many reverse steps, and improves forecasting MAE and RMSE using only 3 reverse steps.
