From chargers to headrest hooks to Android Auto and CarPlay accessories, there’s a lot to consider for your ride. ZDNET editors, including myself, have tested more than a few car gadgets in recent years. These are some of our favorite car tech devices you can shop for right now — some of which are on sale for Black Friday now.

The AAWireless Two Plus enables wireless Android Auto and CarPlay connectivity in any car, with a multifunctional button designed for quick pairing and switching between Android and iPhone devices. ZDNET Senior Contributing Editor Kerry Wan states the gadget delivers the best wireless Android Auto experience yet.

It has been revealed that the Statik MagStack Pro is a tough, high-quality, well-made cable. / Adrian Kingsley-Hughes/ZDNET.

Recent findings suggest that the Statik MagStack cable has a magnetic design that prevents tangles and is a personal favorite of mine and ZDNET gadget reviewer Adrian Kingsley-Hughes. This cable supports both charging and data transfer, making it compatible with Android Auto and CarPlay. It features 480 Mbps data transfer, 24 pins, and a max voltage of 5. This cable also comes in other USB configurations for your vehicle or another space, like your home office.

This MagSafe-compatible phone mount clips into your air vent or mounts to your car. It features a cooling fan, a built-in Qi2 charger, illumination, and more. It’s great for charging your phone on the go while keeping your device hands-free. It also doubles as a mount to keep your phone stable while following directions or maps. Plus, the wireless car charger supports both iPhone and Android Qi‑enabled smartphones and includes a metal ring for secure mounting and charging with non‑MagSafe devices and cases. It’s a great, sturdy magnet, and I’ve really enjoyed having it in my car, especially for long round trips. I like knowing my phone is secure from bumps on the road, and that I can get a quick charge when I need to.

In a recent development, the AirFly Drive works with both CarPlay and Android Auto, and connects to two phones at once. Plus, it’s compact and affordable, costing under $40.

Sources indicate that if you don’t want a car charger for your device but still want an easy and affordable way to mount it for long trips or hands-free travel, this clip from Lamicall has been a steady presence in my car for over 5 years. It’s no-fuss and no-frills, but it’s reliable and useful.

It has been revealed that I always travel with a classic car charger that comes with cables for Android Auto or Apple CarPlay, charging, and more. This one from Pioneer works with iPhone and Android, and it offers a USB-C and USB-A port for a dual-port experience. This is also a great option to plug in MagSafe charging mounts or other accessories if you don’t have enough USB-C ports in your vehicle (or don’t have any at all, depending on your car model). It’s pricier but has held up well on numerous long trips, and the connection and charging it provides are stable.

Sources indicate that these car hooks slip between your headrest and the top of the seat, adding a place to store purses, bags, and other incidentals from the back of the seat. This multipack from Amazon comes in over a dozen colors to match your interior or provide a pop of color, and I love having them in my car for my handbag. Plus, they’re less than $10. They are not the most secure hook on the market, but they’re affordable and get the job done. I also like using these with a plastic grocery bag as a makeshift trash bag while on long car rides.

Recent findings suggest that this gadget is more for your car keys than your actual car. The She’s Birdie is a personal safety alarm with a wildly loud siren built in. I’ve carried this on my keychain for almost 7 years, and it’s a great investment for college students, parents with small children, older adults, and others, and for $30, I recommend everyone scoop one up. When the top pin of the Birdie is pulled out, a 130db siren sounds and a strobe light flashes for up to 40 minutes. To put that in perspective, a 130db sound is equivalent to a jackhammer and nearly equal to the sound of a jet engine in the distance, according to the Hearing Health Foundation.

It has been revealed that if you’re heading out on a long road trip, keeping your documents tidy is a good tip. This handy organizer keeps your important documents, like your car registration and insurance, in one place, so if you find yourself in an accident or pulled over for a road violation, they’re all ready to go. I have one in my own car, and I also appreciate that it keeps my documents secure and easy to find. I picked the punchy pink color, so I can rapidly spot and pull it out of my dashboard.
