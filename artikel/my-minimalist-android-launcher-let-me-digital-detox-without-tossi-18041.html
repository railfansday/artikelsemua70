Recent findings suggest that I’ve been on a quest to minimize my dependence on my Android phone. It started with blocking sensitive information from appearing on my lock screen. The next step was turning off notifications on my lock screen completely. I started feeling more at ease throughout the day. I no longer felt the pull to pick up my phone to see if someone had replied to something, if something happened in the news, or if I missed social media posts.

Then I considered my Android phone’s home screen launcher, which is the window to our mobile digital world. I’d already switched from the default Pixel launcher to Mango Launcher, which I was enjoying and which slightly lessened the distractions. But could a more minimal launcher bring me even more peace?

In a recent development, I’d already tried AIO Launcher, and found it to be one of the coolest minimal launchers that I’d ever tried. Then I found three that are even better: Ying Yang Launcher, Olauncher, and Indistractable Minimalist Launcher.

According to recent reports, yin Yang Launcher aims to help you use your phone less, and it does a remarkable job. With Yin Yang Launcher, your Android screen is replaced with two options: basic and minimal. The minimal option is what I chose to focus on, as it breaks the Android display into four pages:.

If you swipe down from the center of the display, you get a search page, where you can search for installed apps or search the web. Of course, if you swipe down from the top, you get the standard Android Notification Shade.

Recent findings suggest that long-press the main page to access the settings, where you can customize the home screen, appearance, launcher settings, system settings, and the Mind Space. The Mind Space is a unique feature that serves as a focus mode, where you can select which apps to use and set focus schedules. You can also trigger a manual focus mode and set how long it lasts and which apps are allowed. If you purchase the Pro version of Yin Yang Launcher, you can enable strict mode, which means you cannot disable focus mode until the set time elapses.

Just using Yin Yang Launcher gives me a sense of peace that no other launcher offers.

Sources indicate that yin Yang Launcher is free to install from the Google Play Store. There is also a Pro version that adds more features and removes ads. The Pro version is $14.99/yr or $29.99/lifetime.

In a recent development, I’ve written about Olauncher before. It’s similar to Yin Yang Launcher, without the extra features. With Olauncher, you get a home page with the apps you choose to list; a right swipe opens the phone app, a left swipe opens the camera app, a swipe up opens the full list of apps, and a swipe down opens the Notification Shade.

Long-press the home screen to gain access to the Olauncher Settings, where you can customize the appearance, gestures, the number of apps on the home screen, and a few more options.

It has been revealed that olauncher is about as minimal as it gets. If you want more, you can purchase the Pro version, which adds features like weather, widgets, fonts, folders, AMOLED wallpapers, and more. Right now, Olauncher Pro is still in early access, so you can use it for free.

According to recent reports, indistractable Minimalist Launcher is all about digital detox, and it does a great job with it. You get a text-based layout that lists the time and date, the year and day in progress, your calendar, and a text-based app launcher list of favorites you set.

That’s the long and short of Indistractable Minimalist Launcher, and for anyone looking for a complete digital detox, this is what you want. Indistractable Minimalist Launcher looks like a throwback to an older age of computing, and that’s intentional. If you want the absolute bare minimum, with no distractions, install this launcher.

Right now, Indistractable Minimalist Launcher is in early access, so it’s free to install and use from the Google Play Store.

As for my current minimalist launcher of choice, I prefer Yin Yang Launcher for phones and the aforementioned AIO Launcher for tablets.
