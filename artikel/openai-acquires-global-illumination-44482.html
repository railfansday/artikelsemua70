OpenAI acquires Global Illumination

🌐 Source: Openai • 📅 16 Aug 2023 • ⏱️ 1 min read (109 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

The entire team has joined OpenAI.

Recent findings suggest that openAI has acquired the team at Global Illumination⁠(opens in a latest window), a organization founded by Thomas Dimson, Taylor Gordon, and Joey Flynn. The entire team has joined OpenAI to work on our core products including ChatGPT. Global Illumination is a company that has been leveraging AI to build creative tools, infrastructure, and digital experiences. The team previously designed and built products early on at Instagram and Facebook and have also made significant contributions at YouTube, Google, Pixar, Riot Games, and other notable companies. We’re very excited for the impact they’ll have here at OpenAI.
