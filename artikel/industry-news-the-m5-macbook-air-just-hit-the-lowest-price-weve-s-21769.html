The 13-inch Apple MacBook Air M5 is now on sale for Black Friday, with the 13-inch starting at $1,049.99 at Amazon and Best Buy, or 19% off, and the 15-inch starting at $1,249.00 at Amazon and Best Buy, or 17% off — the lowest prices we’ve seen since Apple’s blanket price increase across its entire MacBook and iPad lineup in June.

This means consumers are paying 15% to 25% more now than they would have on the same device a couple of months ago. While Amazon has changed its prices to match, some of the latest MacBook Air models are still less expensive there than at Apple. For example, the 13-inch MacBook Air M5 now starts at $1,199.00 in the Apple Store, but it’s $1,049.99 at Amazon and Best Buy– a $150 discount. The 15-inch starts at $1,499.00 in the Apple Store, but is $250 off at Amazon and Best Buy.

The increased cost is largely due to unprecedented demand for RAM and NAND flash storage from AI infrastructure companies, which are scooping it up for themselves, leading to a shortage for consumer electronics. This price increase should come as no surprise, however. Former Apple CEO Tim Cook had warned of an impending price hike in an interview with the Wall Street Journal, admitting that it had reached a point where higher prices were “unavoidable.”.
