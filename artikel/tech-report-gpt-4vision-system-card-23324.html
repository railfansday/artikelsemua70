Tech Report: GPT-4V(ision) system card

🌐 Source: Openai • 📅 25 Sep 2023 • ⏱️ 1 min read (133 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

It has been revealed that GPT‑4 with vision (GPT‑4V) enables customers to instruct GPT‑4 to analyze image inputs provided by the user, and is the latest capability we are making broadly available. Incorporating additional modalities (such as image inputs) into large language models (LLMs) is viewed by some as a key frontier in artificial intelligence research and development. Multimodal LLMs offer the possibility of expanding the impact of language-only systems with novel interfaces and capabilities, enabling them to solve latest tasks and provide novel experiences for their users. In this system card, we analyze the safety properties of GPT‑4V. Our work on safety for GPT‑4V builds on the work done for GPT‑4 and here we dive deeper into the evaluations, preparation, and mitigation work done specifically for image inputs.
