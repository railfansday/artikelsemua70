Zeidler Group unveils AI tool to speed up DDQ and RFP work

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Zeidler Group, an international specialist in investment fund legal, regulatory and compliance services, has rolled out DART (Due Diligence and RFP Tool), an AI-driven platform built to help financial market firms handle due diligence questionnaires (DDQs) and requests for proposals (RFPs) more efficiently.

The tool’s central feature is an intelligent Autofill function. It pulls from a firm’s internal documentation and its archive of earlier answers to complete DDQs and RFPs automatically, so teams no longer need to begin every questionnaire from a blank page. Questions that cannot be answered this way are flagged, which lets staff direct their effort to the areas that need human input.

According to recent reports, DART was created after clients asked for it, and it draws on the technology underpinning Zeidler Due Diligence (ZDD). It targets a persistent burden for investment managers: finishing lengthy, frequently repetitive questionnaires that need contributions from several departments.

At many firms, compliance, operations, legal and other teams still handle these requests largely by hand. They track down information, return to questions already answered in the past, gather contributions and obtain sign-off. As requests grow in number and complexity, this work can pull resources away from more valuable tasks.

The platform puts the whole process into a single shared workspace that pairs AI automation with structured workflows and human oversight. Firms can follow a questionnaire from the moment it arrives, through assigning questions to the right colleagues, to review, approval and submission. DART also offers a central repository of documents and past answers that can be reused, tools for assigning tasks across teams, a formal review and approval stage, and full tracking of incoming requests, responsibilities and progress.

Zeidler Group provides specialist law, regulatory and compliance services for investment funds worldwide. Its ZDD platform helps firms carry out due diligence on delegates, counterparties and service providers.

Zeidler Group chief commercial officer Serena Goldberg said, “DART is a direct response to what our clients have been asking us for. Asset managers are under increasing pressure to do more with their existing resources, yet highly skilled teams continue to spend significant time on repetitive administrative processes.

“We saw an opportunity to change that. By combining AI-powered automation with a practical, collaborative workflow, DART helps firms work more efficiently while maintaining control over their responses. It is another example of how we are translating client feedback into technology that addresses real operational challenges.”.

Zeidler Group head of due diligence Mathilde Stich said, “Anyone who has worked on a complex DDQ or RFP knows how much time can be spent gathering information, following up with colleagues and answering questions that the organisation has already addressed many times before.

Recent findings suggest that “With DART, we wanted to eliminate as much of that repetitive work as possible. The AI prepares responses using existing information and highlights where further input is required, while teams retain responsibility for reviewing and approving the final answers. The result is a more structured, collaborative process that allows people to focus on the questions that genuinely require their expertise.”.
