📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Schroders Wealth Management has chosen Backbase, the provider of an AI-native Wealth & Banking OS, to bring its advisers and clients together on a single digital platform as part of a multi-year investment in how customers engage with the firm.

It has been revealed that under the agreement, Backbase’s technology will be rolled out across Schroders’ international wealth arm as well as Cazenove Capital in the UK and the Channel Islands. The platform will bring every stage of the client relationship onto one system, covering onboarding, servicing, portfolio visibility and operations. A fresh client app and an adviser workspace are expected to launch in 2027.

The aim is to give clients an easier and more joined-up digital journey, whether they are signing up with the firm, checking portfolio details or handling their relationship on a day-to-day basis. Advisers, meanwhile, will gain access to client data and servicing tools in a single location, which is intended to cut complexity and free up more of their time for client work.

Industry observers note that web, mobile, onboarding and servicing functions will all be provided through the one platform. Backbase’s system will operate on Schroders Wealth Management’s own infrastructure, meaning the firm retains control over its client data.

Backbase founder and CEO Jouk Pleiter stated, “Wealth managers increasingly want their digital experience to match the quality of the advice behind it, and that is the shift Backbase is built to support. Schroders Wealth Management is building toward a model where relationships get stronger as the firm grows, and every conversation starts from a deep understanding of the client.”.

Schroders Wealth Management global chief operating officer Andreas Piepke stated, “Our clients choose us for the advice and the relationship, and every investment we make has to strengthen both.

“Ultimately, this is about making it easier for clients to interact with us and giving our advisers more time and better information to focus on what matters most: understanding our clients and helping them achieve their goals. The digital experience created by Backbase will support that digital transformation, enabling future services and innovation. Over time, this foundation will support further automation, streamlined journeys and carefully governed AI-enabled services.”.
