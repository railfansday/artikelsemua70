Recent findings suggest that kitchenAid’s strategy is one we should all live by—if it ain’t broke, don’t fix it. The KitchenAid stand mixer has remained nearly unchanged since its introduction in 1914 and has become such a legendary kitchen staple that we called the KitchenAid Artisan 7-Quart Lift Stand Mixer an “everlasting” kitchen must-have and included it in our Buy It For Life guide. KitchenAid’s heralded and beloved kitchen appliances, such as stand mixers, coffee makers, grinders, and blenders, often go on sale, so you can get these great, but pricey, essentials for less.

Buying upcoming kitchen appliances is one of the biggest and most expensive (and notable!) purchases you can make, so be sure to take advantage of these sales. During the KitchenAid Black Friday sale, you can save up to 33% on KitchenAid dishwashers and select models. Plus, you’ll get an extra 5% off at checkout when you use KitchenAid promo code BFDISH5 through December 2 on refrigerators, ranges, cooktops, dishwashers, microwaves, wall ovens, ice makers, wine centers, and hoods. There are also many other perks when you buy right now at KitchenAid, including free delivery and haul-away on major appliances over $599 when you’re signed in, an extra year of warranty, 0% APR Financing, and free returns.

According to recent reports, one of the best perks of shopping online at KitchenAid is the rotating deals and discounts on some of their best-selling appliances. If you really want to level up this spring, consider these sleek, long-lasting appliances from KitchenAid. Other great deals include free delivery and haul-away on major appliances over $399 when you're signed in to your KitchenAid account.

Speaking of great offers, there are other KitchenAid promo codes specifically for essential workers, such as teachers, students, health care workers, military, and first responders, as well as those over 50. If you are a member of one of those groups, all you need to do is verify your account with SheerID to receive the extra discounts (on top of any other accounts if you are an account holder). The verification form is in the “Profile Information” section under “My Account” on KitchenAid’s site. Don’t fall into one of these categories? No worries! Those who have recently moved can also save 15%.

Even without a discount code, you can still save with regular deals, including great savings on refurbished items and free delivery on all appliances sitewide. Account holders also get access to exclusive perks and discounts sitewide—all you have to do is create an account. Be on the lookout for other great savings events from KitchenAid (like a discount on that stand mixer you’ve been eyeing all year).
