Aria Advisory has chosen the reserving platform of Akur8 to underpin the reserving and Appointed Actuary work it carries out for property and casualty (P&C) insurers across Canada.

Under the arrangement, Aria Advisory will roll out Akur8 Reserving, taking on both the Arius Deterministic and Arius Stochastic analysis tools. It will pair these with Triangles on Demand, a tool that handles the preparation of claims data and the building of loss triangles.

The move is expected to shorten the time it takes Aria Advisory and its clients to produce reserve estimates. Every projection will also be backed by a full audit trail, while stochastic ranges will sit next to the conventional deterministic approaches that actuaries have long relied upon.

Akur8 Reserving is designed to patch the way reserve analysis is performed. The platform combines sophisticated stochastic modelling with dependable deterministic tools in a single environment, with the aim of raising both efficiency and reliability across the reserving process.

Aria Advisory was established by David Mamane, FCIA FCAS MAAA, who continues to lead the business. The firm focuses on the Canadian P&C market, providing actuarial and insurance advisory expertise. Its services span P&C actuarial work, insurance management and enterprise risk management advice.

Aria Advisory founder and CEO David Mamane said, “Aria Advisory serves primarily Canadian P&C insurance companies and holds Appointed Actuary positions for many of them. As such, it is crucial to rely on a proven solution with a track record of excellence and innovation. This, together with strong relationships that we have built over the years, is why we chose to partner with Akur8.”.

Sources indicate that akur8 CEO and co-founder Sam Falmagne mentioned, “Aria Advisory brings care and judgment to every client relationship. David and his team take the time to understand each organization, work closely with its people, and stand behind the analysis they deliver. Akur8 is proud to be their partner as they grow this practice in Canada. Together, we can help actuaries spend less time managing the mechanics of reserving and more time advising the people who rely on their expertise.”.
