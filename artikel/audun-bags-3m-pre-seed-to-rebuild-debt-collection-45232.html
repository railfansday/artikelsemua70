📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Audun, an AI-native debt collection start-up, has secured $3m in pre-seed funding as it sets out to overhaul one of the most manual corners of financial services.

The round drew support from Alliance VC, Y Combinator, Robinhood Ventures, Skyfall Ventures and FirstDegree, along with a group of angel investors.

In a recent development, audun is taking a different route from most innovators in the space. For years, change in debt collection has largely meant building software and selling it to collection agencies. Rather than offering another product of that kind, Audun has set itself up as a licensed agency in its own right. It is already recovering money for clients using its own platform, which handles every stage of a case, from the opening reminder through to the final payment.

The organization argues that technology is moving faster than ever, yet collection practices look much as they did two decades ago. In its view, every debtor is pushed through an identical routine, while case handlers are tied up with repetitive admin. Audun believes the distance between what technology makes possible and what the sector actually provides is now wider than at any point before.

Audun’s platform takes over the routine, administrative side of collection so that its staff can devote their attention to the conversations where human judgement matters most.
