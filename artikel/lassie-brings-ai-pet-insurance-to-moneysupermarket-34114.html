Lassie, the Swedish pet health and insurance business, has struck a deal with MoneySuperMarket that makes the comparison platform the sole UK site where consumers can compare and purchase its prevention-focused pet cover for an initial three months.

The arrangement is designed to widen Lassie’s reach among UK pet owners. Through its app, policyholders can make a claim simply by taking a photo of their vet’s receipt, with the firm’s AI-driven process seeing upwards of 60% of claims worldwide wrapped up in roughly six minutes end to end. The same app also lets customers handle the day-to-day management of their policy.

Lassie entered the UK market in July and pairs insurance with tailored preventative care, aiming to support healthier pets through routine activities. Consumers receive weekly programmes shaped around their animal’s breed, age and life stage, covering areas such as nutrition, vaccinations, socialisation and training. The platform offers over 200 short courses alongside expert video content, and owners collect points for finishing health-related tasks. These points can then be converted into cheaper premiums, discounts and loyalty perks.

The InsurTech counts the owners of over 300,000 cats and dogs as customers across the UK, Sweden, Germany and France.

Recent findings suggest that moneySuperMarket director of insurance Sara Newell stated, “We’re always looking for newly introduced ways to give our customers more choice and help them find pet insurance that works for them. Lassie brings something genuinely different to the market by combining cover with practical tools and rewards that help owners look after their pets. We’re pleased to make Lassie available through a UK price comparison site for the first time, making it easier for pet owners to compare their options and find the right cover.”.

It has been revealed that lassie CEO and co-founder Hedda Båverud Olsson mentioned, “Partnering with MoneySuperMarket puts Lassie in front of a far larger share of UK pet owners at the moment they’re looking for cover. We know that many pet health problems can be prevented, so we believe pet insurance should do more than pay claims once something has gone wrong. We built Lassie to help owners stay ahead of issues, using AI, behavioural science and gamification to make everyday pet care simpler and more rewarding. We’re delighted to bring this approach to MoneySuperMarket’s customers, helping them keep their pets healthier for longer and making it simple to claim when they need to.”.
