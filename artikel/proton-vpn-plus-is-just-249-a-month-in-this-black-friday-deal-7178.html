According to recent reports, proton claims VPN Plus will let you connect up to 10 devices at the same time, and you can choose from more than 20,000 servers in over 140 countries. Proton VPN is our pick for the best free VPN, but the free plan limits you to one device and servers in five countries, and you won’t be able to stream with it. Given that, the paid plan offers great value, especially at this price.
