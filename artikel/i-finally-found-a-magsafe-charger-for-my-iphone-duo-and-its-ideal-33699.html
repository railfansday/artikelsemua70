I finally found a MagSafe charger for my iPhone Duo – and it’s ideal for travel

Sources indicate that the iPhone Duo is one of the most exciting phones I’ve tried this year. It’s fresh, fun, and best of all: it folds in half. However, its unorthodox short, wide design isn’t compatible with traditional MagSafe accessories, especially battery packs designed for regular iPhones. Most magnetic power banks are left hanging off the bottom of the phone, which both looks bad and compromises charging efficiency.

Industry observers note that mobile accessories brand Baseus has a solution, though. It just released a slim, wide power bank that fits the foldable iPhone perfectly. I’ve been using the Baseus PicoGo AM72 power bank for over a week now, and can already say that it’s a must-have for power users.

According to recent reports, the Baseus PicoGo AM72 power bank measures 85.3 × 81 × 7.9 mm, which easily sits within the iPhone Duo’s 117.8 x 84.1 × 11.3 mm design. This wide form factor allows it to attach to the phone without hanging off the sides. The rounded corners are a nice touch for design consistency, and looks like it was made for the Duo with an uninterrupted feel.

On one side, the PicoGo AM72 has a circular ring that mimics the MagSafe coils, allowing you to properly align it with the back of the iPhone. It is raised from the body, allowing for a small gap between the phone and power bank. Baseus claims it helps keep temperatures in check for both devices. In my testing, it got warm to the touch, but never uncomfortably hot.

At the bottom, you get a power button and two lanyard-attachment holes. Plus, there’s a Type-C port on its side. It comes in Silver and Blue, but the latter is expected to start shipping in Dec. 2026.

The Baseus PicoGo AM72 power bank packs a 5,000mAh cell inside its 7.9mm body, and supports 15W Qi2 wireless fast charging and up to 25W wired power transfer. It recharges at 18W over USB-C, and is rated to provide less than one full charge on an iPhone Duo. You can always carry a 10,000mAh power bank for multiple charges, but it won’t give you this combination of quick top-up and portability.

When charging my iPhone wirelessly, I could slide it in my pocket and take it out without any challenges. The battery pack stayed in place, thanks to its strong magnets. I tried both wireless and wired charging. In wired mode (25W), my iPhone Duo went from 10% to 50% within 25 minutes, while wireless charging took about 38 minutes (at 15W). Oh, and you don’t need your own cable. Baseus includes a high-quality braided cable in the box — another nice touch.

You can simply attach the PicoGo AM72 to the back of your iPhone Duo, and it’ll start charging your iPhone with the press of its power button. However, if you want to monitor charging status and check battery health, you can install the Baseus app via NFC. It allows you to set up the power bank by simply touching it to the back of the iPhone.

Unlike the iPhone Air, Apple didn’t launch a dedicated power bank for its new iPhone Duo, and that’s for a reason: it has great battery life. In my week of use, Apple’s foldable iPhone has consistently given me above-average battery life, but if you have battery anxiety and travel often, I recommend buying the PicoGo AM72.

It has been revealed that it’s thin, light, and gives you a quick top-up within minutes. The braided USB-C to -C cable is a good add-on as well. At $80, this isn’t the cheapest power bank on the market, but it is worth the asking price, thanks to its premium build quality and finish.
