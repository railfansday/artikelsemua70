The Garmin Fenix 8 Pro is not the latest model, but it’s an excellent option, especially at $250 off. It connects seamlessly to Android and iOS smartphones, in addition to golf club sensors, bike sensors, and more. Just about every sport is covered with an updated smartphone experience, so you can customize the watch settings on your phone and sync them over to the watch.

The smartwatch features a vibrant AMOLED display for ultra-readability and an LED flashlight, which reviewer Matt Miller says “makes the Fenix 8 Pro an essential tool in my collection.” Review: Garmin Fenix 8 Pro.

Sources indicate that the Forerunner 165 is our pick for the best Garmin watch for casual runners and pickleball players. Our reviewer Matt Miller notes that even at this more affordable price, Garmin’s Forerunner 165 includes a brilliant AMOLED touchscreen display for that elegant Android smartwatch visual experience found in Samsung and Apple smartwatches.

Industry observers note that the Apple Watch Series 12 earned our Editors’ Choice award and a rare 5/5 “Excellent” rating. In her in-depth review, wearables and health tech expert Nina Raemont said, “The Apple Watch Series 12 is as good as a highly accurate smartwatch can get. Apple adds stronger sensors to the back of the watch to boost heart rate sensing accuracy, and this functionality touches almost all of the Apple Watch’s newest health features. The upcoming longevity features rival Oura, Whoop, and other competitors, while keeping the price of the watch lower and subscription-free (for now).”.

Review: Finally, the health tracker I’ve been waiting for.

The Pixel Watch 5 is the latest in Google’s lineup. Raemont called it a “faster, intelligent smartwatch with some helpful health and wellness features under the hood” in her review. She was also a huge fan of the Strength Training feature, which turned out to be good enough to replace her favorite strength training app.

“The Pixel Watch 5 feature should help beginners feel more comfortable in the gym by giving them workout recommendations and taking them through their exercises and rest periods,” she wrote.

Review: The Pixel Watch 5 could help me lift heavy – and replace my top strength training app.

If you want to closely manage your health and are in the Android ecosystem, the Samsung Galaxy Watch 8 provides an antioxidant test, ECG, sleep apnea detection, body composition, and more, so that you always know your key health and wellness metrics.
