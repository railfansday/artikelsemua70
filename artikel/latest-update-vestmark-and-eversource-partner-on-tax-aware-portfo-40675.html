Vestmark, a wealth management technology and outsourced services specialist, has teamed up with EverSource Wealth Advisors, a fast-expanding registered investment advisor (RIA) serving independent advisory businesses across the US, to broaden access to model portfolios and tax-conscious portfolio management.

It has been revealed that as part of the agreement, EverSource will deploy Vestmark VAST, the company’s tax management and overlay offering, alongside the Vestmark Model Marketplace. The aim is to equip advisors to build more tailored portfolios that account for tax considerations, while also opening up a wider selection of investment strategies.

EverSource’s assets under management have climbed from roughly $1.2bn in 2022 to over $6bn in 2026. To support this expansion, the firm turned to Vestmark to simplify how new advisory businesses are brought on board, strengthen the way tax-aware portfolios are put into practice and give advisors more choice among third-party models.

A deciding factor was Vestmark’s compatibility with the technology vendors EverSource already relies on, meaning the RIA can grow its investment capabilities without overhauling its existing set-up.

According to recent reports, using the tax-analysis and proposal tools within VAST, EverSource advisors will be able to assess the likely tax impact of moving a portfolio before any investment changes are made. This is expected to give clients clearer visibility of how their decisions affect their tax position, as well as making the onboarding of portfolios and continuing tax management more efficient.

Meanwhile, the Model Marketplace will connect EverSource with a wide pool of third-party asset managers and model strategies, while still allowing the firm to include managers it already partners with. Combined, the two offerings are designed to make the delivery of both in-house and external strategies smoother and to help EverSource keep costs under control as it scales.

Vestmark provides technology and services for the wealth management industry. Its platform underpins over $2tn in assets, upwards of 5.5 million investor accounts and in excess of 72,000 financial advisors.

Vestmark EVP, advisory solutions Rob Battista noted, “Growing RIAs need the ability to deliver more personalized investment solutions without adding operational complexity.

“By combining tax-aware portfolio implementation, access to a broad range of investment strategies, and integration with EverSource’s existing technology, we’re helping the firm scale its investment operations while giving advisors greater flexibility to serve their clients.”.

EverSource Wealth Advisors chief investment officer Daniel Phillips remarked, “Our stewardship-oriented investment philosophy is built around giving advisors the tools to help clients balance investment objectives, risk, taxes, and values-based considerations.

Sources indicate that “Vestmark gives our advisors better tools to evaluate the tax implications of portfolio decisions and communicate those considerations to clients. Just as important, its ability to work with our existing technology will help us bring additional advisors and client accounts onto our platform without adding unnecessary complexity.”.
