AI features and assistants are swarming many of the platforms and apps we use on a daily basis. Whether in your email, on social media, or while shopping online, there’s a chance you’ve seen a mention of an AI tool somewhere.

Sources indicate that but companies are increasingly giving you more control over that. Just like you can turn off AI in your search results, your computer, and your browser, you can now also disable at least some AI features in your favorite apps, most recently Canva.

Also: I tested Siri Recap on my Apple Watch for a week – then turned it off, for good.

Canva is a popular graphic design tool that just added the ability to switch off its generative AI tools. As a creative application, Canva leverages AI to generate images and extend images with Magic Expand.

Following this process to turn off AI in Canva won’t disable all AI-based features. Some assistive editing tools, like background removal and Magic Grab, cannot be turned off.

Canva can use information from your general usage to train its AI features, which is enabled by default.

You can prevent AI from learning from your general Canva usage by going to your Privacy Controls in your account and turning off the toggle for “AI-powered features can learn and improve with your general usage.”.
