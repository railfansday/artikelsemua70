Between putting up decorations, entertaining guests, and meal prep, the holidays are busy enough without worrying about vacuuming. Thankfully, a proper robot vacuum can do the dirty work while you focus on more enjoyable things. And thanks to Black Friday, you can pick up some of the best we’ve tested for up to 60 percent off.

Industry observers note that we’re seeing some excellent deals on a wide range of robot vacuums, including some of our favorite models from Roborock, Dreame, and Eufy. There are also rare discounts already available on premium models like the Matic and Matic Serene, which impressed us with their almost humanlike navigation, while midrange options such as the Eufy Omni C28 have dropped into budget territory. Whether you want a high-end model that cleans itself or something budget-friendly but still powerful, we’ve rounded up the best deals we’ve found so far.

Despite lacking a multifunction dock, the budget-friendly robot vacuum and mop offers an impressive set of features for the price, including AI-powered obstacle avoidance, sonic mopping, automatic mop lifting, and a self-emptying dock.

Roborock’s first roller-mop robot vacuum, the Curv 2 Flow, features flagship specs at a midrange price, including excellent mopping, a retractable mop cover, 20,000Pa suction, AI-powered obstacle avoidance, and a compact design.

Sources indicate that the Matic robot vacuum and mop is ideal for cluttered homes with pets, multiple floors, and a mixture of carpets and hardwood. It offers almost humanlike navigation, tackling high-pile rugs and mopping up wet spills. The quiet bot also requires less maintenance than many rivals thanks to its onboard water tank and dust bag. Read our review.

The Eufy Omni C28 combines excellent mopping performance with a small self-cleaning dock that’s well suited for apartments and other smaller spaces. It navigates around furniture and into tight areas with ease, while its roller mop does a great job cleaning hard floors.

Industry observers note that the Dreame X60 Ultra is a powerful robot vacuum and mop that does a great job of cleaning carpets thanks to its dual roller brushes and 35,000Pa of suction. It can climb over tall thresholds, squeeze under low furniture, and automatically detach its mop pads when vacuuming carpets.

If you have all hardwood floors, the Narwal Flow 2 is an excellent option. A superb mop with a wide flat roller pad and an excellent vacuum, the Flow 2 also has top-notch navigation and obstacle detection. Its sleek base station is also less of an eyesore than most.

Industry observers note that roborock’s Saros 20 stands out for its ability to climb tall thresholds, avoid obstacles with impressive accuracy, and clean under low furniture thanks to its slim design. It also combines powerful 36,000Pa suction with great spinning mop pads, which is why we recommend it for homes with multiple floor types and tricky transitions between rooms.

Ecovacs’ robot vacuum and mop can pretreat stains, keep carpets dry with a smart mop cover, and deliver powerful 22,000Pa suction. It also includes an all-in-one dock that automatically empties the dustbin, refills the water tanks, and washes the mop with heated, pressurized water.

Sources indicate that the Matter-compatible SwitchBot K11 Plus is ideal for small apartments, bedrooms, and home offices. It can easily navigate around chair and desk legs and into tight spaces that most other bots miss. It also features a commendable 6,000Pa of suction power — which is great for this price point — and works well on both hardwood and carpet.

With high suction power, an improved brush system, and the ability to scale thresholds up to 6cm, the X50 Ultra stands out as a great robot vacuum-mop hybrid. While its 20,000Pa suction isn’t quite as powerful as the X60 Ultra’s and it can’t tackle especially high thresholds as well, it’s still an excellent option. Its edge cleaning also isn’t quite as effective as the X60 Ultra’s, but you’re otherwise getting many of the same features for a lot less.

Industry observers note that the Dreame L40 Ultra Gen 2 delivers powerful 25,000Pa suction, AI-powered obstacle avoidance, and a self-cleaning dock that empties the dustbin, washes the mop, and refills its water tanks. Unlike the X50 Ultra, however, it lacks the ability to climb high thresholds or stairs.
