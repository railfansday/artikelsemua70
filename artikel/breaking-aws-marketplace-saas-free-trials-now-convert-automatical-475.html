Breaking: AWS Marketplace SaaS free trials now convert automatically to paid subscriptions

🌐 Source: Amazon • 📅 10 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (140 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Recent findings suggest that today, AWS Marketplace announces that free trials of usage-based software as a service (SaaS) products can now convert automatically to paid subscriptions. When a free trial ends, the buyer's subscription continues at the seller's usage-based pricing, so buyers pay only for what they use after the trial. This helps sellers turn more trial users into paying customers. Buyers get a simple way to evaluate software before they buy, with no fresh subscription and no interruption in access.

In a recent development, sellers can create a free trial in AWS Partner Central, or patch an existing free trial offer to convert automatically. This capability is available for public offers in all AWS Regions where AWS Marketplace is available.

To learn more, see the AWS Marketplace Seller Guide and the AWS Marketplace Buyer Guide.
