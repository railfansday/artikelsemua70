PersonaFactory: Stylistic User Prompt Augmentation with Synthetic Persona Bank

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 27 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (227 words, 📖 Standard)

arXiv:2611.34785v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Large language models increasingly replace real people in the AI development pipeline, as they are used to generate training dialogues at scale and to drive alignment and evaluation. However, out-of-the-box models produce monolingual and stylistically flat texts as synthetic user prompts, failing to replicate the real user-AI communication mechanics. We present PersonaFactory, an end-to-end pipeline that generates detailed user personas and uses them to augment existing user prompts from their point of view, producing a bank of over 740000 structured synthetic personas detailing their background and communication behaviors, and a corpus of roughly 1.24 million persona-styled synthetic conversations spanning 85 languages. We evaluate the effect of personal features on style adoption and intent preservation, using an LLM-judge and heuristic metrics to assess augmentation fidelity together with the downstream impact on assistant persona control through training data. Results show that our structured, attribute-based style representation enables per-axis control and better intent preservation at comparable style fidelity than free-text persona descriptions used in existing methods. A human expert evaluation found the augmented conversations plausible, with favorable ratings for intent preservation, style naturalness, and multilingual authenticity. Finally, PersonaFactory conversations achieve comparable performance to original training data on capability benchmarks, are preferred on real user prompts at equal reply length, and adapt their reply style more closely to users without explicit steering.
