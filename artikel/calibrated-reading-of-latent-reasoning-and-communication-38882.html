Calibrated Reading of Latent Reasoning and Communication

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 26 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (143 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

arXiv:2611.32143v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Latent reasoning and key-value communication store intermediate information in continuous representations, limiting direct inspection of model behavior. Existing activation readers trade the low cost of task-specific probes against the flexibility of language-model decoders. We propose the Calibrated Latent Reader, CLR, a compact transformer that predicts reasoning outcomes and message content through task-conditioned interactions between states and candidate answers. Source adapters support transfer across models, while conformal calibration provides coverage and false-alarm guarantees under exchangeability. On two CODI models, CLR improves accuracy over MLP and attention probes by 4-18 percentage points for final-answer prediction and reference-value membership. On CODI-GPT-2, it achieves accuracy comparable to a 1.7B LatentQA-based verbalizer using 437-648 times fewer inference FLOPs. On synthetic Qwen3-4B communications, comparing decoded facts with public commitments detects 93.1% of message substitutions at a nominal 5% false-positive rate.
