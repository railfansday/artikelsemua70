MGT brings ChatGPT quoting to independent agents

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

MGT, an AI-native neo-insurer focused on commercial P&C cover, has made its AI quoting assistant Aimee available through the ChatGPT app directories. This makes it the first insurance carrier to let independent agents generate quotes directly inside the AI tool.

According to recent reports, independent agents appointed with MGT can now install Aimee and price cover for businesses by describing them in everyday language, without switching to a separate system. The tool right now supports MGT’s Business Owner’s Policy.

In a recent development, the process has four stages. First, an agent locates MGT Quoting in the Plugins section of ChatGPT and logs in with their existing MGT credentials. Next, they describe the business they want to insure, either by typing a description or by uploading documents they already hold, such as an ACORD application.

Aimee then works out the business classification, checks it against MGT’s appetite, asks only for missing details, and produces a price, while explaining any assumptions it made. Finally, each quote opens in the MGT portal, where the agent can review it and bind the policy.

The launch builds on MGT’s earlier statement that it would be the first carrier to offer agentic quoting through ChatGPT. Since 2025, Aimee has handled appetite queries from agents on MGT’s website. It now provides quotes on a platform that has processed more than $83bn of quoted total insured value for close to 15,000 appointed agents.

Sources indicate that insurance for small businesses has long depended on paperwork, lengthy email exchanges and manually re-entered data, and agents rarely have time to spare. MGT’s approach lets agents describe a business conversationally, learn early whether it fits the carrier’s appetite, and receive a quote they can inspect. The company presents this as a way to ease a long-standing pain point between carriers and agents. It adds that carriers who operate inside the AI assistants agents increasingly use will be simpler to work with.

MGT co-founder and co-CEO Graham Topol said, “We are thrilled to open up our MCP so brokers can quote where they already work. They don’t need another form or another login. They need time back for their clients.

It has been revealed that “Agents shouldn’t have to come to us. We went to them. Every carrier talks about being easy to do business with; we’re the first one you can quote by typing a sentence into ChatGPT. Because we built MGT AI-native from day one, this took us weeks, not years.”.

MGT head of insurance product & underwriting Niall Fitzgerald said, “Speed only matters if agents can trust the quote.

Sources indicate that “Aimee shows her work. She tells the agent what she assumed and why, and when she doesn’t know something, she asks instead of guessing. Every quote is grounded in the same underwriting appetite our team applies every day.”.
