Android Auto and Apple CarPlay bring apps to your car display so you can focus better on the road while driving, allowing you to control features such as navigation, maps, calls, text messages, and music. And right now, several useful devices that help you connect to Android Auto or CarPlay are on sale for Black Friday.

Recent findings suggest that there are two ways to connect with either Android Auto or Apple CarPlay in your vehicle. If you have a newer compatible car, you can automatically access Android Auto or Apple CarPlay. If not, many people need to use wired cables or adapters to achieve the same benefit.

Also: Black Friday Live Blog 2026.

Wireless Android Auto adapters are often an affordable and practical fix for those who aren’t looking to buy a newer car or pay out cash to upgrade their vehicle’s existing system to make it compatible. Wired Android Auto or CarPlay connections are popular for better audio streaming quality, reduced overheating, and improved latency, thanks to the hardwired connection between the device and the car.

Industry observers note that regardless of which method you prefer, there are plenty of Android Auto and Apple CarPlay devices on sale for Black Friday. Here are the deals to shop now.

The AAWireless Two Plus enables wireless Android Auto and CarPlay connectivity, with a multifunctional button designed for quick pairing and switching between Android and iPhone devices. Senior Contributing Editor Kerry Wan notes the gadget delivers the best wireless Android Auto experience yet.

Also: My favorite Android Auto wireless adapter just got its biggest upgrade ever – and iOS people will love it.

Recent findings suggest that the AirFly Drive works with both CarPlay and Android Auto, and connects to two phones at once. Plus, it’s compact and affordable, costing under $40.

Also: I was anti-wireless CarPlay until I tried TwelveSouth’s newly introduced adapter.

The AAWireless Two typically costs $65 and gives you wireless Android Auto support when plugged in. A latest built-in button lets you promptly swap between paired devices, and senior contributing editor Kerry Wan says this is the best option if you don’t need both Apple and Android support.

Also: Finally, an Android Auto adapter that’s reliable, highly functional, and won’t break the bank.

Industry observers note that the Statik MagStack Pro is a tough, high-quality, well-made cable. / Adrian Kingsley-Hughes/ZDNET.

Sources indicate that the Statik MagStack cable has a magnetic design that prevents tangles and is a personal favorite of mine and ZDNET gadget reviewer Adrian Kingsley-Hughes. This cable supports both charging and data transfer, making it compatible with Android Auto and CarPlay. It features 480 Mbps data transfer, 24 pins, and a max voltage of 5. This cable also comes in other USB configurations for your vehicle or another space, like your home office.

Also: This is the only charging cable you’ll ever need to avoid a tangling mess (and it’s cheap).

This braided USB cable from Anker is built to avoid tangles or tearing. It comes in three colors and two length options so that you can find a fit for your vehicle. Most importantly, it supports both Android Auto and Apple CarPlay, costs under $15, and has a 4.6/5 rating from 5,000 customers. I’m a huge fan of many Anker accessories, including their braided cables for their durability and ease of keeping tidy, so I’d vouch for this one given the brand name and reviews alone.
