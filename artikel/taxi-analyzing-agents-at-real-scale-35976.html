Taxi: Analyzing Agents at (Real) Scale

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 26 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (165 words, 📖 Standard)

According to recent reports, arXiv:2611.31457v1 Announce Type: fresh Abstract: In this technical report, we present Taxi -- a system for debugging, clustering, and monitoring agents at scale. Taxi scales to millions of steps efficiently. This is made possible by a combination of scalable analysis methods. Taxi relies primarily on clustering to generate a taxonomy over steps for a structured overview of the data, and provides full SQL access for engineers and automated agents to ask complex questions. Clustering and querying are best used together in a hybrid pipeline guided by a coding agent. Taxi also includes an experimental integration of Jev-style decision models: models that map state and bounded questions directly to typed, probabilistic decisions. They add low-cost semantic routing and evaluation where embeddings or literal queries alone are insufficient. In addition, Taxi provides an extensible ecosystem, including built-in evaluators and daily reports. To accommodate growth, Taxi is designed to be modular, hackable, and easy to plug-and-play for individuals and teams.
