Latest Update: Potential emergence in LLM agents: a statistical physics perspective

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 23 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (143 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

arXiv:2611.27618v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: There has been significant recent interest in understanding the behaviour of LLM agents from the perspective of human preferences or corpus statistics. In this paper we propose agent states as intermediate variables through which post-training can be measured and predicted. From a statistical physics perspective, we study the emergence of a global potential which governs the transition of state in LLM agent reasoning. We introduce a partition function to model the ideal KL-regularised transition distribution and track potential structure, mean reward and state entropy. We demonstrate our approach in two Qwen3.5-9B tasks: safety-test selection and physics-reasoning auditing. We observe that the potential structure strengthens during post-training as predicted from our framework, while joint changes in reward and state entropy provide consistency evidence. States thus provide a quantitative description of how post-training organises agent behaviour.
