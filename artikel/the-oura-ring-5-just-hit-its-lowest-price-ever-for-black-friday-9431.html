In a recent development, smart rings have taken the wearable market by storm over the past few years, but one brand reigns supreme: Oura. The Oura Ring 5 debuted in May, and it’s the newest, thinnest model yet. It’s also a ZDNET Editors’ Choice Award and Gift of the Year Award winner, and our pick for the best smart ring you can buy.

Industry observers note that since the Oura Ring is a big-ticket item, we rarely see it go on sale, but during Amazon’s Black Friday sale, the Oura Ring 5 is discounted to the lowest price we’ve seen since its release. Right now, you can snag the Oura Ring 5 on Amazon for $100 off. This deal reduces the gold, brushed silver, deep rose, and stealth colors to $399, while the silver and black variations are discounted to $299. We don’t expect this price to drop any lower than this, so we’d recommend buying now if you’re in the market.

ZDNET’s wearable reviewer, Nina Raemont, wore the Oura Ring 5 for a month after its release and noticed right away its smaller form factor. Compared to the Oura Ring 4, the Oura Ring 5 is 40% smaller. Raemont said the smaller size allowed her to forget she was even wearing the ring at all, which, as most smart ring wearers know, is a major deal.

Also: Amazon’s Black Friday sale is live.

Aside from its smaller size, the Oura Ring 5 also has more battery life than its predecessor.

According to recent reports, “I tested the battery life of the Oura Ring 5 by wearing it and recording its percentages each day to see just how quickly the smart ring’s battery depletes. With the 5, I got around one day more of battery life during my first week,” Raemont wrote in her review.

The Oura Ring 5 also received a ZDNET Editors’ Choice Award, with Raemont noting, “The Oura Ring 5 is the first smart ring I’ve tested that feels like jewelry first and technology second.”.

All this to say, the Oura Ring 5 is one of the standout devices this year, and its 20% discount makes it even more appealing to buy during this Black Friday shopping period.
