We put 7 rugged tablets in our lab sauna – here’s how they performed at 106°F

ZDNET’s lab in Louisville, KY, has come up with some pretty creative ways to test devices, such as using a silicon ear to test earbuds or recreating a living room with different floor types to test robot vacuums.

Also: We tested 7 rugged tablets – these 2 outlasted the cold and heat best.

Industry observers note that our latest test examined seven of the best rugged tablets from Samsung, Oukitel, Dell, Getac, and DT Research and how well they handled heat. Gianmarco Chumbe, our product testing manager, built a dry sauna for the tablets that mimics a very hot day (up to 106°F) to see which tablet lasted the longest under the extreme conditions.

It turns out Chumbe has experience building this kind of thing. “I’m a reptile keeper, and I don’t like paying for luxury enclosures, so I build them myself,” he said. “When I set out to replicate a hot environment holding around 105°F, to simulate what users might experience on a very hot worksite, I realized it was a very similar setup to what I’d already been building at home.”.

Chumbe built a 40 × 20 × 20-inch enclosure with walls made of half-inch PVC board and a quarter-inch acrylic front panel to view the tablets during testing. A 400 Watt fan-forced PTC heater sat at one end of the chamber, while a 20 × 12-in aluminum baffle, positioned 10 inches in front of the heater, blocked its direct airflow, so no tablet was directly heated by it. Most importantly, a circulation fan ran continuously to keep the air evenly mixed.

“The only difference is that reptiles need a temperature gradient, from a hot side to a slightly cooler one, whereas [for tablets] we needed a constant temperature spread evenly across the whole rig. We solved that by building in proper air mixing,” he mentioned. Also, the tablets’ sauna didn’t include any form of moisture.

Sources indicate that the thermostat was consistently set between 104 and 106°F, with the heater cycling every 2 minutes (30 seconds on and 90 seconds off). A second, independent thermostat cut power to the heater if the air reached 115°F, protecting the units if the main thermostat failed. Finally, a single 1 ⅛-in. port, sealed around the cables, carried the power and sensor cords.

Testing began after placing the seven tablets on a raised rack inside the sauna. Each tablet started at 100% battery, with Airplane Mode on, and the screens were set to 500 nits of brightness (or the closest equivalent). Chumbe ran a looped playback of the same 1080p H.264 video file (no audio track) in VLC, with the system audio muted on each of the tablets.

To document battery degradation, a lightweight Python script ran on each tablet throughout the test and logged the battery level every minute until the tablets shut down or entered a dormant state at 1% battery life.

Lastly, even though these tablets could certainly operate in hotter temperatures, we capped out the sauna at 106°F since the rig was designed to operate unattended. Higher temperatures increase the risk of batteries overheating, and thus fire hazard.

It has been revealed that out of the seven tablets we tested in the sauna, the Oukitel RT10 lasted the longest at 32.07 hours. However, that wasn’t the tablet Chumbe found most impressive in the tablet sauna.

Recent findings suggest that “What surprised me most was how resilient the Samsung Galaxy Active 5 Pro was. Even after sitting at 1% battery for hours, it still let me use all of its features, including saving, editing, and uploading test data,” Chumbe said. “It’s a small unit compared to the others, but it holds its ground remarkably well.”.

The Samsung Galaxy Active 5 Pro lasted 15.20 hours in the tablet sauna, outperforming other more expensive rugged models like the Getac F120 and the  DT Research Rugged Tablet DT312RP. The Active 5 Pro’s 10,100mAh battery came in second place (lasting 22.88 hours) in our baseline room-temperature testing, and first place in our cold test, lasting 12.47 hours at -6°F.

While you may not be able to build your own tablet sauna at home, our lab’s test results show how rugged tablets can withstand extreme conditions for those who need to use one while camping/hiking, out in the field, or on a construction site on a hot summer day.

The Oukitel RT10 and the Samsung Galaxy Tab Active 5 Pro are both solid picks for rugged tablets that can withstand the heat (or a tablet sauna).
