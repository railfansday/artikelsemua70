ADB Ventures backs Neem to scale FinTech rails in Pakistan

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Neem, a provider of unified financial infrastructure for businesses in Pakistan, has secured backing from ADB Ventures as part of its Pre-Series A round to expand its presence across the country.

Sources indicate that the size of the investment from ADB Ventures, the venture capital arm of the Asian Development Bank (ADB), was not disclosed. Neem will use the capital to grow its financial infrastructure platform and lending portfolio, improve its product capabilities and reach more of the Pakistani market.

Several other investors joined the Pre-Series A round. These include DNI Group, HI2 Global, AKD Securities Limited, Epic Angels and the Pakistan Startup Fund. Strategic angel investors from PayNet, Aspire, Stripe and other international FinTech and payments companies also took part.

In a recent development, neem’s lending offering includes earned wage access. This lets employees draw on wages they have already earned, with a particular focus on blue-collar workers in industries that employ large workforces. The service aims to give workers a responsible option and reduce their dependence on informal borrowing. The company also intends to offer financing to businesses. Merchants using its platform would be able to obtain working capital based on their transaction and settlement records, rather than only on conventional financial statements.

In a recent development, neem’s platform brings together the tools businesses need to handle the flow of money. Clients can collect payments, send out funds, oversee balances, automate settlements and reconcile transactions in one place. The company works with more than 70 enterprise customers in sectors including logistics, utilities, insurance, manufacturing and retail.

The new funding marks the start of Neem’s next growth phase. The firm intends to extend its digital financial rails to help businesses and the broader Pakistani economy continue to digitise.

Sources indicate that ADB country director for Pakistan Emma Fan said, “Pakistan is laying a strong foundation for digital payments, and the next step is ensuring that businesses of all sizes can fully benefit from this progress. Neem is helping enterprises move away from cash and fragmented systems toward payment solutions that are faster, more transparent, and easier to manage.”.

Recent findings suggest that neem co-founder Vladimira Briestenska said, “Pakistan’s businesses do not lack the ambition to go digital. They lack financial infrastructure that works as one connected system. ADB Ventures’ investment is a strong vote of confidence in our approach and in the opportunity to build the financial infrastructure Pakistan needs. With this support, we aim to bring more businesses, workers and communities into the formal digital economy.”.
