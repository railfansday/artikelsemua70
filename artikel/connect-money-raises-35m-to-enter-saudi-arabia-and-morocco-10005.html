📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Connect Money, an Egypt-based FinTech offering embedded finance and white-label card services, has secured $3.5m in a pre-Series A round as it gears up to take its platform into Saudi Arabia and Morocco.

The round was led by returning backers Mohamed Farouk, Algebra Ventures and DisrupTech Ventures, according to a report from Wamda. The deal blends new funding with the sale of secondary shares, giving a number of the startup’s early backers a partial exit. Connect Money has not revealed what portion of the $3.5m is new capital.

The company will use the money to move into its two latest target markets, with obtaining regulatory sign-off and building the necessary infrastructure being the main priorities. In Saudi Arabia, the firm is pursuing a licence from the Saudi Central Bank (SAMA). In Morocco, it is putting in place local operations and technology systems, with a commercial launch pencilled in for the first half of 2027.

In a recent development, the move will take Connect Money’s embedded finance offering outside its home market for the first time. It is aimed at businesses that want to provide payment products to their customers but would rather not build banking infrastructure themselves.

Connect Money notes it has issued more than 300,000 cards so far. The platform aims to help businesses reach customers who rely on cash by giving them access to digital payments, instant lending tools and a network of more than 20,000 marketplace partners.

The firm takes care of the complicated parts of card issuance, so that companies can launch their own banking proposition through white-label cards paired with a digital banking app. Connect Money claims this lets clients skip long regulatory processes, large upfront spending and continuing operational costs. It also says businesses can open up fresh banking-related revenue streams without any initial investment.
