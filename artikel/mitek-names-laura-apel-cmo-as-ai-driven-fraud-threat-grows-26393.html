📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Mitek Systems has hired Laura Apel as its chief marketing officer as the tech giant looks to sharpen its commercial edge in a market increasingly shaped by AI-enabled fraud.

In a recent development, apel took up the post on 16 November 2026 and reports to CEO Edward H. West. She will run Mitek’s worldwide marketing function, with responsibility for brand, demand generation, product marketing and communications.

In a recent development, her mandate centres on raising the tech giant’s profile, strengthening relationships with customers and partners, and bringing tighter coordination to its go-to-market activity. She is also expected to reinforce Mitek’s standing as an authoritative voice in identity verification and fraud prevention, a field that continues to change quickly.

Apel joins with a long track record in B2B technology marketing across communications, network API platforms, digital identity and fraud prevention. Her most recent role was heading global marketing for the API business unit of Vonage, which is owned by Ericsson. There, she worked on positioning a portfolio of multiple products, generating demand and linking marketing activity to commercial targets.

Before Vonage, she directed global marketing at Mitto, a Communications Platform as a Service (CPaaS) business, while it was scaling rapidly. Prior to that, she established and managed marketing teams at Telesign, which offers a digital identity and communications platform.

In a recent development, across these roles, Apel has steered marketing through phases of strong growth, including helping a business increase annual recurring revenue from around $40m to $300m. Her approach puts revenue at the centre, tying brand strategy to demand generation, explaining complex technology in terms of customer benefit, lifting return on marketing spend and building closer working ties between marketing and sales teams.

Mitek specialises in helping organisations verify digital identities and prevent fraud, positioning its technology around protecting trust in online interactions.

According to recent reports, mitek CEO Edward H. West stated, “Laura brings a powerful combination of strategic vision, commercial expertise and a proven ability to translate marketing into measurable business results. Her experience building high-performing global marketing organizations and accelerating commercial growth will be invaluable as we expand our impact and advance our mission of protecting trust across digital interactions.”.

Apel added, “Having spent much of my career in communications and digital identity, I’ve seen firsthand how central trust has become to every digital interaction. What excites me about Mitek is the strength of its technology, the trust it has built with customers and the opportunity to make an even greater impact as AI-driven fraud makes digital identity and fraud prevention more critical than ever.

“I look forward to building on that foundation, scaling our go-to-market engine, deepening our connections with customers and partners, and helping more organizations navigate the evolving challenges of digital trust.”.
