📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

If you’re a T-Mobile customer, you might want to consider signing up for the T-Mobile Visa card from Capital One to potentially save massive on your bill. Cardholders earn 2% back in T-Mobile Rewards on all purchases, and 5% back on any phone, device, or accessory at T-Mobile. You’ll also get $5 off per line per month when you use your card for T-Mobile AutoPay on eligible plans, for up to eight lines.

According to recent reports, your rewards never expire, so you can use them any time. Redeem them toward your T-Mobile bill, or a new device or accessory.

Also: The best phones to buy in 2026.

It has been revealed that the T-Mobile Visa card has no annual fee. You’ll get perks on travel, dining, and more, saving up to 50% on hotels and car rentals when you use your card to book with T-Mobile Travel, and 6% back via statement credits at participating restaurants when you link your card with T-Mobile Dining Rewards. When you redeem your rewards, you need to use your card to pay for any portion of the transaction not covered by the rewards.

Connect your card to your T-Life app to track your rewards, make payments, or set up AutoPay. You can also choose your monthly payment due date.

Interested? To be eligible to apply for the T-Mobile Visa, you need to be an active T-Mobile consumer postpaid wireless customer with at least one active line on an eligible phone proposal, residing in the US, Puerto Rico, or a US military location.

See all of the terms and conditions and learn how to apply for your T-Mobile Visa card at T-Mobile's website now.

Disclosures: T-Mobile Visa cardholders earn 2% back in T-Mobile Rewards on all other net purchases (purchases minus any credits or returns) using their T-Mobile Visa anywhere else Visa is accepted.

It has been revealed that T-Mobile Visa cardholders earn 5% back in T-Mobile Rewards on net Qualifying Purchases (purchases minus any credits or returns) made online at T-Mobile.com and in the T-Life app, and at T-Mobile stores and Authorized Retailers (https://www.t-mobile.com/stores/locator). Qualifying Purchases include phones, devices, and accessories. Cash advances and balance transfers are not considered purchases and will not earn T-Mobile Rewards. Qualifying Purchases exclude payments for device equipment installment strategy (EIP), wireless service, and prepaid wireless service, T-Mobile for Business, and other services. You may not accrue enhanced earning on purchases made using 3rd party digital wallets.

When redeeming T-Mobile rewards, your T-Mobile Visa must be used to pay any portion of the transaction not covered by T-Mobile Rewards. T-Mobile Rewards may not be redeemed for backorder purchases. Redemptions may not be available when using 3rd party digital wallets. See your Rewards Terms and Conditions for more details.

According to recent reports, the AutoPay discount requires enrollment in Wireless AutoPay using your T-Mobile Visa on an eligible post-paid wireless plan. Discount will appear on wireless statement starting the next billing cycle after enrollment. Customers who are at the moment receiving the Autopay discount via other qualifying methods will not receive additional discounts for re-enrolling using the card. For more information on Autopay discount eligibility, please visit t-mobile.com/cards/visa-credit-rewards/faqs.

Rewards do not expire for the life of the T-Mobile Visa account.
