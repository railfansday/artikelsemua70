Sources indicate that become an OpenAI Campus Lead. Help students gain confidence using ChatGPT and Codex while building projects together.

Recent findings suggest that students have been among the first to find fresh ways AI can support learning, creativity, and problem-solving. As OpenAI builds more capable tools, we believe students will continue leading the way and bring others along with them.

The OpenAI Student Collective gives undergraduate Campus Leads the tools, training, funding, and support to help that energy grow on campus.

We’re looking for students excited to bring peers from every field together, help them gain confidence with OpenAI tools, and showcase what they create with a global community.

Campus Leads work in pairs to create a place where students learn the latest AI tools, start projects, and share what they create along the way.

Welcoming events where students learn new AI skills, explore what they can make, and choose a project to keep building during Studio Hours.

Weekly coworking sessions where students spend focused time on their projects, connect with peers, and get help when they need it.

Campus-wide celebrations of what students are creating with AI.

A shared space for students to stay connected, ask questions, and discover upcoming opportunities.

A ChatGPT subscription and Codex credits for the duration of the program.

Funding, credits, and merch to help you organize campus events.

Training, resources, and guidance on the latest OpenAI tools and technology.

Access to the OpenAI team and a global network of Campus Leads.

A cash stipend each semester in recognition of your time and leadership.

Opportunities for a HQ visit in June 2027 and other surprises along the way.

Applications are open through August 31, 2026 for students in Japan, Korea, the United Kingdom, Germany, and France. Applications for the Fall 2026 cohort have closed in the United States, Canada, and India.

If applications are closed in your country—or if the program isn't yet available where you study—you can still submit an application to be considered for future cohorts as the program expands.

Students from every discipline are encouraged to apply. We're looking for undergraduate students who are curious, community-minded, and excited to introduce others to AI.

You love ChatGPT, Codex, and OpenAI tools.

You’re enrolled in an undergraduate college or university program as of August 2026 and expect to graduate after December 2027.

You’re committed to 4–6 hours per week of participation from August 2026–June 2027.

You study in the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, France, Germany, India, Japan, or South Korea and have work authorization in your country of study. Studying elsewhere? You can register your interest by submitting an application, and we’ll let you know when the Collective becomes available in your country.
