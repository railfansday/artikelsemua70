Spend limits in the new AWS experience now support SMS alerts

🌐 Source: Amazon • 📅 20 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (223 words, 📖 Standard)

Industry observers note that builders using the new AWS experience on the Paid plan can now receive spend limit alerts by text message (SMS), alongside existing email notifications. A spend limit sets the maximum monthly cost for a project. Notifications are sent as a project approaches its limit, and when a project reaches its limit, AWS automatically pauses it. SMS gives builders a second way to stay informed about their project costs.

Sources indicate that email alerts can go unnoticed when builders are away from their inbox, leaving little time to respond before a project is paused. SMS alerts close this gap with a text message when a project's actual costs reach 90% of its spend limit, so builders have time to act, such as raising the limit or stopping resources they don't need.

To turn on SMS alerts, verify your mobile phone number in AWS Settings and turn on text alerts for spend limits. Text alerts are only sent to verified numbers, and you can opt out at any time. SMS alerts for spend limits are available at no additional charge for US phone numbers, including Puerto Rico, Guam, the US Virgin Islands, and American Samoa.

To learn more, see Create a spend limit in AWS Settings, the AWS Account Management Reference Guide, or Get started with AWS.
