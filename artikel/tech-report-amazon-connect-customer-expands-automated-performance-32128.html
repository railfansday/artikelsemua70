Tech Report: Amazon Connect Customer expands automated performance evaluations to 45 newly introduced languages

🌐 Source: Amazon • 📅 23 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (125 words, ⚡ Quick Read)
💡 Key Takeaways
  • Recent findings suggest that amazon Connect Customer now uses generative AI to automate evaluations of human and AI agent performance in 45 additional languages, such as Arabic, Dutch, Indonesian, and Polish.
  • According to recent reports, this feature is supported in all AWS commercial regions where Amazon Connect Customer is offered.

Recent findings suggest that amazon Connect Customer now uses generative AI to automate evaluations of human and AI agent performance in 45 additional languages, such as Arabic, Dutch, Indonesian, and Polish. Managers define custom evaluation criteria in natural language and receive AI-generated evaluations with justifications in their preferred language. Performance evaluations also support cross-language evaluation and can complete assessments in English, even when the conversation is in another language. This enables multilingual contact centers to use a standardized evaluation framework across languages.

According to recent reports, this feature is supported in all AWS commercial regions where Amazon Connect Customer is offered. For information about Amazon Connect pricing, please visit our pricing page. To learn more, visit our documentation and our webpage.
