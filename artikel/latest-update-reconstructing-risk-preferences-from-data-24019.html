Latest Update: Reconstructing risk preferences from data

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 23 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (139 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

arXiv:2611.27692v1 Announce Type: upcoming Abstract: We consider the problem of reconstructing risk preferences from data. Specifically, for a finite dataset consisting of contingent claims and their evaluations, we aim at reconstructing a risk measure that is finite-valued, cash-additive, convex, and Lipschitz continuous. Our first result establishes necessary and sufficient conditions for a dataset to be compatible with a risk measure, that is the evaluations are stemming from a risk measure applied to the contingent claims. Our second result provides a reconstruction formula for compatible datasets, which we term \emph{universal risk measure}. We further extend the reconstruction to monotone risk measures and establish sharp Lipschitz constants for both general and monotone reconstructions. We further show that we explicitly reconstruct linear preferences and evaluations by the Expected Shortfall. We conclude with a numerical application to European options.
