DexOPD: Dual-Extrapolation On-Policy Distillation for Diffusion Models

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 26 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (206 words, 📖 Standard)

arXiv:2611.31969v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Flow-based diffusion models have become a mainstream paradigm for text-to-image generation, where task-specific post-training produces specialist models with complementary strengths. Multi-teacher on-policy distillation (OPD) offers an efficient way to consolidate these specialists into a single unified student. Recent methods strengthen supervision through teacher extrapolation, constructing stronger proxy teachers that allow distilled students to surpass the original specialists. However, their extrapolation directions remain defined solely by frozen teacher and reference models, leaving the student's own training progress unexploited. To address this limitation, we introduce DexOPD, a dual-extrapolation on-policy distillation method for diffusion models. DexOPD retains extrapolated teacher supervision while leveraging a lagging historical student as the reference for student self-extrapolation. Sampling across multiple self-extrapolation gains from shared initial noise broadens exploration along the student's evolving trajectory, and task rewards convert pairwise differences in candidate quality into a normalized auxiliary regression direction. Empirically, we evaluate DexOPD on GenEval, OCR, and DrawBench. The resulting unified SD3.5-Medium student achieves a GenEval reward of 0.9736, an OCR score of 0.9515, and a DrawBench Aesthetic score of 6.4875, surpassing the corresponding specialist teachers as well as all evaluated OPD and multi-task reinforcement learning baselines across these three benchmarks while preserving standard single-model inference.
