In a recent development, during Black Friday, the Anker 100W Universal Charger is on sale for $59.99 at Amazon, Best Buy, and Walmart, a $20 discount from its list price. That’s a few dollars lower than the previous best, with both the dark gray and silver versions available from all three retailers. With four USB ports and support for over 200 countries, you can keep all of your devices charged wherever you end up.

Anker’s durable travel adapter delivers up to 100W of power and works in over 200 countries. It doesn’t support AC plugs, but it does offer a trio of USB-C ports and a nifty LCD display to keep tabs on the charge percentage.

According to recent reports, anker’s travel charger supports outlets in over 200 countries, including Type A for the United States, Canada, Japan, and China; Type C for the European Union; and Type G for the UK and Singapore. It doesn’t support Type I, the outlet used in Australia, Fresh Zealand, and Argentina. Also, there’s no AC adapter. It’s a USB-only affair. To that end, you can charge up to four devices at once, with three USB-C ports and one USB-A port, with two of the USB-C ports capable of delivering up to 100W when used individually. There’s even a tiny display on the unit that lets you check how much power is going to your devices at a glance.
