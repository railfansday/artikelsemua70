Latest Update: LProp: Propagating Latent Uncertainty through Evidence-Conditioned Belief Revision in LLM Agents

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 20 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (149 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

arXiv:2611.23564v1 Announce Type: latest Abstract: When LLM agents pass intermediate judgments downstream without uncertainty, later decisions may treat tentative assumptions as facts. Effective uncertainty propagation must preserve information for correction and revision inherited beliefs as new evidence arrives. Yet scalar scores omit information needed for revision, while detailed descriptions and reflection incur additional costs. We introduce Latent Uncertainty, a latent-space estimator that preserves uncertainty-relevant information without lengthy descriptions. Building on this, we propose LProp (Latent Uncertainty Propagation), which revises inherited answer distributions using latent representations and new evidence without additional trajectory sampling. We evaluate both methods across multiple-choice, yes/no, open-ended question answering, and mathematical reasoning tasks. Our approach outperforms baselines on most metrics, while LProp achieves the lowest mean inference-token usage in all eight propagation settings. Matched feature ablations further demonstrate that inherited latent content improves error recovery beyond answer probabilities or model capacity alone.
