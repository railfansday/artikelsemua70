LMAX Group debuts perpetual futures on Helio platform

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

LMAX Group, a global cross-asset marketplace for FX and digital assets, has rolled out its institutional perpetual futures on Helio, the trading platform run by Fintake, giving brokers a single-account route to perpetuals alongside FX and CFDs.

The integration aims to help brokers respond to growing appetite for perpetuals among institutional clients. Through one account, brokers can tap into LMAX’s deep liquidity and ultra-low latency execution while also trading FX and CFDs.

Recent findings suggest that perpetual futures now lead trading activity across digital asset markets. Citing CoinGecko data, LMAX said that annual volumes on centralised exchanges climbed 47.4% compared with the previous year, hitting an all-time high of $86.2tn in 2025. Because perpetuals offer round-the-clock market access, efficient use of capital and no need to roll contracts, professional traders can hold directional positions without the operational drag associated with conventional dated futures.

The setup has been designed for speed, stripping out the usual onboarding and infrastructure hurdles. Brokers that already have LMAX credentials can be operational within five days, with orders routed natively to LMAX’s order books. There is no requirement for an additional bridge or extensive engineering work.

According to recent reports, brokers and their traders can reach LMAX liquidity via Helio’s AI-driven web interface, its mobile app or through API, with funding information made visible in real time. As LMAX broadens its cross-asset offering, brokers using Helio are also set to benefit from forthcoming access to perpetual futures on real-world assets (RWA).

According to recent reports, LMAX Group managing director, digital assets Jenna Wright remarked, “Perpetual futures account for the vast majority of institutional digital asset volume, and brokers need a reliable, institutional-grade venue to capture that flow. Combining LMAX’s deep liquidity with Helio’s turnkey infrastructure cuts time-to-market from months to days and lays the groundwork for our upcoming expansion into RWA perpetuals.”.

Industry observers note that fintake co-founder and CEO Ahmad Mentioned said, “Pairing LMAX’s institutional execution with Helio’s native perpetuals architecture gives brokers an immediate competitive edge across web, mobile, and API. Professional traders get the 24/7 access, capital efficiency, and funding transparency they expect, all through a single platform.”.
