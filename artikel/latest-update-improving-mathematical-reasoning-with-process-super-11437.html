Latest Update: Improving mathematical reasoning with process supervision

🌐 Source: Openai • 📅 31 May 2023 • ⏱️ 1 min read (71 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Sources indicate that we’ve trained a model to achieve a newly introduced state-of-the-art in mathematical problem solving by rewarding each correct step of reasoning (“process supervision”) instead of simply rewarding the correct final answer (“outcome supervision”). In addition to boosting performance relative to outcome supervision, process supervision also has an important alignment benefit: it directly trains the model to produce a chain-of-thought that is endorsed by humans.
