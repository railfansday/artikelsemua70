Industry News: SimAI: Mechanistic User Simulation through Psychological Network Models

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 18 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (178 words, 📖 Standard)

Industry observers note that arXiv:2611.21808v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Realistic user simulation enables the study of human interactions when direct experimentation is costly or potentially harmful. However, existing simulators largely overlook the psychological mechanisms that drive user behavior. We introduce SimAI, a framework for mechanistic user simulation that models how psychological factors interact to shape user responses. To overcome the challenge of supervising open-ended behavior, we propose a graph reconstruction objective that provides graph-level rewards based on how well simulated conversations reflect the underlying psychological relationships. To support this approach, we construct PSYCHE, a dataset of 18,576 profile-network pairs derived from 516 psychotherapy cases. Automatic evaluations show that SimAI achieves greater simulation and behavioral diversity than existing patient simulators while maintaining strong profile alignment. In an interactive study with 30 mental-health professionals and trainees, SimAI receives the highest average ratings for conversational authenticity, realistic help-seeking behavior, and clinical training utility. Beyond simulation quality, SimAI enables realistic clinical training and uncovers safety failures in prolonged AI interactions with vulnerable users. Code is available at https://github.com/behavioral-data/ANGEL-UserSim.
