Tech Report: Whose Side Are You On? A Framework and Benchmark for Assessing LLM Political Stances through Politicians Social Media Posts

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 27 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (180 words, 📖 Standard)

According to recent reports, arXiv:2611.34098v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Large language models (LLMs) increasingly participate in political discussions, making it important to understand which positions they support or oppose. We propose a framework for assessing LLMs' political stances through simulated social media interactions. The framework uses politicians' posts and multiple prompting methods to identify consistently expressed stances. Using this framework, we construct WorldPOLL, comprising 4,600 political posts from X/Twitter collected from approximately 200 politicians representing ruling and opposition blocs across 23 countries. Our quantitative analysis highlights how support and opposition vary across models, countries, and political directions, while our qualitative analysis identifies the political views and policies models support or oppose. Together, these analyses reveal shared positions across models and differences across national contexts. We also examine whether models express consistent stances across prompting methods, investigate the reasons for disagreement, and assess the effects of model size and instruction tuning in stance inconsistency. WorldPOLL offers a practical way to measure LLM political tendencies, identify the views behind them, and assess their consistency in real-world political discussions.
