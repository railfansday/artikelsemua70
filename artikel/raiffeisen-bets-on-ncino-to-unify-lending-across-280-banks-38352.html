Raiffeisen bets on nCino to unify lending across 280 banks

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Sources indicate that raiffeisenbankengruppe Oesterreich, Austria’s largest corporate banking group, is replacing its ageing lending technology with the nCino Platform. According to nCino, the cooperative is betting that one standardised lending journey can serve almost 280 regional banks.

The group has supported corporate borrowers for over 100 years through a cooperative model that blends local relationships with national reach. That scale, however, has created complexity, and nCino reports that Raiffeisen concluded its technology had to change to keep the lending experience modern and efficient.

Speaking at the nCino EMEA Summit in London, Raiffeisen Austria product portfolio management representative Rainer Wöhrenschimmel said, “Our current solution is about 15 years old, and it’s going to be out of our legacy in the next one or two years. It’s not built for any future lending at Raiffeisen, so we need a modern platform.”.

nCino notes that the old systems had become a barrier rather than a base to build on. They made consistent working across the network difficult and slowed the group’s response to shifting customer expectations and regulatory demands. The decision, therefore, was less about swapping out software and more about creating a foundation the entire group could scale on.

Recent findings suggest that consistency sat at the heart of the challenge. Each of the group’s banks serves its own community, yet Raiffeisen wanted every institution to lend in the same modern way and every corporate client to receive the same experience.

Recent findings suggest that wöhrenschimmel noted, “I think most of the lending we’re doing by now is standard for nCino, but building it on our own, it won’t be standard at all,” says Wöhrenschimmel. “We’re 280 banks, and within 280 banks there are a lot of different versions of every lending part.”.

According to nCino, the platform now covers the full corporate lending lifecycle, from first application to final disbursement, across every product line.

Efficiency is the immediate goal. Before the switch, Raiffeisen’s lending staff spent considerable time on administrative tasks that delivered little value to clients. Wöhrenschimmel noted, “Our sales and risk departments had to check everything twice, so they just can’t focus on their real expertise.”.

Recent findings suggest that nCino states the new platform allows those teams to redirect their energy toward judgement and client relationships. For a group built on local trust, that freed-up time can flow back into understanding customers and supporting regional economies.

Looking further ahead, artificial intelligence is expected to take on work that has long been manual. Wöhrenschimmel said, “I see AI helping with all the data entry, with all the risks and checks we have to do by now mostly manually.”.

nCino describes its platform as designed for agentic banking. Data enrichment could pre-fill applications, while AI-assisted risk management could help teams identify problems sooner and decide with greater speed and confidence. Because these tools sit on a shared platform, they can be rolled out across the whole group at once rather than bank by bank.

Sources indicate that raiffeisen also values the relationship beyond the technology. In a tightly regulated market, it needs a partner that tracks change on its behalf.

Wöhrenschimmel noted, “nCino is a partner that drives and builds our platform on both the regulatory and the user side, guiding us through the market and regulatory side as well”.

For nCino, the result pairs a century of cooperative banking heritage with a continuously evolving platform, giving Raiffeisen a corporate lending foundation shaped around how the group operates and the trust its clients place in it.
