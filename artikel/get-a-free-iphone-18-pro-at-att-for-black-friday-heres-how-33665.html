TVs and laptops aren’t the only things you’ll find on sale this Black Friday. Shoppers looking to upgrade to the newly introduced iPhone 18 Pro can potentially pick it up for free with this special trade-in offer at AT&T — saving you a staggering $1,200.

You’ll get the full discount in exchange for any device valued at $230 or more, which includes any iPhone 15 Pro Max or newer model in any condition. You can also save up to $930 with a trade-in valued at $130 or more, and even old and damaged devices valued at $35 or more can still earn you up to $350.

Industry observers note that with this offer, you can get the basic 256GB iPhone 18 Pro for free, or the iPhone 18 Pro Max for less than $3 per month. Just note that the savings will be applied as bill credits over 36 months, so you’ll have to stick with AT&T for three years to get the full discount. If you cancel service before then, you’ll be on the hook for the remaining cost of the phone.

Industry observers note that the iPhone 18 Pro is the latest model in Apple’s lineup and our overall top pick for the best phone of 2026. Plus, it features a stunning 6.9-inch ProMotion display with an impressive 120Hz refresh rate, which makes it great for streaming reveals and video calls.

Apple’s latest phones don’t come cheap, so any chance to pick one up for less is a pretty serious bargain. This deal may require a trade-in, but can potentially save you as much as $1,200, earning it a rating of 4/5 on our system.
