IntellectAI, the InsurTech arm of Intellect Design Arena, has introduced Xponent Portfolio Navigator (XPN), an AI-powered application that lets commercial and specialty insurers and reinsurers steer their entire book of business rather than simply report on it.

Industry observers note that the application gives carriers, MGAs and reinsurers a view of the main metrics behind their profit, loss and risk exposure. It highlights areas where a portfolio is moving away from its intended course and lets users reprice, refer or decline to renew the affected business without leaving the same screen. Action can be taken on anything from one policy to as many as 30,000 at once, and the impact of each quote can be assessed before binding.

According to IntellectAI, although portfolio results are determined across the whole book, many insurers still respond to them case by case. Spotting an issue usually involves generating a business intelligence (BI) report, extracting details from source systems and then switching to another tool to take action. In many cases, losses have already materialised by the time a response is made.

Sources indicate that XPN aims to remove this delay by bringing portfolio analytics, contextual AI and underwriting workflow together in a single application. Each figure can be traced to the policies, quotes and claims behind it, and each decision can be sent to agentic workflows, referral underwriters, managers or the portfolio committee.

Recent findings suggest that the product runs on Purple Fabric, Intellect’s governed AI platform. Insurers can choose for each action whether AI agents carry out the task or a person reviews it, define the boundaries for automation and retain a complete audit trail of every decision. XPN adds to IntellectAI’s expanding platform, which also includes MSOCK, the firm’s system of connected knowledge, allowing carriers to either purchase ready-made products such as Xponent or develop their own using the same base.

In a recent development, intellect Design Arena provides AI-first, enterprise-grade financial technology and has three decades of experience in the sector. Its platforms cover wholesale banking, consumer banking, central banking, wealth, capital markets, treasury, insurance and digital technology for commerce.

In a recent development, its AI-first architecture is built around eMACH.ai, Purple Fabric and iTurmeric, a composable integration and configuration platform. The firm works with more than 500 customers across 62 countries.

Sources indicate that XPN is available through two routes. Insurers can add it to Intellect Xponent, IntellectAI’s underwriting workbench, which places portfolio steering next to risk-level underwriting in one view. Alternatively, it can be bought as a standalone product and linked via APIs to existing policy administration, claims and data warehouse systems.

Recent findings suggest that intellectAI CEO Banesh Prabhu noted, “Commercial and specialty insurers are being asked to grow profitably in a market that keeps shifting under them. Xponent Portfolio Navigator gives their underwriting leaders a live view of the whole book and the ability to act on it right away. That’s how portfolio steering becomes a daily discipline instead of a quarterly report.”.

IntellectAI EVP and insurance business head Sandeep Haridas said, “Insurers don’t lack portfolio data. They lack a fast way to act on it. XPN lets an underwriting leader find drift, size the fix, and set it in motion in one sitting, whether that’s one policy or an entire segment.”.
