Deals on unlocked iPhones don’t come around very often, making this a rare chance to snag Apple’s lightest phone for less without signing up with a carrier. The iPhone Air is incredibly thin, at just 5.6mm thick, with its thickest point being the rear camera. To be this thin, the iPhone Air can only accommodate one 48MP camera, a 3,149 mAh battery, and a single-firing speaker.

ZDNET contributor Kerry Wan reviewed the iPhone Air at its launch, commending its portability and innovation while highlighting its efficiency and power-management challenges. The iPhone Air isn’t for everyone, and may disappoint casual people accustomed to Apple’s traditional iPhone form factor. But if you want a change without moving to Android, this is the phone to try.

Sources indicate that though it’s not the latest model, the iPhone 17 Pro isn’t a useless phone whatsoever. The iPhone 17 Pro debuted with a striking fresh Electric Orange color, an A19 Pro processor, a 6.3-inch display, and a pro-level camera system, consisting of an 18MP Center Stage camera, 48MP Fusion Main camera, 48MP Fusion Ultra Wide camera, and a 48MP Fusion Telephoto lens.

Recent findings suggest that the iPhone 17 Pro’s unibody aluminum design is quite sturdy, as ZDNET’s Kerry Wan used his without a case for an entire year, with a few bumps, scratches, and dents to show for it. It’s a durable phone, but not indestructible. Still, the iPhone 17 Pro’s support for Apple Intelligence and Siri AI, advanced camera system, and long-lasting battery make it a solid choice for people who need a workhorse phone.

It has been revealed that the iPhone 18 Pro is our pick for the best phone of 2026, and it’s even better when you can upgrade to it for free. We awarded the iPhone 18 Pro an Editors’ Choice award, thanks to its upgraded A20 Pro processor, improved heat management, and a camera system with an impressive variable aperture feature.

According to recent reports, with this Black Friday offer at AT&T, any trade-in valued at $230 or more (including an iPhone 15 Pro or newer in any condition) will save you $1,200, putting the latest Pro model in your pocket for free. Other devices valued at $130 or more can save you up to $930, and even old and damaged trade-ins valued at just $35 or more can still save you up to $350.

As always, the fine print shouldn’t be ignored; the savings will be applied as bill credits over 36 months, so you’ll have to stick with AT&T for the entire three years to get the full discount. If you cancel your cell service before the contract ends, you’ll have to pay the phone’s remaining balance in full.

You can also get an Apple Watch SE 3 for just $1 per month with the purchase, but you’ll need to activate a new watch line.

The iPhone Duo is an interesting phone, finally bringing Apple’s user interface to a foldable phone and bending the rules of how one can use an iPhone. The iPhone Duo supports the Apple Pencil for doodling and note-taking and sports a premium, durable design, featuring a 7.6-inch LTPO OLED panel with a nano-texture finish to bring everything together.

We gave the iPhone Duo an Editors’ Choice award for its polished design, great performance, and solid battery endurance. App optimization for the iPhone Duo is still ongoing, so keep that in mind.

Industry observers note that apple’s first-ever foldable comes with a staggering $2,000 starting price, but this Black Friday trade-in offer at Best Buy can reduce it. Both AT&T and Verizon customers can save up to $1,200 with an eligible trade-in, but there are a couple of conditions: AT&T customers must be on an AT&T Elite 2.0 or Premium 2.0 plan, while other plans can still save up to $930. Verizon customers will need to add a upcoming line to get the full discount, though upgrading an existing line can still save you up to $1,020. Also note that the discount will depend on the year and condition of the device you’re trading in, with the latest Apple, Samsung, and Google phones netting you the most.

Sources indicate that the iPhone 18 Pro Max is identical in features to the iPhone 18 Pro, but its larger stature makes interacting with quick-action buttons such as the Camera Control and Action button easier, according to ZDNET’s Kerry Wan. Additionally, a larger frame comes with a larger battery for longer use time, but also a heavier body.
