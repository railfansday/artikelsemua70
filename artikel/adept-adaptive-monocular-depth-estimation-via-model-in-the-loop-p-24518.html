ADEPT: Adaptive Monocular Depth Estimation via Model-In-The-Loop Photometric Tuning with Capture Risk Prediction

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 23 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (185 words, 📖 Standard)

It has been revealed that arXiv:2611.27423v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Exposure and gain determine the visual evidence available to monocular depth estimation (MDE), particularly when robot motion creates a tradeoff between light collection and motion blur. We present ADEPT, a framework that adapts these settings using learned feedback from a frozen depth model. During training, ground-truth depth identifies the lowest-error reference within a group of physically evaluated camera settings. A compact predictor learns reference-relative disagreement (RRD) between each candidate's depth prediction and the reference prediction using regression and ranking losses. At deployment, it scores observed captures using frozen-model features and camera, motion, and illumination context, without ground truth or reference images. A contextual Gaussian-process controller uses recent scores to select subsequent exposure and gain settings under motion and photometric constraints. We evaluate offline camera selection with relative and metric Depth Anything v2 and demonstrate online control with the affine-invariant variant on a mobile robot. Across nine equally weighted scene-illumination conditions, ADEPT reduces mean scale-and-shift-aligned $\mathrm{Abs}_{\mathrm{rel}}$ by 14.6% relative to built-in auto-exposure and by 10.0% relative to the best-performing evaluated prior controller.
