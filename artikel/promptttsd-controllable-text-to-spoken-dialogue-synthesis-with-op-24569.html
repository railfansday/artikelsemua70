PromptTTSD: Controllable Text to Spoken Dialogue Synthesis with Open-Ended Text Descriptions

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 23 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (216 words, 📖 Standard)

Recent findings suggest that arXiv:2611.27601v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Spoken dialogue synthesis aims to generate multi-turn conversational speech from dialogue scripts while maintaining speaker consistency and contextual appropriateness. However, existing models largely lack fine-grained controllability, which is critical for capturing the dynamic, context-dependent style shifts in natural conversations. While prompt-based TTS has enabled such control for single-speaker speech, existing datasets heavily rely on predefined tags, which fundamentally limits their capacity to capture open-ended styles. To address this gap, we propose a scalable pipeline for extracting conversational speech from multi-narrator audiobooks, featuring an improved two-stage alignment algorithm to preserve dialogue continuity, paired with LLM-based extraction of style descriptions directly from narrative text. We release PromptDialog, a large-scale multi-speaker conversational speech dataset comprising 8,383 hours of Chinese speech and 120 hours of English speech, with open-ended style descriptions for 79% of utterances. We further introduce PromptTTSD, a spoken dialogue synthesis model built via a prompt-aware fine-tuning strategy that integrates style prompts into text-speech interleaved sequences. PromptTTSD is an open-source spoken dialogue synthesis model that supports both zero-shot voice cloning and natural language style control. Experiments demonstrate that PromptTTSD not only maintains strong fundamental synthesis capabilities but also achieves competitive open-ended style control compared with proprietary models, and substantially improves contextual appropriateness in multi-turn dialogues.
