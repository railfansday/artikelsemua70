ClusterVAD: Threshold-Free Unsupervised Voice Activity Detection with Pre-Trained Neural Representations

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 26 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (187 words, 📖 Standard)

Recent findings suggest that arXiv:2611.33059v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Voice activity detection (VAD) is one of the first steps in speech processing pipelines, from transcription and diarization to speech language models. Supervised detectors need labelled data and inherit the domain and annotation conventions of their training corpora. Existing unsupervised detectors rely on hand-crafted acoustic features which cannot benefit from data and degrade in far-field, noisy or atypical speech. We propose ClusterVAD, an unsupervised VAD built on frozen speech foundation model representations. Fitted once on a small set of untranscribed speech, it clusters the representations and labels each cluster as speech or non-speech with sonority, an acoustic measure. At inference, a frame-level speech probability is computed from the cluster assignments, and a two-state hidden Markov model fitted to each recording enables threshold-free detection. Across benchmarks covering meetings, far-field and noisy recordings, Mandarin and dysarthric speech, ClusterVAD obtains the lowest detection error on pause-level references and is competitive on speaker turn-level references. These results indicate that speech foundation models inherently capture the distinction between speech and non-speech, which can be leveraged without training a detector.
