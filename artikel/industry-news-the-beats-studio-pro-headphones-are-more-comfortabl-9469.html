Featuring 40mm drivers with a dual-layer design, the Beats Studio Pro offer better sound quality than their predecessor. You get aggressive bass and strong highs, although there’s a lack of depth in instrument separation. One can switch to USB-C mode for better audio quality, thanks to support for 24-bit/48-kHz resolution on Apple Music and Tidal. Plus, there are three EQ presets — Beats Signature Sound, Entertainment, and Conversation — available in USB-C mode.

While I wouldn’t pick them over Bose, Sony, or Sonos headphones at the retail price, the $140 sale offer makes them easy to recommend.

The Beats Studio Pro feature six microphones for noise cancellation and voice calling. The active noise cancellation (ANC) is good for the fresh price. You get decent passive noise isolation suitable for everyday use, such as in cafes and on commutes, where they can easily drown out the public chatter. You also get very good call quality.
