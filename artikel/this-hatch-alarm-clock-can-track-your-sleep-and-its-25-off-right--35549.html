For the first time since its release this summer, the Hatch Sleep Clock is on sale for Black Friday at 25% off, so you’ll pay $187.49 instead of the usual $249.99.

The Hatch Sleep Clock uses a motion-detection sensor and ultra-low-power radio signals to track when you get into bed, your respiration rate, sleep stages, and any movement throughout the night. Similar to other sleep trackers like the Oura Ring, it measures five sleep metrics: time slept, time awake, REM, light sleep (which Hatch calls Core), and deep sleep.

I personally slept next to it for a week and found it to be fairly accurate in tracking my sleep stages compared to my Oura Ring, which I wear every night.

On the nights that I tossed and turned a little more than usual, Hatch would suggest a new or different audio to add to your “bedtime playlist” for that night, such as a meditation, a podcast, an audiobook, or a sound bath. I also greatly appreciated the fact that you don’t get a score based on how you sleep (which can sometimes cause more anxiety than it does to help).

In a recent development, as someone who has worn an Oura Ring since 2022, I find the Hatch Sleep Clock to be a great alternative if you want to track your sleep without the data and scores. This deal is only for Black Friday, so snag it for 25% off while you can.
