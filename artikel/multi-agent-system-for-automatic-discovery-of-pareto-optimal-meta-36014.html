Multi-agent System for Automatic Discovery of Pareto-optimal Meta-solvers for PDEs

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 26 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (214 words, 📖 Standard)

arXiv:2611.31611v1 Announce Type: newly introduced Abstract: Designing linear solvers for discretized partial differential equations requires problem-specific expertise. We present a multi-agent framework, in which language-model agents propose, critique, implement, and evaluate solver configurations assembled from Krylov methods, preconditioners, block factorizations, and neural operators. Inside the proposing agent, a non-dominated sorting genetic evolutionary algorithm evolves the population of meta-solvers toward the Pareto front of six measured quantities, so that one run returns a front of trade-offs rather than a single tuned solver. The system runs on small open-weight language models served on a single laptop, and we compare it with a single agent on four problems. For three-dimensional acoustic scattering and elasticity, it delivers 47 and 48 convergent hybrid solvers out of 60 candidates. Relative to the Jacobi-preconditioned Krylov baselines, the best of the discovered solvers reduce the iteration counts by factors of 4.7 and 60 and the online solve time by factors of 2.3 and 3.0, whereas the single agent produces no executable blueprint. For coupled dynamic thermoelasticity in two and three dimensions, where the baselines fail, it completes 98 of 100 transient candidates and, compared with the single agent, reaches a given accuracy 15 times faster in two dimensions and a displacement error 20 times lower in three dimensions.
