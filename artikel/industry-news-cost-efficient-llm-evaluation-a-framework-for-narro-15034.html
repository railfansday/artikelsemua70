Industry News: Cost-Efficient LLM Evaluation: A Framework for Narrow Confidence Intervals Under Fixed Budgets

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 20 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (281 words, 📖 Standard)

Industry observers note that arXiv:2609.29140v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: Large language models (LLMs) play an increasingly important role in both academic research and industry. Reliable evaluation helps researchers understand model capabilities and assess progress during model development. However, evaluation can be costly, and researchers often need to assess performance within a fixed budget. Because model outputs can vary across runs, this budget must be allocated between testing different tasks and repeating selected tasks. A confidence interval quantifies the uncertainty in the resulting performance estimate. Obtaining narrower intervals at the same confidence level and evaluation cost provides a more precise assessment of model performance, helping researchers draw clearer conclusions from limited observations. We introduce a framework that allocates a fixed evaluation budget across tasks and repeated runs, with or without full task coverage. The framework then uses disagreements between runs on the same task to bound estimation error, allowing narrower confidence intervals at the same confidence level when the sampled repeats show little variability. When repeated runs on each task yield the same outcome, we prove that our framework achieves the fastest theoretically possible rates for narrowing confidence intervals in the worst case as the number of tasks and the budget for repeats grow. We establish matching lower bounds both when every task must be covered and when tasks may be skipped, even for evaluators who use earlier results to decide which run to carry out next. On LiveCodeBench, our framework yielded narrower confidence intervals on average than uniform random sampling in 15 of 16 model configurations, with a median width reduction of 30.6% at the same confidence level and the same budget of 1,100 outputs per configuration.
