Hy-MT3: More Accurate, Robust and Practical Multilingual Translation Models in the Wild

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 23 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (245 words, 📖 Standard)

It has been revealed that arXiv:2611.27673v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Hy-MT3 is the third generation of the Hunyuan multilingual translation model family, including \textbf{Hy-MT3-1.8B}, \textbf{Hy-MT3-7B} and \textbf{Hy-MT3-30B-A3B (MoE)}. Compared with Hy-MT2, Hy-MT3 translates \textbf{more accurately, more completely and more faithfully to user constraints}: on real-world scenario data, the share of samples with mistranslations is reduced by \textbf{43\%}, omissions or additions by \textbf{74\%}, and instruction-following errors by \textbf{40\%}, while language coverage is expanded from \textbf{38 to 102} languages. With only 3B activated parameters, \textbf{Hy-MT3-30B-A3B achieves the best overall performance among all evaluated models in non-thinking mode}, including flagship models such as Qwen3.8-Max, Kimi-K3.1-Pro and DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash, and approaches the strongest closed-source thinking models such as GPT-6.1-Sol and Gemini-4-Argon, while surpassing models that consume \textbf{more than 30$\times$} its inference compute. Beyond general translation, Hy-MT3 is optimized for two industrial scenarios. For \textbf{game localization}, built with high-quality game-domain data and a professional evaluation system from the Honor of Kings team, Hy-MT3-30B-A3B matches or outperforms strong closed-source models such as Gemini 3.1 Pro on all core metrics, and can be plugged into game development pipelines through an open-source toolchain. For \textbf{image translation}, Hy-MT3 improves context understanding and error correction to better tolerate OCR errors, achieving the best result among models of comparable size. Finally, we open-source \textbf{TransRubric}, a rubric-based automatic evaluation suite for translation, which outperforms other evaluation schemes on the WMT26 metrics shared task test set and is more stable with respect to human evaluation.
