Amazon WorkSpaces Advisor now helps you find issues before your consumers notice them (Public Preview)

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Industry observers note that when a WorkSpace is slow or won't connect, the IT administrator trying to help has traditionally had to dig through Amazon CloudWatch metrics, logs, and configuration data across multiple tools to find out why. And in the meantime, the user on the other end can't get their work done. In April this year, we released Amazon WorkSpaces Advisor, an AI-powered agent that helps IT administrators diagnose and fix WorkSpaces performance, connectivity and provisioning challenges and get users back to work faster. With Advisor, administrators can investigate a WorkSpace from the console and, in minutes, see the likely root cause and recommended actions they can take right away. Since then, we've continued to invest in Advisor so your team can spend less time chasing down issues one by one, and more time on the projects your business is counting on you to deliver.

Industry observers note that today, we're excited to announce the preview of proactive troubleshooting in WorkSpaces Advisor, which moves your team from reacting to individual tickets to staying ahead of challenges across your whole environment. Advisor can identify issues across your WorkSpaces, explain the likely cause of each one and recommend a fix. You don't need to start an investigation, wait for a user to report a problem, or set alarm thresholds first.

If the same problem affects many WorkSpaces, Advisor will show it as a single matter with the likely cause and a recommended fix. Your team fixes the root cause once instead of chasing the same symptom one WorkSpace at a time. For example, Advisor can tell you when a group of WorkSpaces became unhealthy at the same time, when WorkSpaces are running low on CPU or memory, or when a certificate your people need to sign in is about to expire. That gives you time to act before a small issue turns into an outage or a queue of support tickets. You'll find these issues in the WorkSpaces console.

Proactive troubleshooting also extends Advisor to your customers, which means fewer tickets reach your help desk and the ones that do come with better information. When a session is slow or won't connect, Advisor will tell users whether the problem is with their device, their network, or their WorkSpace. It then walks them through the fixes you've approved, such as restarting their WorkSpace. When an issue does need IT, the user gets a clear explanation to pass along, so your team starts from a diagnosis instead of "my WorkSpace is slow."  You stay in control because you decide which fixes users can make on their own, and you can turn this off for any directory.

Proactive troubleshooting is available in preview at no additional charge in AWS commercial Regions where Amazon WorkSpaces Personal is available. Troubleshooting for end consumers requires version 5.37 or later of the WorkSpaces client for Windows, with the latest experience turned on. Troubleshooting for users requires the WorkSpaces client for Windows version 5.37 or later with new experience. To get started, see the Amazon WorkSpaces Advisor Administration Guide.
