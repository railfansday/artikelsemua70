VP, Global Startups & Investor Ecosystem.

The front door to AI in the workplace.

As thousands of founders, investors, and technology leaders gather in Helsinki for Slush 2026, the European startup ecosystem is undergoing a profound transformation. The conversation has moved from  AI experiments to operating agents. Across Europe, the Middle East, and Africa (EMEA), pioneering startups are deploying autonomous AI agents that reason, integrate deeply with core systems, and automate complex workflows at scale.

At Google Cloud and Google DeepMind we’re focused on delivering a complete AI stack for startups,including; Gemini Enterprise and the Gemini agent; the industry’s broadest choice of proprietary models including Gemini, Claude, GPT; open models like Gemma; multiple compute options including both GPUs and TPUs;  the infrastructure required to build and scale AI applications; and the tools to manage huge volumes of data, monitor token spend, and more.

This week at Slush, we are proud to highlight a remarkable group of European startups leveraging Google Cloud’s secure infrastructure and Gemini models to redefine their industries:.

CDON (Sweden): As the largest e-commerce marketplace in the Nordics, CDON modernized its backend and data infrastructure on Google Cloud. Its analytics pipelines run on Google BigQuery, dbt, and Google Cloud Storage, utilizing Python and FastAPI to ingest massive volumes of retail marketplace data. CDON democratizes real-time business reporting across brands and lays the groundwork for predictive personalization and generative AI workloads.

Sources indicate that chalkie.ai (UK):  A fast-growing education technology platform, Chalkie.ai uses Gemini's advanced reasoning capabilities to transform lesson planning for over 500,000 educators. By exporting directly to Google Slides Google Slides, teachers can instantly generate curriculum-aligned presentations and materials directly within their instructional tools while maintaining strict student data privacy standards.

Finova (UK): A leader in UK lending and savings software, has rolled out the UK’s first whole-of-market AI mortgage application agent, built on Google Cloud and Gemini. By deploying Gemini’s advanced reasoning to automate complex mortgage applications across multiple lender portals, Finova removes manual data entry bottlenecks and reduces errors. This saves mortgage brokers at least 30 minutes per application and reclaims thousands of operational hours industry-wide.

Sources indicate that fyndiq (Sweden): Swedish e-commerce marketplace Fyndiq (part of CDON Group) anchors its predictive analytics and data operations in Google BigQuery, dbt, and Google Cloud Storage. Fyndiq models transaction and product feed data to deliver near real-time business insights to merchandisers. By applying Google’s machine learning and performance models, Fyndiq optimizes customer acquisition and regional campaign reach.

Via Health Tech (Germany): Berlin-based VIA HealthTech is the AI assistant for psychotherapists. Powered by Gemini's multimodal audio and reasoning capabilities on Google Cloud, VIA turns therapy sessions into structured clinical notes within seconds and drafts psychological reports, giving therapists their time back for their patients. Trust is built in: VIA's security and compliance setup is BSI C5 attested and ISO 27001 certified, so therapists can use AI without compromising their professional secrecy. More than 4,000 clinicians already rely on it.

Building sustainable AI businesses requires infrastructure that combines high computational performance with strict data governance and predictable cost efficiency. This week we introduced the Google for Startups Accelerator: Sovereign AI (Europe & Israel). This equity-free, 12-week hybrid program is tailored for late-Seed to Series A startups handling high-stakes, regulated workloads across sectors like GovTech, HealthTech, and financial services. Participating founders receive 1:1 architectural mentorship from Google Cloud engineers, technical advisory support to prepare for EU AI Act regulatory sandboxes, and hands-on guidance deploying across our tiered sovereign portfolio—from the Google Cloud Data Boundary and partner-operated clouds like S3NS to air-gapped Google Distributed Cloud environments and open-weight models like Gemma.

Additionally, through the Google for Startups Cloud Program, startups across EMEA receive up to $350,000 in cloud credits , dedicated engineering support, and preferred access to partner tools and our AI infrastructure and models.

Whether you are building agents, scaling multi-modal applications, or migrating to scalable cloud infrastructure, Google Cloud provides the foundation for the next generation of EMEA startups .

If you are attending Slush 2026 in Helsinki, visit the Google Cloud booth to meet our engineering and startup teams, explore interactive demos, and learn more about applying to the Google for Startups Cloud Program.
