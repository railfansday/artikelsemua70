President, Global Partner Ecosystem & Channels.

The front door to AI in the workplace.

The Google Cloud partner ecosystem plays an essential role in driving customer success. As we enter a transformative latest era, our partners are instrumental in helping move AI from early pilots into true production-grade deployment. Closing that gap takes far more than technology alone. It requires skilled practitioners who can rethink workflows, navigate organizational change, and deliver impact. Operating across every industry and region, the Google Cloud partner ecosystem is driving this transformation forward.

In a recent development, at the end of each year, we are honored to celebrate our Partner All-stars who consistently go above and beyond for our customers. The Google Cloud Partner All-stars program recognizes outstanding talent across five distinct disciplines:.

AI Leadership (Fresh for 2026)Our AI Leadership All-stars are the trusted experts leading the transition to an AI-driven workplace. They help businesses completely rethink their operations from top to bottom. By guiding enterprise-wide transformations, they make it easier for organizations to adapt, innovate, and successfully adopt these new tools.

Recent findings suggest that salesThis year’s Sales All-stars are at the cutting edge of customer change. These individuals embody Google Cloud’s commitment to the customer transformation journey–consistently meeting and exceeding sales targets and delivering exceptional end-to-end customer experiences. They prioritize long-term customer relationships over short-term sales, helping deliver return on investment.

MarketingOur Marketing All-stars are the individuals driving strategic programs and key events that address our customer’s most pressing concerns and highest priorities. They work with cross-functional teams to ensure the success of campaigns and events. They take a data-driven approach to marketing, investing resources and time in programs that drive the biggest impact. These All-stars are curious and innovative, consistently exploring areas of opportunity to improve future work.

Industry observers note that solutions EngineeringThis year’s Solutions Engineering All-stars have delivered superior customer experiences throughout 2026. In order to meet complex customer needs in innovative ways, these individuals take full responsibility for end-to-end solutioning and collaborate across product areas. These experts maintain this high standard while continuously sharpening their professional skills and holding active Google technical certifications.

Delivery ExcellenceDelivery Excellence All-stars are the highest-performing technical practitioners in Google Cloud's Delivery Readiness program. Proving their expertise in the field, these individuals drive successful customer projects while completing advanced delivery skill badges. By embracing Google Cloud’s rapid-delivery methodologies, they accelerate secure migrations, execute 4-to-6-week iterative AI sprints, and ensure verified production deployments.

Celebrating Excellence in 2026Our ecosystem is made up of exceptional organizations full of extraordinary individuals. On behalf of the entire Google Cloud organization, we extend our sincere congratulations and our thanks to the 2026 Partner All-stars. Your technical expertise, strategic leadership, and commitment to customer outcomes set the standard across our global partner network.

Sources indicate that to discover this year’s honorees, search #PartnerAllstars on social media. Please join us in congratulating these leaders, who are shaping the future of enterprise cloud and AI.
