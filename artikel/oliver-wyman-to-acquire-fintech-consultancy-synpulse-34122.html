📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Oliver Wyman, the management consulting business owned by Marsh, has agreed to buy Synpulse, a technology consulting and AI platform firm focused on financial services, in a move designed to bring strategy and technology delivery under one roof.

The financial terms have not been made public, and completion is anticipated in early 2027.

Sources indicate that financial institutions including banks, insurers and asset managers are being reshaped by rapid developments in data, technology and AI, alongside shifting business models. With that pace of change increasing, these organisations are seeking advisers capable of supporting them through each phase of transformation, from setting strategy to implementing technology and executing change.

Sources indicate that the two firms have already collaborated through joint teams on several end-to-end transformation programmes. Bringing Synpulse into the fold will allow them to keep working in tandem, pooling complementary skills and staff to serve clients across strategy, technology and implementation.

It has been revealed that synpulse contributes technology know-how that supports financial institutions in updating legacy systems, streamlining operations and adapting to evolving customer demands. Its work in responsible AI strategy and digital ecosystem transformation helps clients launch products more rapidly, simplify how they operate and offer more tailored customer experiences.

Sources indicate that founded in 1996, Synpulse advises banks, insurers, wealth managers and other financial institutions on strategy, design, technology implementation and operations. It operates alongside its technology arm, Synpulse8, and a network of more than 100 ecosystem partners. Roughly 1,200 staff work across over 20 locations globally, and once the deal closes these employees will continue to be based in offices across North America, Europe and Asia.

Ted Moynihan said, “Our clients don’t separate strategy from technology and execution—they need great ideas to deliver real change.

Recent findings suggest that “Synpulse brings exceptional technology consulting and AI engineering capabilities, deep financial services expertise and a track record of delivering complex enterprise change. Together, we will deliver a winning combination of talent and technology to help clients achieve their biggest ambitions.”.

Industry observers note that synpulse co-CEO Yves Roesti mentioned, “Bringing our teams together creates opportunities to expand our reach across markets, take on larger and more complex transformation projects, and accelerate our growth across regions. It also strengthens our ability to invest in AI and technology, further enhancing the capabilities we bring to our clients.”.
