Asset4D-Bench: Benchmarking Coding Agents for Editable 3D and 4D Reconstruction

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 27 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (144 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

arXiv:2611.33728v1 Announce Type: newly introduced Abstract: A cart can follow its reference trajectory while its cargo floats above the deck. Asset4D-Bench tests whether coding agents recover objects, motion and interactions from visual references as editable Blender scenes. Its 114 conditions span image editing, video reconstruction with matched full or partial starter assets, and independently selected from-scratch tasks. We independently reopen projects and measure them against native reference states. The 228 archived scores include original delivery outcomes and versioned measures of scene quality. On 24 from-scratch tasks, each model earns full dynamics credit on 14. Of these, three GPT-6.1-Sol and eight GPT-6-Astra reconstructions earn zero relation points. Under the fixed scoring weights, relations contribute 5.15 points to the 6.50-point Sol-minus-Astra difference in mean recovered fidelity. Differences in execution and recovery policy across archived runs preclude a causal estimate of starter-asset effects.
