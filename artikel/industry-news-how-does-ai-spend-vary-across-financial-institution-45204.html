Industry News: How does AI spend vary across financial institution sizes?

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Nearly a third of RegTech spend in financial institutions is being used for AI tools, according to a new report.

This finding comes from the ‘AI in Risk and Compliance 2026’ report, which was co-authored by RegTech Analyst and Parker & Lawrence Research. The report examines how AI investment is translating into measurable returns, and the governance gap putting the gains at risk.

Industry observers note that as part of the study, a survey gathered responses from 300 senior compliance decision-makers at financial institutions and 100 technology vendors. This sat alongside detailed interviews with regulators and market experts to get an accurate picture of AI in risk and compliance in 2026.

The 300 institutions surveyed in the report forecast $703m in AI spend within risk and compliance during 2026. The Global State of RegTech 2026, which was also compiled by RegTech Analyst and Parker & Lawrence Research, surveyed a group of 300 institutions that reported $2.37bn for their total RegTech spend. When put together, it establishes a directional benchmark of around 30% of reported risk and compliance technology spend being allocated to AI.

The report also found that spend figures vary significantly with firm size, with AI spend rising sharply alongside organisational scale.

Sources indicate that companies with between 50 and 249 employees have a mean AI spend of $80,000, with AI accounting for around 4% of their RegTech spend. This grows steadily, with companies with between 500 and 999 employees have a mean AI spend of $430,000, which is around 7% of their RegTech spend.

Recent findings suggest that there is a step-change in spending among companies with more than 1,000 employees, which accelerates among firms with 5,000 or more. Companies with between 1,000 and 4,999 employees have a mean spend of $1.5m, accounting for just under 20% of their total RegTech spend. As for larger firms with teams of 5,000 – 9,999, mean AI spend is $4.7m accounting for around a third of their total RegTech spending.

In a recent development, interestingly, it is not the largest firms spending the biggest proportion of their RegTech budget on AI. The share peaks in the 10,000 – 19,999 employee band, with 43.89%, before falling sharply among firms with 20,000 or more employees, with AI accounting for 25.89% of their RegTech spend.

This is just one snapshot of the report, it also explores the global AI deployment intensity with a new index score.
