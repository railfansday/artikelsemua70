The full download size for GTA VI tops out at over 220GB on the PlayStation 5. This absolute beast of a game is a bonkers good time, and its size means you have to plan well in advance for the installation process. Fortunately, using an Ethernet connection, limiting internet usage, and closing active games and apps can help speed up the download on PS5 and Xbox.

I preordered and preloaded GTA VI on my PS5 the night before the official launch day, and it ended up taking around 13 hours to fully download and install. If you’ve been waiting to see whether it was actually worth the decade of hype — and if it would get post-launch patches — I learned a thing or two to help you speed up the process.

Industry observers note that even if you free up every available bit of bandwidth and all of your console’s resources, you’ll still likely have to wait several hours for GTA VI to fully download and install. But I’ve put together a short list of tips to make it as painless as possible.

Sources indicate that A Wi-Fi connection is very convenient since you don’t have to run a cable across your house to connect your PC or game console to the internet. But what you gain in convenience, you lose in terms of speed and consistency. An Ethernet connection is much more stable and can give you download speeds that come much closer to your ISP’s claims than a Wi-Fi connection could.

Don’t want to run a 100-foot cable across your home? A mesh network like the TP-Link Deco 7 Pro gives you the best of both worlds: the freedom of a wireless internet connection and the reliability of Ethernet. The satellite mesh router has an Ethernet port that allows you to plug in your Xbox Series X|S or PlayStation 5 for a hardwired connection.

I use an earlier TP-Link Deco mesh model in my home office/game room and initially started my GTA VI download over Wi-Fi, but it was going to take around 40 hours, according to the PS5’s status bar. So I switched to my office’s satellite mesh router’s Ethernet port, and it instantly cut the estimated download time from 40 hours to about 13 hours. Still a very long time to wait, but I’d rather wait overnight than almost two full days.

This might seem obvious, but there is only so much bandwidth your home network can handle for everything you do online. And while having multiple customers online at the same time usually doesn’t cause any problems, certain online activities can really eat into that bandwidth and cause a data bottleneck. Streaming and online gaming are among the biggest culprits, requiring significant bandwidth. Limiting these activities while downloading GTA VI can help free up bandwidth for the download.

Having an active game or app open while downloading GTA VI or any other game title means that your Xbox has to work harder to handle both tasks. Even if it’s a completely offline game, your console can still use resources to communicate with Microsoft servers, check for updates, and actually run your program. Having your Xbox focus solely on the GTA VI download means that it will be able to devote more of its available resources to the download, which can help shave a significant amount of time off the process.

Sources indicate that rest Mode on the PS5 is great not only for charging controllers and giving your console a break but also for speeding up game downloads. Rest Mode allows your PS5 to work in a hybrid state — most functions are disabled, but you can still download games and updates if your console is set to stay connected to the internet.

According to recent reports, downloading in Rest Mode can be quite a bit faster than doing so while the console is powered on and active, as it won’t need to devote as many resources to other programs. Rest Mode also handles installation and file copying, so you can set up your GTA VI download before you go to bed and wake up the next morning ready to jump into the action.
