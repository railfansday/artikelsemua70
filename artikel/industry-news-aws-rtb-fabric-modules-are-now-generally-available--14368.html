Industry News: AWS RTB Fabric Modules are now generally available through AWS Marketplace

🌐 Source: Amazon • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 2 min read (354 words, 📖 Standard)
💡 Key Takeaways
  • Recent findings suggest that AWS RTB Fabric Modules from AdTech partners including Pixalate, WURFL by ScientiaMobile, and Picnic are now available through AWS Marketplace.
  • To optimize advertising revenue, AdTech companies rely on partner signals to increase bid rates.
  • AWS RTB Fabric helps optimize real-time bidding for supply, demand, and third-party vendors.

Recent findings suggest that AWS RTB Fabric Modules from AdTech partners including Pixalate, WURFL by ScientiaMobile, and Picnic are now available through AWS Marketplace. With AWS RTB Fabric Modules, any demand-side platform (DSP), supply-side platform (SSP), and publisher on RTB Fabric can discover, configure, and deploy partner modules for use cases such as brand safety, fraud detection, device detection, and inventory quality directly in their real-time bidding workloads. Modules run inline at single-digit millisecond performance, with no custom integration, and partner charges appear on your single AWS bill.

To optimize advertising revenue, AdTech companies rely on partner signals to increase bid rates. Previously, each fresh partner required a custom integration that could take weeks to months, its own contract and procurement cycle, and added latency to the bid path. Now, DSPs, SSPs, and publishers can find modules in AWS Marketplace and attach them to RTB Fabric in minutes. Modules filter, enrich, or observe bid requests before they reach the bidder, to improve bid performance and effectiveness without needing to make any code changes. At launch, modules are available from AdTech partners including Pixalate for invalid traffic filtering (IVT) and screening, WURFL by ScientiaMobile for device detection and contextual signals, and Picnic for inventory quality. For third-party AdTech module providers, they can now more easily reach AWS RTB Fabric customers from a single AWS Marketplace listing.

AWS RTB Fabric helps optimize real-time bidding for supply, demand, and third-party vendors. Supply and demand partners such as Amazon Ads, Azerion, Bigabid, GumGum, Kargo, MobileFuse, TripleLift, UNICORN, Viant, Yieldmo, and more run bidding traffic over a private, high-performance network with single-digit millisecond latency and up to 80% lower networking costs than standard cloud networking, with no upfront commitments. Third-party vendors such as Pixalate, WURFL by ScientiaMobile, and Picnic now deliver their signals directly in the hot path through AWS RTB Fabric Modules. AWS RTB Fabric is generally available in the following AWS Regions: US East (N. Virginia), US West (Oregon), Asia Pacific (Singapore), Asia Pacific (Tokyo), Europe (Frankfurt), and Europe (Ireland). See the AWS RTB Fabric Product Page and User Guide to learn more.
