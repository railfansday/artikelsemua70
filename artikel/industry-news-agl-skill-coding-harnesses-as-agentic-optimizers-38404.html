Industry News: AGL-SKILL: Coding Harnesses as Agentic Optimizers

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 26 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (158 words, 📖 Standard)

According to recent reports, arXiv:2611.31794v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Frontier models are increasingly used through harnesses as agents, with growing autonomy to do useful work. Agent performance can often be enhanced by the use of skills: reusable workflows, code, and prompts that provide task-related information or guide the behaviour of the model. Skills, being textual in nature, present themselves as another editable surface to be optimized; current methods have improved on handcrafting and one-shot generation by treating skill development as an iterative process, using score-driven search to tweak and improve performance. In this paper, we demonstrate the efficacy of the agents themselves as optimizers: across five benchmarks, a coding agent without guidance substantially improves every target and outperforms published skill-optimization methods. More broadly, this shows the capability of agents to do such work and to refine their own behaviour to provide task-specific context, as long as they are given a metric to measure against.
