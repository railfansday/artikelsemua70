Why Combined Ratio Solutions thinks insurance technology should be owned, not rented

The best things are never easy, and that mantra has stayed with Luke Magnan and Michael Jones as they have built Combined Ratio Solutions in the InsurTech landscape.

Industry observers note that the solution was originally created after the duo built a policy administration tool for a client. As part of the deal, they retained the rights to the intellectual property, despite not having any plans for it.

Several years later they explored launching a business with the tool, but after assessing the market and what was needed, they decided that simply becoming another policy administration solution provider was not the best route. Magnan remarked, “We were going to look like every other startup software manufacturer in the industry. And we didn’t want to do that.” Instead, they chose a path they believed would have “a longer-term view of success than what other companies can offer,” he added.

In a recent development, combined Ratio Solutions released its OSPolicy in November 2025 under an open-source licensing model, making it available to everyone. This policy administration platform covers the end-to-end policy lifecycle within the P&C space. It supports account clearance, submission processing, upcoming business, renewals, cancellations and endorsements, with configurable UI/UX so clients can tailor it to their specific needs. The technology is free to use, and clients keep the full ownership rights to the code they create. Firms can work with Combined Ratio Solutions to support the development of the platform, or they can just go their own way.

In a recent development, magnan mentioned, “Everybody owns OSPolicy. You could check it out at GitHub today, you could build it, and you would never have to talk to us about it. You could bring in other developers to configure it or extend it. At the end of the day, we think core products like policy administration are a commodity and we think it is wild you would rent a commodity.”.

In a recent development, having the control of and flexibility over OSPolicy is not only beneficial to insurers now but will become increasingly vital as they embed more AI tools into their workflows. Magnan describes OSPolicy as the foundation that enables its clients to build their AI strategies with ownership as the most important differentiator. Firms might be tempted by using a SaaS provider as their basis and then add their own AI modules on top, but this can swiftly inflate subscriptionfees.

Industry observers note that he said, “It’d be like renting an apartment and then doing a full kitchen remodel. You might say to yourself, I want an icer kitchen, and this is what I want. But the truth is that next year, when you’ve got to renegotiate your lease, that landlord’s going to know and maybe they’re going to make it more expensive or change something that makes it not work or kick you out of it.”.

The price increases can sometimes increase periodically by as much as three times. However, a common deal Magnan has seen is where prices are tied to a percentage of gross written premiums. This might be a perfect deal when the firm is small, but if they are successful and grow their business, they end up paying more for the software.

“It really means they’re profiting on your appetite for risk and your success.” Firms might swiftly realise this deal becomes worse value for money, but they are locked in. They could seek an alternative provider, but they’d have to spend time and money rebuilding with the new provider and then most likely end up with the same type of deal. Combined Ratio Solutions aims to be the alternative route.

SaaS software has been an appealing way for firms over the past couple of decades to access newer technology without having to spend masses of time and resources building it in-house. However, with the rapid advancements of AI, Magnan is confident the popularity of subscription models is coming to an end. Instead, firms will see greater value in ownership.

He noted, “AI is a force multiplier for strong technical resources, and it is just cheaper to build enterprise-calibre software now. I think that everybody is going to be more interested in getting rid of third-party software company dependencies and owning their own technology. We provide an accelerator for that.”.

Sources indicate that through Combined Ratio Solutions, firms don’t have to start from scratch. They can take the basis and use it as a transition away from SaaS services, until they are ready to build their own fully personalised foundation.

Recent findings suggest that it offers a proprietary set of tools, leveraging AI models, that can handle insurance product and workflow configuration, establish policy documents, build rating intends and much more. Firms can get started quickly and either decide to stick with the Combined Ratio Solutions tools to continue quick and cost-effective development or go their own way. “But at the end of the day, you’re never locked into anything,” he added.

Sources indicate that combined Ratio Solutions serves as a partnership tech giant that only wants to do deals that will add real value to clients. At the start of the relationship, they will ask clients where that value would be, whether it is solving a problem or going after a fresh opportunity. It will then build a tool that will immediately start capturing that value, “in a time frame far shorter than what you imagine, and for a price far less than what you imagine,” he added.

It has been revealed that magnan offered a perfect example of how working with Combined Ratio Solutions and gaining ownership of infrastructure can add greater value to a client. He explained that one of Combined Ratio Solutions’ clients recently went through a merger and acquisition deal and was able to take their OSPolicy implementation with them as an asset. This technology helped to increase its valuation during the sale. If that had been a system built through a SaaS platform, it wouldn’t have been a source of value, but a liability that required continued investment from the upcoming owner.

What Combined Ratio Solutions sees as its value to clients is its dedication to helping them improve their bottom line.

“Our name is Combined Ratio Solutions. We are the only software company that I think has an actual eye toward every dollar that you give us; we want to improve your combined ratio. We are helping you increase premium, helping you write better risks, so you lower your losses, or we are lowering your expenses. And hopefully it’s a mix of all three of those. That makes your combined ratio better.”.

Magnan concluded by emphasising that the market is full of technology companies taking advantage of insurance firms while focusing more on their own growth than providing insurers with the tools they need to improve their offering.

It has been revealed that “We think that the industry is filled with a lot of charlatans and hucksters right now, both in the core platform and certainly in the AI conversation. There area lot of people who do not have the integrity to be operating in the space. And we are strongly hoping that with our different organisational structure and our different business model, we want to burn them right out of this industry.”.

Recent findings suggest that this is Combined Ratio Solutions’ main ambition. It wants to revolutionise the core infrastructure ecosystem that gives control back to companies and away from third-party providers. While it has ambitions for the growth of Combined Ratio Solutions, its bigger mission is to see OSPolicy transform the landscape for the better, even if firms take it and never work with Combined Ratio Solutions.

Sources indicate that he concluded, “It’ll be a change for the better. What we are banking on is that that change is enough for us. No owners would let us do it, but we have the flexibility to take a significant swing, and this is our big swing.“.
