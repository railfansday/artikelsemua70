Monitorable-by-design architectures for a post-Astra world: a compartmentalization-based approach

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 26 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (141 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Sources indicate that arXiv:2611.31610v1 Announce Type: latest Abstract: Frontier models like GPT-6 Astra are increasingly able to do substantial reasoning without a monitorable chain of thought, chains of thought are becoming less legible, and are often monitored asynchronously to reduce cost. With the future of chain of thought monitoring unclear, it may be necessary to design LLMs with monitorability in mind. We propose a modification to existing LLM architectures based on GRAM, SGTM, DEMix, BTS, and similar methods to compartmentalize dangerous capabilities so that their use, especially in combination with dangerous reasoning types that may indicate strategic adversarial use, can be monitored more cheaply than chain of thought in a way that might outlast monitorable chain of thought. We propose several variants of this architecture designed to balance tradeoffs and discuss other benefits, such as capability removal.
