Tech Report: The Largest Tractable Subclass of Multi-Scale Allen Temporal Reasoning

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 20 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (82 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Industry observers note that arXiv:2611.24498v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: In this paper, we propose a novel model for temporal reasoning over multi-scale intervals. We show that this model yields a complete reasoning framework and exhibits other particularly interesting properties. Furthermore, we identify the maximal tractable subalgebra for this model, as well as for the other complete model, in the qualitative scales context, which exhibits weaker properties but whose results can be derived by transfer from our new model.
