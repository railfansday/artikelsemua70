Google is rebranding its Spark AI agent as “Tasks” and making it free to use. The feature, which is powered by Google’s new Gemini 4 Hydrogen AI model, can do things like create meal looks, manage your calendar, and dig through your emails to find recurring expenses you might not need.

In a recent development, consumers in the US will get access to a few other features, including the ability for the AI agent to call businesses to compare product pricing and check inventory, purchase items with Google Pay, and sign into accounts that support Google Password Manager. US users can also assign Gemini tasks by emailing gemini@google.com or texting it.

Tasks in Gemini is just one of the many agentic AI features that have emerged over the past year, including Claude Duet, OpenAI’s Dots, and Meta’s Muse. Google notes Gemini will perform tasks in a “hardware-isolated virtual machine that’s dedicated to your account and never shared with other users.” You can also provide instructions for Gemini while it completes tasks. When performing more sensitive tasks, like purchasing an item, it will stop working and ask for your permission before following through.
