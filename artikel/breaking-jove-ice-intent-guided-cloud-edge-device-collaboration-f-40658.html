Breaking: JOVE ICE: Intent Guided Cloud Edge Device Collaboration for AI Glasses

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 27 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (240 words, 📖 Standard)

In a recent development, arXiv:2611.33735v1 Announce Type: fresh Abstract: Recent advances in large language models (LLMs) and LLM based agents have expanded the capabilities of AI glasses, enabling egocentric visual question answering, memory retrieval, and proactive assistance. These applications involve diverse inputs, including speech, images, and video, and range from simple device control to complex visual reasoning, posing challenges for efficient computation and communication. While video centric capabilities have received considerable attention, simple text based and device executable tasks remain underexplored despite their prevalence in daily interactions. Efficiently supporting diverse workloads within a unified assistant remains an open challenge. We propose JOVE ICE, an intent guided cloud, edge, and device collaborative framework that routes requests among AI glasses, a paired smartphone, and cloud servers according to user intent. JOVE ICE integrates hierarchical intent classification with dynamic subagent orchestration to select efficient execution paths and flexibly incorporate new applications through specialized subagents. Analysis of real world AI glasses requests reveals that simple device control tasks account for over 30% of interactions, which JOVE ICE handles directly on device to avoid unnecessary cloud computation and costs. Evaluations on three public benchmarks demonstrate that JOVE ICE reduces time to first token by approximately 13% and token usage by over 11% compared with conventional device and cloud architectures, while maintaining task quality. These results demonstrate the potential of intent guided collaboration to improve the efficiency and scalability of AI glasses.
