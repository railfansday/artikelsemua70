A Continuous Relaxation of Quantization-Aware Training

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 23 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (175 words, 📖 Standard)

arXiv:2611.27056v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Quantization is a critical piece of the deployment pipeline in the era of large generative models. Low-bit quantization, particularly 2-bits or sub-2-bits, is a notoriously difficult problem, particularly when using a channelwise quantization scheme which has been shown as a leading candidate for hardware-efficiency. The key challenges with low-bit quantization range from limited quantization levels to saddle points or flat regions in the loss surface. In this paper, we look at the problem of flat quantization loss surfaces through a lens of dynamically changing bitwidth. Unlike existing techniques which either change the bit-precision abruptly or keep it fixed, we propose to gradually alter the bitwidth as the model trains. To this end, we propose RelaxQAT, a continuous relaxation of the QAT process. We also provide simple theoretical and empirical insights behind why RelaxQAT helps the QAT performance. Our extensive experiments demonstrate up to 6.3% average accuracy gains on vision and up to 7.7% better average accuracy on natural language tasks, compared to the leading QAT methods.
