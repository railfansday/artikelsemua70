Recent findings suggest that charging generates waste heat, and that heat is the biggest contributor to battery chemical wear, so anything you can do to keep the temperature down is a good thing. The Ugreen MagFlow Pro 3-in-1 Qi 2.2-certified wireless charging station is built to keep your iPhone cool while charging.

It has been revealed that the MagFlow Pro 3-in-1 keeps the trifecta of your Apple devices — iPhone, Apple Watch, and AirPods — charged, as its name implied. There’s a 25W Qi 2.2 charging pad, plus two additional pads for the watch and earbuds, for a total of 35W of charging power.

The iPhone magnetic charging pad tilts through 90° to offer the best viewing position if you intend to look at your phone while it’s charging.

Industry observers note that so far, that’s quite standard for a charging station, but the iPhone charging pad hides a a dual-cooling system. While conventional wireless chargers can hit 108°F/42°C and beyond, this charging station combines a thermoelectric cooler (TEC) with a fan. The TEC, also known as a Peltier cooler, is a solid-state heat pump that moves heat from one side of a ceramic sandwich to the other with no moving parts and no refrigerant gas — it’s all done with electricity alone.

The catch is that the TEC doesn’t get rid of heat; it moves it. And it even adds its own heat on top, from the power it uses to make the cooling happen.

Because of this, you need a way to dissipate that heat into the environment, and an effective way to do that is with a fan, which is exactly what this charging station has in the form of a variable-speed, low-noise unit that works to keep the magnetic charging surface as low as 51.8°F/11°C while it’s working (take the phone off and the pad can fall to below freezing).

Not only does this drop in temperature result in a more stable wireless charging experience (by reducing thermal throttling), but it also helps reduce battery wear.

Sources indicate that people worry — and rightly so — about noise from fans, especially when the unit is destined for a nightstand. At 45dB, the noise level from this charging station is what you’d typically experience in a library, a quiet office, or an empty classroom. While many people won’t be bothered by this, it’s above the World Health Organization’s recommended guideline of 30dB for bedrooms (every extra 10dB sounds roughly twice as loud).

However, based on my non-laboratory testing, I’d hazard a guess that this 45dB was measured at about 12 inches from the charging station; your head will be significantly farther away from it than that. In my bedroom, I was getting noise readings in the range of 35dB.

Sources indicate that what I can say is that this fan doesn’t bother me in the least. Another neat feature is the Smart LED Display, which offers real-time charging status so you can keep an eye on the power each device is drawing, as well as total charging power and temperature. Also, it is used to show a low-input-power alert if you use a power adapter that can’t supply the minimum required 45W for optimal performance.

Something that I really appreciate about this charging station is that Ugreen has put the owner in the driver’s seat and built dedicated controls into the unit to let you easily toggle the temperature readout between °C and °F, turn the display off, and adjust screen brightness, while a separate button allows the TEC cooling and fan to be switched on or off as needed if you want a quieter night’s sleep.

As long as your power adapter can deliver 45W or more (and good news — the charging station ships with one that does just that), it has no problem hitting its stated output.
