Optimization of a Hybrid Solar-Biogas-Battery Energy Storage Microgrid: A Case Study

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 26 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (158 words, 📖 Standard)

arXiv:2611.33593v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Low-income regions like eastern Uganda often lack reliable grid power. This paper presents the techno-economic optimization of the Soroti Integrated Renewable Energy Microgrid (SIREM), a hybrid renewable system for grid-access expansion where Soroti District household grid access stands at 16.7\%. The microgrid integrates concentrating solar power (CSP) with thermal storage, biogas combined cycle generation, and lithium iron phosphate battery storage. Using Xendee optimization software with six years of NASA POWER data, the optimal configuration comprises a 37 MW CSP field, 70 MWh molten-salt storage, a 5 MW CSP turbine, a 6 MW biogas CCGT, and a 10 MWh battery system. The design delivers 61.3 GWh annually with a 42.91% IRR, 2-year payback, and levelized cost of electricity of 0.1991 USD/kWh generation (0.3752 USD/kWh load-served), achieving 32.2% total savings versus grid-only supply while exporting 28.8 GWh annually, demonstrating the viability of hybrid renewable microgrids for grid expansion in sub-Saharan Africa.
