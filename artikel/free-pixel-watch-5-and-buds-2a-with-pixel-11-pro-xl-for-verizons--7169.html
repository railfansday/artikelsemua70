In a recent development, if you’re a Pixel user looking to upgrade your entire lineup of gadgets, Verizon’s got a Black Friday deal that you won’t want to miss. For customers on the carrier’s Simplicity Plan, the Google Pixel 11 Pro XL is already $580 off, which drops the price down to $720, or $20 per month. Plus, you’ll get a free Pixel Watch 5 and a pair of Google Pixel Buds 2A with the purchase.

It has been revealed that just note that there are a couple of conditions to get this deal. To get both the smartwatch and earbuds, you’ll also have to activate a newly introduced line on Verizon’s $7.50 per month watch plan. Plus, the savings will be applied as bill credits over 36 months, which means you’ll have to keep your service plan for the full three years to get the discount.
