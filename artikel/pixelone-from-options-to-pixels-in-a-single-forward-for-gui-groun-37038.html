PixelOne: From Options to Pixels in a Single Forward for GUI Grounding

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 26 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (179 words, 📖 Standard)

arXiv:2611.30235v2 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: GUI grounding requires precise clicks at low latency. Recent models such as GUI-Decisions and GUI-Swift predict coordinates in one forward pass using fixed global bins with local refinement. However, screenshot downsampling removes detail needed to locate small controls, while fixed bins cover larger pixel ranges at higher resolutions. We introduce PixelOne, which transfers spatial knowledge from a search teacher to a student that predicts clicks directly from screenshots at native resolution. The teacher uses VideoJEV's text-option scorer to select rows and columns and progressively zoom into candidate regions. The student inherits the teacher's parameters and matches its distributions over explored regions through spatial distillation alongside box supervision. At inference, the same decision head selects a visual token, and a lightweight pixel head predicts the click within it, all in one forward pass. A click-aligned loss encourages pixels inside the target box to score above those outside. Across four benchmarks, PixelOne-Pro-35B-A3B achieves 83.8% mean accuracy, the highest among the evaluated single-forward grounders, with a mean latency of 0.43 s per target.
