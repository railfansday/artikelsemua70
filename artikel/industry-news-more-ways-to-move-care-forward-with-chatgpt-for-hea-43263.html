Introducing computer-use agents, a latest healthcare model configuration, and new healthcare plugins that work across healthcare software to get real work done.

Healthcare teams need to piece together information from the EHR, hospital policies, scheduling systems, and payer portals to deliver care and manage operations. With ChatGPT for Healthcare, we’re bringing the tools teams already use and the medical evidence they need into one platform to help them spend less time navigating systems and more time caring for patients.

Today, we’re sharing advances in computer use that help AI agents carry out multi-step work across existing healthcare software. We’re also improving how trusted clinical search finds, evaluates, and cites medical evidence. A new healthcare model configuration in the Responses API lets developers build trusted clinical search into their own applications. And we’re expanding our healthcare plugin ecosystem with more than 20 new plugins.

Together, these capabilities help healthcare teams find the information they need, make decisions grounded in medical evidence, and carry out next steps across the systems they already use.

It has been revealed that organizations often need to move information between disconnected systems to prepare documentation, coordinate care, and complete administrative tasks. Computer-use agents can help with these steps by navigating the software that teams already use: reading screens, clicking, typing, and moving between applications.

Today, we’re sharing advances in computer-use agents for healthcare, including more capable, safe, and affordable models for clinical and administrative workflows, and access controls and audit logs for secure deployments. We’re also sharing our work with healthcare organizations that are deploying agents, including Abridge, Ambience, Cigna Healthcare, and CVS Health, to process referrals, move documents between systems, and bring together the information care teams need. In the demo below, a clinician uses Voice in the ChatGPT app to ask for help following up on an MRI request. The agent reviews the chart and drafts an order and patient reply for review.

According to recent reports, healthcare organizations can start with a workflow that spans existing applications, set which applications agents can access, and establish clear controls for which steps require human review. To support HIPAA-compliant workflows involving protected health information, organizations must use eligible services covered by their BAA with OpenAI, apply required security and retention settings, and extend protections to connected systems. Content shared with ChatGPT for Healthcare is not used to train models.

Read more about our computer use research, customer stories, and demonstrations in Advancing computer-use agents for healthcare teams⁠.

Industry observers note that we’re improving trusted clinical search in ChatGPT for Healthcare and ChatGPT for Clinicians, including how ChatGPT finds, evaluates, and cites medical evidence. These updates help clinicians connect relevant evidence to clinical questions and translate it into practical next steps, with citations they can inspect.

To measure the impact of these updates, in a blind evaluation, physicians reviewed responses to real-world clinical questions across multiple specialties, including multi-turn conversations. In over 1300 reviews, physicians rated the updated experience more highly across all eight dimensions evaluated, including factual correctness, safety, actionability, and source authoritativeness.

It has been revealed that developers can now bring cited medical evidence into their applications with a healthcare configuration of select OpenAI models, including GPT‑6.2 Astra and GPT‑6.2 Sol. Available through the Responses API today, the configuration pairs the models with a harness tailored to clinical questions and trusted clinical search.

In a recent development, GPT‑6.2 Astra with the healthcare configuration ranked first among the systems we evaluated on HealthBench Professional, a benchmark of 525 challenging tasks written by physicians to test how well AI can answer questions about patient care, draft clinical documentation, and synthesize medical evidence. It earned a length-adjusted score of 74.4%, 6.3 percentage points ahead of the highest-scoring non-OpenAI system, Claude Fable 5.2 with web search.

All model configurations have web search enabled and are evaluated at their highest reasoning effort.

In a recent development, during a visit, clinicians rely on rapid point-of-care responses. On HealthBench Professional, GPT‑6.2 Astra configured for healthcare delivered higher-quality responses in an average of 23 seconds, compared with nearly three minutes for the same model using general-purpose web search even when using its highest reasoning setting.

According to recent reports, for developers deploying these tools at scale, cost per response matters too. In a separate comparison on the same benchmark, Astra with the healthcare configuration scored higher than Claude Fable 5.2 with web search at less than half the API cost per scored response ($1.37 vs. $2.90), with both systems at their highest reasoning settings.

Customers are already seeing the value of building with the new model configuration.

Sources indicate that the healthcare configuration builds on the API’s existing privacy and data controls. Organizations can support HIPAA-compliant workflows with an applicable Business Associate Agreement with OpenAI.

We’re expanding our healthcare plugin ecosystem with more than 20 new healthcare plugins from leading companies across clinical care, care coordination, and business operations. All of these are available today in ChatGPT for Healthcare, including through ChartGPT, which brings ChatGPT into the EHR workflow⁠.

These plugins let teams bring more of their work into the same conversation: reviewing patient records and hospital protocols, preparing prescriptions for clinician review, coordinating patient outreach, checking insurance eligibility, and analyzing healthcare costs and operational performance.
