I wasn’t new to the idea of a keyboard cover, but my previous experiences included bulky options that left weird residues and junk all over my keyboard. So, I turned to the internet, and that’s how I found the Uppercase GhostCover while searching on Amazon.

Keep your keys clean and fingerprint-free.

Recent findings suggest that the GhostCover is a paper-thin, clear keyboard cover that plops right on top of my MacBook Pro M3 keys. It’s silky to the touch, but is so sleek I never notice it’s even there. It’s translucent, so it doesn’t hinder my view of the keys, and it has a dedicated cutout for Touch ID. My laptop still closes flush, too, so it’s not bulky in the slightest.

It’s perfect. It regularly costs only $13, but it’s on sale for $9 (save $4) ahead of Black Friday. And I really wish I had bought it before one too many of my beloved mocha lattes permanently stuck my Escape key after accidental spills.

Sources indicate that the best part about the GhostCover is that it easily lifts off your keyboard, so you can easily clean your keys and your cover. I frequently shake off crumbs and other debris from my GhostCover for a quick refresh, and I’ve wiped down larger messes off of it with no problems.

The esteemed coworker in question.

I’ve had my GhostCover for nearly two years now, and it’s held up great. In fact, I love it so much that I shared it in our editors’ tech products we loved in the 2025 guide before the holiday season last year. It’s still my top MacBook accessory pick in 2026.

Investing in protection for your tech is universally recommended and just a good idea. For less than $10, I don’t think there’s a better investment in keyboard protection. Fingerprints, crumbs, and spills are easier to manage with a GhostCover on, and I appreciate nothing more than being able to continue my snacking and working in solace. Just buy the keyboard cover!
