Industry observers note that we’re introducing the Rosalind Academic Plan to help more academic scientists access GPT‑Rosalind⁠, our dedicated life sciences model, which combines frontier reasoning with Rosalind workbench⁠(opens in a latest window) that enables specialized tool orchestration across medicinal chemistry, genomics, wet-lab assistance, and other scientific applications. PIs and lab leads at universities without an OpenAI Enterprise agreement can apply on behalf of their labs through our trusted access process. Labs at universities with an existing agreement must apply through their university’s Enterprise access process.

According to recent reports, once approved, PIs and lab leads can activate free access to GPT‑Rosalind for up to 30 named members, alongside the other OpenAI frontier models included in the plan. API access is not included at this time. Verified PIs and lab leads manage member permissions and are responsible for their lab’s approved use.
