📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

finny, a WealthTech start-up offering personalised financial advice, has reportedly secured Rs 5.3 Cr (around $560,000) through an angel round, according to a report from BW Disrupt.

According to recent reports, A group of high-profile backers joined the round. Among them were Ramanathan RV, who founded both Hyperface and Juspay, and Anil Goel, who serves as Global CTO at Nielsen. Uday Sodhi, previously an executive at Sony Pictures, also took part, as did Aditya Pittie, MD and CEO of Pittie Group. Angel syndicate Zorvian LLP was among the other participants.

According to recent reports, the manufacturer intends to split the latest money across three priorities. A portion will go towards R&D, with an emphasis on building out its AI-driven products. Further funds are earmarked for growing its team of financial advisers, while the remainder will support content and marketing work designed to raise awareness of tailored financial planning.

The business wants to move professional advice beyond the wealthiest clients and make it available to the mass-affluent market. Its focus is on salaried households, supporting them with managing their investments, preparing for retirement and building towards lasting financial independence.

finny is a SEBI-registered adviser that charges no commission. Its service centres on the FIRE (financial independence, retire early) approach, giving customers a tailored roadmap with practical steps. Customers can work out the age at which they could stop depending on a salary, along with the size of the fund needed, based on their goals and spending. A FIRE score reviews their finances across three areas: whether savings are on track, whether investments are growing efficiently, and how well a portfolio could cope with personal or market shocks.

Prospective clients can book a free 45-minute one-to-one session with a SEBI-registered adviser to spot weaknesses in their finances and agree next steps. The firm also provides a one-off portfolio reset covering investment clean-up, tax harvesting and cash flow guidance. This can be followed by an ongoing personal CFO strategy, under which an adviser monitors investments and taxes throughout the year.
