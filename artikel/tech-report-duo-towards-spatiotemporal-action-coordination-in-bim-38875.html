Tech Report: Duo: Towards Spatiotemporal Action Coordination in Bimanual Policies

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 26 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (193 words, 📖 Standard)

arXiv:2611.32108v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Bimanual manipulation requires each arm to perform distinct motions while coordinating with the other. Common flow-based robotic policies combine both arms' actions into one token per future timestep, giving attention distinct positions across time but not across arms. We introduce Duo, a simple but effective design that models spatial and temporal dependencies within one shared action head. Specifically, Duo separates both arms into two action tokens per timestep, allowing each arm's motion to interact via cross-arm attention during flow matching. On RoboTwin 2.0, Duo improves the overall success of all four base policies, from 0.45B to 3.62B parameters, with gains of 12.55 and 9.02 percentage points for FLOWER (0.89B) and $\pi_{0.5}$ (3.62B), respectively. Despite its compact size, FLOWER-Duo achieves the best randomized-generalization, precision, and memory scores on RoboDojo. In real-world tasks, it outperforms $\pi_{0.5}$ in both success rate and execution speed, while scaling effectively with increasing training data. To provide deeper insight into the design of Duo, we conduct systematic ablations on inter-arm coordination, action grouping, and token granularity, confirming that cross-arm attention drives the improvement while arm-wise interaction offers the best accuracy-compute trade-off.
