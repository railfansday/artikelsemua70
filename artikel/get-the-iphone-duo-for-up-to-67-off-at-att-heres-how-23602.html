If you’ve been holding out for an iPhone Duo Black Friday deal, we’ve finally found a good one: AT&T will give new and existing customers up to $1,200 off Apple’s coveted new foldable phone (or an iPhone 18 Pro or 18 Pro Max) with an eligible trade-in — plus an iPad and an Apple Watch SE 3 for free when you set up lines for each device.

This deal would drop the price of the iPhone Duo from $1,999 to $799, saving you about 67%.

Recent findings suggest that the fine print: You’ll need to activate an AT&T Premium 2.0 strategy or higher, which starts at $100 per month plus taxes and fees, or select legacy unlimited prepares. To get the full $1,200 discount, you’ll need to trade in an iPhone 15 Pro Max, 16, 16 Plus, 16 Pro, 16 Pro Max, Air, 17, 17 Pro, 17 Pro Max. Those phones can be in any condition at trade-in.

Or, if you have one of these devices with a minimum trade-in value of $230, you could also still be eligible for the full discount: A Google Pixel 9 Pro Fold or any Pixel model after that, or a Samsung Galaxy S24+ and any Galaxy model after that.

If you have an older phone, you can still get money off the iPhone Duo (or iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max) — it’ll just be less.

Recent findings suggest that your discount on the phone will be applied in monthly bill credits over your 36-month contract. If you cancel your service, the credits will end, and you’ll need to pay the remaining balance on your device. Paying your phone off early or upgrading may also stop the credits.

According to recent reports, to get the iPad A16 (our pick for the best iPad you can buy) for $3.99 a month and the Apple Watch SE 3 (our pick for the best affordable Apple Watch) for $0.99 a month, simply add them to your cart at checkout with your phone, and choose the eligible installment and data plans. You’ll get the price of the devices in bill credits over 36 months as well, the same as with the phone. And the same rules apply here too: If you cancel your service for either device, the credits stop, and the device balance is due.

A $35 activation fee applies to all devices, according to AT&T.

Despite the usual mobile carrier deal caveats, this is a great offer if you want a fresh iPhone, have an eligible trade-in, and are already an AT&T customer or are thinking about switching.
