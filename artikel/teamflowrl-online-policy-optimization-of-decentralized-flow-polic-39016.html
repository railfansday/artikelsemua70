TeamFlowRL: Online Policy Optimization of Decentralized Flow Policies for Multi-Agent Coordination

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 26 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (156 words, 📖 Standard)

According to recent reports, arXiv:2611.32645v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Complex cooperative tasks require coordinated multi-agent behavior. Offline generative policies learn expressive cooperative behaviors from trajectories but remain limited by fixed datasets. Online reinforcement learning can improve them through environment interaction. However, fine-tuning flow policies is difficult because action likelihoods are hard to compute. We propose TeamFlowRL, a two-stage framework that pretrains multi-agent flow policies offline and fine-tunes them online. We formulate team policy optimization over the complete denoising trajectories of decentralized flow policies, avoiding intractable action likelihoods. The resulting objective weights each agent's trajectory ratio with a shared team advantage and clips each ratio separately, aligning local policy updates with the common team return. Across 51 paired configurations in seven simulation groups, TeamFlowRL improves every offline initialization, with a 47.3% average gain in normalized score. It also achieves 33.1% higher average success under exploration noise than Gaussian-policy fine-tuning across 6 bimanual tasks.
