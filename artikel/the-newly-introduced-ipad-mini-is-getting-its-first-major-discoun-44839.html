We were significant fans of Apple’s new iPad Mini when we reviewed it in early November, but its steep starting price of $699 was hard to swallow. Thankfully, the small tablet is already getting a sizable discount for Black Friday. Right now, Amazon and Walmart are both selling the 2026 iPad Mini with Wi-Fi and 128GB of storage starting at $599 ($100 off), its lowest price to date.

According to recent reports, the new Mini is a notable upgrade over its predecessor, with a larger 8.5-inch OLED display that delivers deeper blacks and better contrast than the previous model’s 8.3-inch LCD. Despite the bigger screen, the tablet itself is smaller and lighter, making it easier to carry. Apple also added IP67 dust and water resistance, so you have less reason to worry about where you use it. As my colleague David Pierce noted in his review, that made a big difference in his day-to-day use; he felt more comfortable handing the Mini to his spill-prone toddler or keeping it nearby in the kitchen to follow recipes. He even took it into the shower to keep watching a football game.

It has been revealed that the upgraded A20 Pro chip also made apps load faster and demanding games run better in Pierce’s testing, while battery life held steady at around nine hours of screentime. At $599, though, the upcoming Mini is still relatively expensive if you mainly want a tablet for browsing the web or streaming reveals. The last-gen iPad Mini, which runs on Apple’s A17 Pro chip, is available for under $500 if you’d rather save another $100 and can live without the OLED screen and water resistance. But if you’ve been waiting for a more worthwhile upgrade to Apple’s smallest tablet, the current Black Friday discount makes the new model easier to justify.
