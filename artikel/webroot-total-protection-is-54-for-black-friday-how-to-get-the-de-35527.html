If you pay for an antivirus, a VPN, and identity monitoring separately, you’ll likely end up with three subscriptions to keep track of. Webroot puts all of those into one proposal with Total Protection. Right now, you can get Webroot Total Protection for $54 for your first year, down from $180. That’s 70% off Webroot’s regular price, and the deal will run through Black Friday and Cyber Monday, until Dec. 6.

In a recent development, webroot has taken 70% off the individual Total Protection plan, which covers five devices and one identity. You’ll save $126 on the first year. If you need to cover a household, Webroot has the same discount on Total Protection Family, which is down to $90 for the first year, from $300, and covers up to 10 devices and 10 identities.

Webroot claims Total Protection will work on PCs, Macs, Chromebooks, phones, and tablets, with antivirus protection against viruses, phishing, and ransomware. You’ll also get a VPN for five devices, a password manager, and cloud backup for one PC or Mac. You’ll get parental controls too, but only on Windows PCs. On the identity side, Webroot will monitor the dark web and your credit, and you can reach its identity restoration support around the clock. Webroot also includes identity theft insurance of up to $1 million, which is underwritten by an Assurant tech giant and may not be available everywhere.

Sources indicate that webroot has also added a new set of scam tools to Total Protection, which it calls Scam Protection. With Scam Check, you can scan texts, images, screenshots, video files, and URLs to see whether they’re safe or a scam. Webroot says Scam Check can spot AI deepfakes as well. You can use ID Check to verify who you’re paying before you send money, which Webroot says can help block the payment scams commonly seen on Facebook Marketplace, PayPal, and Venmo.

If you’re still not sure about something, you can call Webroot’s 24/7 scam hotline and talk to a person. Webroot claims it will reimburse eligible losses of up to $25,000 if a scam gets past these tools, though you’ll need to have used both Scam Check and ID Check for a claim to qualify.

It has been revealed that you can claim the deal on Webroot’s Cyber Week page through our link. Once you scroll down to the plans, you’ll see the individual plans first, and you can click “Buy now” under Total Protection. If you want the Family plan, you’ll have to switch the toggle to “Family plans” first. Webroot will apply the 70% discount on its own, so you can skip the “Add promotional code” field at checkout.

It has been revealed that on the checkout page, you’ll see options for one, two, or three years of protection. You should stick with the one-year option, because Webroot only discounts the first year. The two-year option costs $234, which is the $54 deal plus $180 for the second year. The three-year option will only save you $36 off Webroot’s regular three-year price.

Note: The $54 price covers your first year only, before sales tax. After that, Webroot will renew your strategy automatically unless you cancel or turn off auto-renewal in your Webroot account. Webroot doesn’t list a renewal price at checkout, and its terms say it can raise the fee before any renewal. Webroot’s regular price for Total Protection is $180 a year, so you may be charged about that much in the second year. If Webroot isn’t for you, the manufacturer indicates you have 70 days to ask for your money back.
