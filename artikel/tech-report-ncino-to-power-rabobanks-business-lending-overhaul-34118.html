📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

nCino, an agentic AI banking platform, has agreed to supply its technology to Rabobank, one of the Netherlands’ three largest banks by assets, as the Dutch lender sets out to overhaul how it handles business loans.

Sources indicate that under the arrangement, Rabobank will adopt the nCino Platform as a single loan origination system, consolidating what has been a more fragmented lending set-up. The aim is to cut the time it takes customers to move from submitting an application to receiving their funds.

Recent findings suggest that rabobank, which was established by its own customers and has a strong reputation in business banking, wants to keep its position as the leading financier of Dutch entrepreneurs. With clients increasingly expecting quicker decisions, clearer processes and less friction, the bank is updating its lending infrastructure to keep pace with and surpass those demands.

Recent findings suggest that the nCino technology is designed to give Rabobank a more straightforward, rapid and open lending process, while drawing together the bank’s expertise, data and relationships to support business owners more effectively. Lending will be unified on a single base, with clients routed through automated, guided or bespoke journeys depending on how complex their requirements are.

Recent findings suggest that rabobank anticipates that a large share of its clients will use the automated route, which should allow relationship managers to devote more time to supporting customers’ growth. nCino’s built-in AI and governance tools are expected to let the bank expand its use of AI across the whole lending process in a responsible way, with staff retaining authority over decisions and with transparency, auditability and oversight embedded throughout.

Industry observers note that nCino serves more than 2,700 customers globally, ranging from community banks, credit unions and independent mortgage banks to some of the world’s biggest financial institutions. Its agentic platform is built specifically for financial services and other regulated sectors, pairing AI agents with human teams in a “dual workforce” model intended to remove inefficiencies, improve decision-making and produce better results for end customers.

Industry observers note that nCino CEO Sean Desmond remarked, “Rabobank is modernizing from a position of strength, and that is a bold bet on what business lending can become. By putting nCino’s AI to work on routine lending, Rabobank is creating more space for its people to focus on the ambitions of its clients, while preserving the rigor and trusted relationships that define its lending. We’re proud to partner with Rabobank on that vision.”.
