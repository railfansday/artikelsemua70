📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Block Scholes, a provider of institutional digital asset derivatives data and analytics, has extended its offering to supply continuous, round-the-clock market data covering pre-IPO firms, equities, indices, commodities and digital assets.

Industry observers note that because the feeds never pause, on-chain trading venues are now able to price, margin and settle options even while traditional markets are shut. This means positions can be valued at any point in the day or week, rather than depending on the price set when markets close on a Friday.

According to recent reports, the manufacturer indicates its data underpins more than 90% of on-chain options volume, which has positioned it as a benchmark source of real-world asset (RWA) option data. Its client base spans traditional financial services groups as well as digital asset-native businesses.

Sources indicate that all of the major on-chain options platforms offering RWA options rely on Block Scholes data, including Derive, Hypercall and Paradex, it claimed. The firm also operates its own oracle, allowing venues to draw index prices, volatility surfaces and option marks from a single independent provider instead of combining multiple separate feeds.

It has been revealed that block Scholes offers continuous pricing now spans every asset class it covers, with seventeen underlying RWAs available at present. These include equities such as Apple, Nvidia and SpaceX, commodities such as oil, and pre-IPO names including Anthropic and OpenAI.

Industry observers note that block Scholes CEO and founder Eamonn Gashier remarked, “With 24/7 trading becoming an increasing reality, Block Scholes is at the forefront of providing market participants with the ability to trade around the clock. This allows them to manage exposure and risk during weekends and public holidays, meaning investors and traders can react to market movements at any time of day.

“With clients ranging from data providers and traditional finance firms to digital asset exchanges and asset managers, we are seeing a rapid growing demand for our pricing and data feeds, making us the industry standard setters.”.
