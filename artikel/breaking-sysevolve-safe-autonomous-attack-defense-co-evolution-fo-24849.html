Breaking: SYSEVOLVE: Safe, Autonomous Attack-Defense Co-evolution for Evolving Cyber Defense

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 23 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (266 words, 📖 Standard)

arXiv:2608.15012v2 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: The offensive capability of large language models (LLMs) has crossed from research demonstration into documented reality. Frontier models have autonomously breached real production systems, and open-weight models now deliver frontier-level exploitation at negligible cost. Existing defenses cannot keep pace with such attacks: human-intensive defense updates over days to weeks, while AI-based defense, on the other side, trained on fixed historical data, does not adapt to an evolving attacker. We trace these persistent failures to one root cause: defense evolves in isolation, cut off from the adversary that should be driving its evolution. That isolation manifests at three layers: the deployment layer, the capability layer, and the adversarial layer. To build a defense that evolves at the speed of attack, we propose co-evolution as the integrating insight, replacing isolated evolution with mutual driving between attack and defense. Based on this insight, we present SysEvolve, a safe and autonomous co-evolutionary system built from three co-designed components, SysField, SysSpear, and SysArmor. SysField constructs realistic multi-host ranges via declarative topology-driven construction. SysSpear generates efficient, safe attack schemes via task-sliced local search, a bidirectional expert-knowledge base, and static-analysis verification. SysArmor performs real-time, interpretable defense via state tracking, CTI-context fusion, and stage-matched response. In evaluation, SysField achieves zero-loss collection at 2.1\% overhead and orchestrates 257 CVEs into 1,148 ranges. SysSpear improves red-team success by over 25\%, attains the best completion on complex multi-vulnerability ranges. SysArmor achieves 10--1000$\times$ greater precision than prior systems and detects real APT attacks in production at Huawei and Sangfor. Our evaluation also reveals four findings about LLM agent capabilities.
