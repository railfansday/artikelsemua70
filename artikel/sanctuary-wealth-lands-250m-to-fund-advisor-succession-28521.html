📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Sanctuary Wealth has received a $250m growth capital commitment from its existing backer Kennedy Lewis Investment Management.

The deal hands Sanctuary a larger pool of money to finance the ambitions, equity sales and succession plans of the firms on its platform.

The fresh funding will be used to widen the specialist expertise available to Sanctuary’s Partner Firms, helping them serve clients across a broader range of needs, win latest business and complete acquisitions.

Sanctuary will also gain more room to supply capital directly to Partner Firm owners, whether they want to fund expansion, cash out part of their stake or get ready for a leadership handover.

Kennedy Lewis, a multi-strategy private credit manager overseeing roughly $40bn in assets, has structured its commitment as $50m in mandatory convertible debt alongside a $200m delayed-draw term loan.

Recent findings suggest that in addition, the investor has chosen to turn its current outstanding convertible debt holding in Sanctuary into equity. The relationship between the two firms dates back to 2022, and since that first investment Sanctuary has doubled its count of Partner Firms and nearly tripled the assets on its platform.

Industry observers note that sanctuary’s network has grown to 125 Partner Firms, representing $71bn in assets either on the platform or presently moving onto it. The firm has completed 13 minority investments in its Partner Firms so far.

In a recent development, over eight years, Sanctuary has built a platform designed to help advisors leaving wirehouses establish their own independent businesses. Its scale and depth have drawn growing attention from advisors and independent wealth managers, prompting the manufacturer to introduce additional affiliation models that give sophisticated advisors greater choice in how they pursue their goals.

A dedicated committee of Sanctuary’s Board reviewed and negotiated competing financing proposals before settling on the Kennedy Lewis deal. Completion is anticipated in the first quarter of 2027, pending standard closing conditions, including the end of the relevant waiting period under the Hart Scott Rodino Act and approval from FINRA.

Sanctuary Wealth CEO Adam Malamed mentioned, “Sanctuary’s platform was built to help wirehouse advisors become independent, deepen relationships with their clients and build enterprise value in firms of their own. That heritage remains central to Sanctuary’s identity and growth strategy. As our Partner Firms continue to evolve, this fresh capital enhances our ability to invest in what they need as established businesses, giving them more ways to grow and their owners more options for realizing the value they have created.”.

Sanctuary Wealth co-presidents Vince Fertitta and Robert Walter remarked, “Our Partner Firms are our clients, and we have to earn their business every day. After years of helping advisors build enterprise value in their firms, we want them to have more ways to put that value to work. Founders should be able to monetize their businesses on their own timeline by selling a minority or majority stake and keep leading the business on the same independent terms that made ownership so attractive to them in the first place.”.

It has been revealed that malamed added, “Sanctuary carefully considered the best path forward for this new phase of our evolution and chose to expand our relationship with Kennedy Lewis. Kennedy Lewis understands our business and is completely aligned with our focus on helping our Partner Firms achieve their full potential. We look forward to continuing this constructive relationship and working together to capture Sanctuary’s long-term opportunity.”.
