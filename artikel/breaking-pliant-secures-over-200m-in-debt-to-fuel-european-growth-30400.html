Breaking: Pliant secures over €200m in debt to fuel European growth

Pliant, the Berlin-based B2B payments FinTech, has lined up more than €200m in fresh debt financing capacity across three separate deals with Ascory Bank AG and Viola Credit.

The fresh capacity is aimed at backing rising credit card transaction volumes and the credit lines Pliant extends to customers throughout Europe.

Industry observers note that commercial card issuing has particular liquidity demands, chiefly the need to fund customer credit balances and to post collateral for card scheme settlement. The new structures provide secured financing against both of these needs, allowing Pliant to handle larger volumes of transactions without compromising the efficiency of its funding model.

Two of the three deals come from Ascory Bank, which has partnered with Pliant for several years. One is a securitisation backed by receivables from Pliant’s European corporate credit card operations. The other covers the collateral needed for Visa’s settlement process, known as the Visa Prefunding Solution, giving Pliant the liquidity to keep pace with growing card activity.

The third deal expands Pliant’s current growth debt facility with Viola Credit and includes an option to raise the amount available. Taken together, the agreements are expected to underpin Pliant’s planned European growth for a minimum of two years.

The debt package comes shortly after Pliant revealed an $80m Series C round, which is earmarked for international expansion and further work on its AI-powered payment infrastructure. The newly introduced facilities sit alongside that equity investment, adding capacity specifically for the European commercial card business.

According to recent reports, founded in 2020, Pliant runs a modular B2B payments platform that brings together commercial card issuing, payment accounts, FX and spend management for both businesses and AI agents. Its customers span travel, e-commerce, insurance and AI-native technology, while FinTechs, software platforms and banks build their own commercial payment products on its infrastructure. Through its API-first platform, AI agents and applications can tap into regulated payment rails with permissions, controls and auditability included.

In a recent development, pliant CEO and co-founder Malte Rau stated, “As our European business continues to scale, it’s critical that our financing infrastructure grows alongside it. These agreements give us significantly greater capacity to support our customers’ payment needs while continuing to expand our commercial card business. They also reflect the strength of the financial infrastructure we’ve built and the confidence our financing partners have in Pliant’s long-term growth.”.

Ascory Bank head of corporate clients Lukas Diehl remarked, “We have worked with Pliant for more than six years, so it is particularly rewarding to see how our partnership has developed over time.

“During that period, both Pliant and Ascory have grown significantly, while maintaining the trust and close collaboration that have characterized our relationship from the beginning. With this financing, we are taking the next step and providing Pliant with a flexible solution that supports its continued growth.”.
