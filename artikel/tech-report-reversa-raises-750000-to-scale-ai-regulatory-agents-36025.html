Tech Report: Reversa raises €750,000 to scale AI regulatory agents

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Reversa, a Madrid-based RegTech firm developing artificial intelligence agents to handle regulatory tasks for businesses and public bodies, has secured €750,000 in fresh capital, which includes a pre-seed round headed by Itnig.

Recent findings suggest that itnig, the investment vehicle of the founders of Factorial, led the pre-seed portion of the raise. The Jobandtalent co-founders also joined, as did angel investors drawn from the legal and technology industries, among them executives at HappyRobot, Fuse Energy and several multinational banking and energy groups. Reversa also receives backing from Enisa and from the Neotec programme run by CDTI.

According to recent reports, the upcoming capital will help Reversa widen the international reach of its regulatory infrastructure and develop sector-focused products on top of it, targeting areas including financial services, energy and real estate. On the urban planning side, the firm is developing agents designed to streamline land-use permits, a recognised obstacle to building new homes. Hiring across engineering, product and sales is also planned.

Sources indicate that reversa’s platform features a regulatory radar, through which agents link directly to official sources, assess how each regulatory change affects a business and support the steps needed to put it into practice. The technology also tackles operational and commercial challenges, such as pinpointing which legal framework applies when a organization enters a fresh market, or automating compliance checks on a new product.

Customers also use it for jurisdiction-specific legal research and for drafting and revising contracts and policies. For public affairs teams, the agents track legislative procedures in real time, helping organisations prepare for upcoming rule changes and engage with the lawmaking process.

It has been revealed that the manufacturer points to the scale of the compliance burden facing organisations worldwide. Citing PwC’s global CEO survey, it notes that business leaders consider the regulatory environment one of the biggest obstacles to transformation. According to TheCityUK and PwC, compliance now accounts for over 13% of operating costs in UK financial services. A National Association of Manufacturers study puts the annual cost of US federal regulation at $3.08 trillion, or 12% of GDP, with smaller firms hit hardest. DLA Piper figures show European regulators imposed roughly €1.2bn in data protection fines in 2025.

Regulation also slows major projects. The European Parliamentary Research Service reports that EU electricity transmission projects can take up to a decade, with permitting consuming over half of that time. In housing, securing a building permit takes between three and 24 months depending on the country, while the European Commission estimates the EU needs an extra 650,000 homes annually. The Bank of Spain calculates that Spain alone faces a shortfall of about 750,000.

Founded in 2025 by Tomás Burgaleta Alonso, Iñigo Martín Llorente and Santiago Aliaga Estella, Reversa organises each jurisdiction’s rules using official sources and automates regulatory monitoring, legal research, compliance and public affairs on that foundation. It describes this category as regulatory superintelligence.

Industry observers note that reversa co-founder and CEO Iñigo Martín Llorente said, “Reversa solves regulatory problems for large companies and public institutions. To do so, it structures the regulation of each jurisdiction from its official sources, keeps it up to date and, on top of that infrastructure, builds specific solutions with artificial intelligence agents for different use cases.”.

Reversa co-founder and CTO Santiago Aliaga Estella noted, “Reversa is a regulatory system of action. It does not just capture information; it delivers the finished work. We build a digital replica of each jurisdiction, with its laws, institutions and procedures, and on top of it we automate workflows with agents that resolve our customers’ tasks end to end.”.
