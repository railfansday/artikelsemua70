The front door to AI in the workplace.

For the third time in a row, Google Cloud is recognized as a Leader in the 2026 Gartner Magic Quadrant for AI Application Development Platforms, positioned highest overall on the Ability to Execute axis.1.

According to recent reports, these days, it’s very clear to us that organizations are looking for AI to handle actual day-to-day work, stay strictly within compliance guardrails, and deliver measurable ROI. In the last year, nearly 500 Google Cloud customers each processed more than one trillion tokens. And a lot of that volume came from heavily regulated sectors like global banking, healthcare, and telecommunications.

Our customers choose Gemini because it delivers an open enterprise platform built for this level of production scale, by:.

Unifying the agent experience across everyday workflows.

Providing unrestricted model choice without vendor lock-in.

Practicing cost discipline before bills spiral, not after.

Governing agents through a single, centralized control plane.

In October, we announced a unified Gemini experience. The same Gemini you collaborate with every day is now your enterprise agent to get real work done. Instead of navigating disconnected developer consoles, consumer tools, and workplace add-ons, Gemini meets work wherever it already happens – collaborating natively inside surfaces like Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, internal chats, web, and mobile.

Sources indicate that you give Gemini high-level business objectives rather than step-by-step instructions, and it orchestrates the necessary tools, context, and coworker agents to deliver a finished result. As employees engage it, the agent retains persistent memory, understands your specific business language, and respects enterprise permissions across every device. When a team automates a complex procedure, they can package it into a shared skill, turning one group’s procedural knowledge into a capability anyone across the organization can use.

Recent findings suggest that “When you can use natural language, anyone from HR to sales can turn their unique business experience into a custom AI agent in minutes. In two months, we created over 600 agents. The majority were built by our frontline business teams right inside Gemini, leaving our developers to focus on more complex challenges.” – Robert Howes, Chief Technology Officer, Dojo.

Sources indicate that google Cloud is now the only hyperscale cloud provider offering access to all three leading frontier model families, from Google, Anthropic and OpenAI, on a unified enterprise platform – and across every surface, from developer tooling in Antigravity to everyday productivity in Gemini for web, mobile, and desktop. Through the model garden, organizations can access over 200 models, drawing down existing cloud commitments on a single invoice without having to negotiate separate contracts.

Recent findings suggest that at the same time, we are deeply proud of the pace of innovation coming out of Google DeepMind. Over the past month alone, we’ve rolled out a full generation of specialized models: Gemini 4 Argon for strong performance across complex software engineering, law, finance, and cyber defense; Gemini 4 Carbon for frontier performance in cybersecurity capabilities and long-horizon agentic workflows; and now Gemini 4 Hydrogen, bringing cutting-edge intelligence with speed and efficiency to everyday coding and agentic execution.

Sources indicate that by treating models as a diversified portfolio, we give our customers the freedom to optimize for speed, precision, and cost across every task. For PayPal, this flexibility is central to their strategy:.

Recent findings suggest that “We built a unified LLM stack. That meant we ran our open source models on top of our inferencing stack on GKE, and we ran our close-sourced models on top of the Gemini platform. Developers get choice – Claude, Gemini, Open Source – through one consistent governed access layer. That’s why we love the model garden.” — Srinivasan Manoharan, Director of AI/ML Platform, PayPal.

Sources indicate that A recurring concern we hear from enterprise leaders is the unpredictability of AI billing. When teams start scaling agents, pay-as-you-go pricing can feel like an open tab with no ceiling. That is why we optimize cost across every layer of the stack – co-designing custom TPU silicon, cluster networking, and foundation models as a single system to drive down baseline costs.

Sources indicate that at the execution level, we give finance and engineering teams the right levers to innovate without risking their margins. Gemini dynamically matches every task to the most cost-effective model, handling high-volume tasks with efficient models, only escalating to frontier reasoning models for complex problem-solving. Paired with native FinOps controls – including project-level monthly spend caps, anomaly detection, and Flexible Savings Plans – leaders get hard financial boundaries and clear visibility.

It has been revealed that for companies processing massive volumes of data, this approach makes all the difference. At Imgix, the engineering team prioritized this architectural discipline as they scaled visual processing:.

According to recent reports, “If we're using the most expensive model every single time, that's going to be a substantial bill for our customers. So we use a cheaper model like Gemini Flash to figure out, ‘Does this image or video even need this AI feature?’ before routing further down the pipeline.” – Alfonso Acosta, Head of Engineering, Imgix.

When organizations empower employees to build and deploy agents at scale, governance cannot be an afterthought. It must be an inherent part of the environment in which those agents operate. With Gemini, every agent is assigned a dedicated Agent Identity, ensuring that all database queries, tool actions, and state transitions generate an auditable record tied directly to authorized roles. Execution occurs inside isolated runtime sandboxes, where Agent Gateway and Model Armor automatically scrub sensitive data, block policy violations, and enforce corporate compliance in real time.

That same control plane extends to how agents interact with corporate intelligence. Rather than requiring organizations to move, replicate, or duplicate sensitive records into yet another external repository, Gemini connects directly to live enterprise systems. Agents ground their reasoning in data across BigQuery, Cloud Storage, SAP, Salesforce, S3, and Azure – inheriting existing user access controls and keeping proprietary data strictly within its original perimeter.

According to recent reports, governed context and centralized control are what make organization-wide agent adoption possible. UKG relied on Gemini’s security, governance, and evaluation controls to skip building that foundation from scratch:.

Industry observers note that “The Gemini platform, it’s solving some of these key problems that we already were trying to solve — from hosting agents to securing them to the governance around them, evals to make sure they’re behaving correctly. I can just use these things out of the box and get back to creating value.” – Thomas Newton, Head of Cloud and Engineering, UKG.

It has been revealed that being recognized by Gartner as a Leader positioned highest in Ability to Execute reflects our commitment to meeting enterprises where they operate today. By combining frontier intelligence, universal agent orchestration across everyday tools, predictable economics, and enterprise governance, Gemini gives developers and business leaders the foundation to build with velocity and operate with confidence.
