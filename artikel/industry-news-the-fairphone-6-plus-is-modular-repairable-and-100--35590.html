Industry observers note that unlike most smartphones, the Fairphone 6 Plus is designed for home repair, with easily replaceable parts that could help extend its lifespan well into the future. That makes its midrange price point easier to justify, especially now that it’s dropped to a new low of $552.49 (about $98 off) at Amazon as part of the retailer’s ongoing Black Friday sale.

The 6.3-inch Fairphone 6 Plus features 12 user-replaceable parts, including the battery, screen, USB-C port, and cameras, all of which you can swap out with the included screwdriver. Replacement parts are relatively affordable, too; a upcoming battery costs $39.99, which helps offset its middling battery life and lack of wireless charging. It also comes with a five-year warranty and up to six Android OS upgrades, making it a good option if you want a phone you can keep using for years.

In a recent development, although repairability is its marquee feature, the 6 Plus is also a solid midrange Android device. Fairphone prioritizes ethically sourced materials, making it a strong choice for environmentally conscious buyers who don’t want to sacrifice performance. Its Snapdragon 7s Gen 4 processor handles everyday tasks well, and its cameras take decent photos, though they’re not quite on par with those of pricier phones.

Sources indicate that what’s more, it offers a clean, bloatware-free Android experience and modular back panels, which you can swap for optional accessories like a card holder or crossbody strap. Our own Allison Johnson particularly liked the phone’s dedicated switch for turning on customizable focus modes called Moments, which can simplify your homescreen and limit access to distracting apps — something we could all use a little less of.
