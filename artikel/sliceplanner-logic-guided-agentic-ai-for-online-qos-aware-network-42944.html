SlicePlanner: Logic-Guided Agentic AI for Online QoS-Aware Network Slicing in Open RAN

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 27 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (181 words, 📖 Standard)

Industry observers note that arXiv:2611.33664v1 Announce Type: upcoming Abstract: Adaptive network slicing in Open Radio Access Network (O-RAN) demands reconfiguration decisions that respond to live traffic conditions while remaining safe with respect to Service Level Agreement (SLA) constraints, a combination that purely data-driven controllers cannot reliably guarantee without continuous retraining. We present SlicePlanner, a logic-guided closed-loop framework that treats each RAN reconfiguration as a controlled hypothesis: proposed from live and historical KeyPerformance Indicator (KPI) evidence, filtered through explicit SLA constraints and mined RAN control rules, and committed only when post-action measurements confirm improvement. SlicePlanner comprises two cooperating agents: SliceSense, which monitors live KPIs, retrieves context-conditioned evidence, and ranks candidate RAN actions through a surrogate-guided admissibility filter; and SliceGuard, which validates post-action KPI responses, rolls back harmful configurations, and refreshes rules under distribution drift. Validated over the air using OpenAirInterface, FlexRIC, and commercial Sierra Wireless 5G devices, SlicePlanner reduces SLA latency violations by up to 86% over static slicing and 50% over Deep Reinforcement Learning (DRL)-based slicing, while maintaining comparable cell throughput and auditable decisions without online retraining.
