Tech Report: Pet InsurTech Napo secures A+ backing and fresh investment

According to recent reports, great Lakes, which holds an A+ rating from AM Best, will provide the capacity, while ERGO Travel Insurance Services Ltd, part of Munich Re company ERGO Group, will oversee the arrangement as MGA under delegated authority from Great Lakes. All new business from 5 November 2026 falls under the partnership, and renewals will transfer from the same date.

The deal came after Great Lakes reviewed Napo’s underwriting proposition and business prepares, and it gives the pet insurer long-term capacity supported by an A+ financial strength rating as it expands.

Alongside the capacity agreement, Napo has added new cover options underwritten by Great Lakes. Its lifetime range now includes 40 options, with yearly vet fee limits between £2,000 and £16,000, plus a choice of excess and co-pay.

Mercia Ventures, an existing backer, led the funding round. IW Capital and Advantage Insurance Organization Limited took part as new investors, and DN Capital and Companion Fund, the pet-care venture arm of Mars Petcare, made follow-on investments.

The company did not disclose the size of the round. Napo plans to direct the capital towards further automation in pricing and claims and towards expanding its direct distribution.

Smyth has sat on Napo’s board as a non-executive director since March 2026 and takes up the chair role after receiving FCA approval. He has more than four decades of experience in UK insurance. He spent the last 15 years building and scaling MGAs, and as chief executive of UK General and Precision Partnership he led a management buyout in 2022, a rebrand to Bspoke Group and a 2025 sale to NFP, an Aon firm. He also chairs the Managing General Agents’ Association.

Industry observers note that jean-Philippe Doumeng co-founded Napo in 2021 and named the firm after his dachshund, Napoleon. He set it up because many pet owners do not realise how fast veterinary costs rise until their animal is sick or hurt, and by then it can be too late to buy a policy.

According to recent reports, napo handles pricing, claims and customer service in-house and applies AI where it makes cover fairer. Its machine-learning pricing models draw on the firm’s own policy and claims data, local vet costs and an AI-generated health profile covering more than 500 breeds. This approach lets Napo price rarer breeds on their individual traits instead of automatically classing them as high risk, and it can detect patterns such as how a dachshund’s risk shifts as it ages.

Industry observers note that on the claims side, technology takes care of the administration, and each customer deals with a single dedicated handler from the first contact through to payment. Napo approves over 95% of claims, typically settles them in about three days and closes over 90% within a week.

Napo co-founder and CEO Jean-Philippe Doumeng said, “Capacity is the foundation of any MGA. We wanted a partner who would look hard at our proposition before committing. Great Lakes and ERGO give us long-term backing, and we are already exploring where else we can work together. We are grateful to CGICE, which has backed Napo since its launch in 2021. The CMA’s reforms are bringing transparency to vet pricing and changing the economics of this risk, and we have built Napo for that market.”.

Napo chair Tim Smyth said, “I am delighted to be taking on the role of Chair at such an exciting stage in Napo’s development. Jean-Philippe and his team have built an impressive business with a clear ambition to challenge traditional approaches to pet insurance, combining innovative technology with a genuine focus on improving the customer experience.

Sources indicate that “The support of Great Lakes and ERGO is a significant endorsement of Napo’s proposition and provides a strong platform for its next phase of growth. I look forward to working with the team to help realise that ambition and establish Napo as a leading force in the UK pet insurance market.”.
