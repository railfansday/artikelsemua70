Industry News: Introducing ChatGPT Plus

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

We’re launching a pilot subscription plan for ChatGPT, a conversational AI that can chat with you, answer follow-up questions, and challenge incorrect assumptions.

For current intends and features, see:.

General access to ChatGPT, even during peak times.

Priority access to latest features and improvements.

ChatGPT Plus is available to customers in the United States and around the world.A.

It has been revealed that we love our free people and will continue to offer free access to ChatGPT. By offering this subscription pricing, we will be able to help support free access availability to as many people as possible.

According to recent reports, we debuted ChatGPT as a research preview so we could learn more about the system’s strengths and weaknesses and gather user feedback to help us improve upon its limitations. Since then, millions of people have given us feedback, we’ve made several important updates⁠(opens in a latest window) and we’ve seen customers find value across a range of professional use-cases, including drafting & editing content, brainstorming ideas, programming help, and learning new topics.

Recent findings suggest that we strategy to refine and expand this offering based on your feedback and needs. We’ll also soon be launching the (ChatGPT API waitlist⁠(opens in a new window)), and we are actively exploring options for lower-cost intends, business plans, and data packs for more availability.

We expanded access to ChatGPT Plus for customers outside of the United States on February 10th, 2023.
