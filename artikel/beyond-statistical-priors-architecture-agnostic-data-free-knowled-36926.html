Beyond Statistical Priors: Architecture-Agnostic Data-Free Knowledge Distillation via Unified Explicit Semantics

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 26 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (218 words, 📖 Standard)

According to recent reports, arXiv:2608.09287v2 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: Data-Free Knowledge Distillation (DFKD) transfers knowledge from a pretrained teacher model to a compact student model by synthesizing semantically informative data, eliminating the need for access to the original training dataset. Existing DFKD methods rely heavily on architecture-specific statistical priors (e.g., Batch Normalization statistics) to guide data synthesis; however, such architecture-dependent priors are often absent in modern architectures such as Vision Transformers (ViTs), resulting in degraded semantic quality of the synthesized data and consequently catastrophic performance degradation. In this paper, we propose UniDFKD, a unified data-free knowledge distillation framework that replaces architecture-specific statistics with explicit, architecture-agnostic semantic priors. UniDFKD governs the entire synthesis-distillation pipeline along three dimensions: (1) Categorical Semantic Conditioning (CSC) defines what to synthesize by persistently modulating the generator with language-derived embeddings to capture semantic diversity; (2) Spatial Semantic Anchoring (SSA) regularizes where evidence belongs by anchoring the teacher's spatial attributions to a Gaussian prior; and (3) Spatial Semantic Distillation (SSD) controls how knowledge is transferred by explicitly aligning teacher-student spatial evidence alongside predictions. Extensive experiments across CNNs and ViTs demonstrate that UniDFKD sets a latest state of the art. It outperforms the strongest baseline NAYER by average absolute margins of 17.23 and 24.49 in top-1 accuracy under homogeneous and heterogeneous settings, respectively.
