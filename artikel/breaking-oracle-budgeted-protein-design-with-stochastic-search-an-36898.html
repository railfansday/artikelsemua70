Breaking: Oracle-Budgeted Protein Design with Stochastic Search and Iterative Imitation

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 26 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (232 words, 📖 Standard)

In a recent development, arXiv:2605.26690v2 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: Optimizing protein sequences under tight oracle budgets requires methods that can explore a vast combinatorial mutational search space. These methods must also remain robust in the presence of surrogate noise and propose mutations on positions where substitutions are predicted to have favorable effects. We introduce SILO (Self-improvement Imitation Learning Optimization), a trajectory-level approach for oracle-budgeted protein design. SILO combines a hierarchical edit policy with incremental stochastic beam search without replacement (SBS) to explore diverse candidate trajectories. A UCB-based proxy ensemble, complemented by a proxy-derived alanine-scan-inspired fitness score (AFS), guides candidate selection for in silico oracle evaluation. The policy is then updated by next-action cross-entropy imitation on the round's best oracle-labeled trajectories. Across eight in silico protein fitness landscapes and five strong baselines from prior work, SILO achieves competitive performance within the evaluated oracle budget, often demonstrating faster early-stage improvement than other reproduced baselines. Two-sided paired t-tests show positive mean differences in favor of SILO across all eight landscapes for both maximum fitness and top-100 mean fitness, with statistically significant improvements after Holm correction on two and four landscapes, respectively. Under low-labeled data and noisy-proxy conditions, SILO indicates competitive, task-dependent performance. Ablations identify SBS as the primary driver of performance, while AFS guidance and iterative imitation provide complementary, task-dependent benefits within the oracle-budgeted optimization loop. Code is available at: https://github.com/grimmlab/SILO.git.
