AWS Elemental MediaLive can now create embedded captions from AI-generated subtitles and more caption sources

🌐 Source: Amazon • 📅 20 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (254 words, 📖 Standard)
💡 Key Takeaways

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Industry observers note that AWS Elemental MediaLive can now create CEA-608/708 embedded captions from additional caption sources, including AI-generated subtitles from AWS Elemental Inference Smart Subtitles. This means channels can deliver broadcast-standard embedded captions even when the source has no captions at all.

The new embedded caption encoder creates CEA-608/708 output captions from Teletext, WebVTT and other input formats. The captions are encoded as part of the live channel and delivered in HLS, TS and other outputs, with no external caption encoder or pre-processing step. MediaLive can also accept WebVTT captions directly from HLS sources and deliver CEA-608/708 out. This makes it easier to bring OTT and web-originated content into your live channels without converting captions first.

Together, these capabilities enable a MediaLive channel to ingest captions in the most widely used formats and output CEA-608/708, WebVTT, TTML or burn-in captions for each destination. This means fewer moving parts in your caption chain, fewer points of failure during live events and an easier path to accessibility compliance across outputs.

It has been revealed that these features are available at no additional cost for MediaLive in all AWS Regions where MediaLive is available. When AWS Elemental Inference smart subtitles is enabled as an input source, usage is billed separately at standard AWS Elemental Inference pricing and available in the following regions: US East (N. Virginia), US West (Oregon), Europe (Ireland), Asia Pacific (Mumbai), and Asia Pacific (Sydney).

To get started, visit the caption encoder guide or the AWS Elemental MediaLive documentation.
