Industry observers note that black Friday is just days away, and it’s a great time to make functional purchases that can help make your day-to-day routine easier. And if you’re really interested in upgrading your routine with some of the best gadgets, Bluetooth gear can make a significant impact at an affordable price. They also make great stocking stuffers for the person who already has everything.

Also: 10 cheap gadgets that are surprisingly useful.

Sources indicate that from Bluetooth-enabled chargers to MagSafe wallets to trackers and more, there’s a lot of gear to consider. Editors at ZDNET have tested all of these picks, including our in-house gadget expert Adrian Kingsley-Hughes and myself. These are some of our favorite nifty Bluetooth gadgets you can shop for ahead of Black Friday.

It has been revealed that the JBL Clip 5 speaker produces a lot of sound for such a small device. / Jack Wallen/ZDNET.

ZDNET’s pick for the best ultra-portable Bluetooth speaker, the JBL Clip 5 can connect to all kinds of different straps and belt loops. One of the best things about this speaker is that it works with the same app as JBL’s other speakers, which is more than many other ultra-portable options can say. On top of being highly portable, the JBL Clip 5 is also rugged, thanks to its IP67 water resistance, allowing the speaker to withstand bouts in the rain or drops in the pool.

Sources indicate that traveling for the holidays soon? This gadget turns your wireless earbuds into the perfect pair for in-flight entertainment. Call it a carry-on essential if you’re a frequent flyer.

Also: The viral wireless audio dongle for in-flight entertainment is better than ever.

The AAWireless Two Plus enables wireless Android Auto and CarPlay connectivity, with a multifunctional button designed for quick pairing and switching between Android and iPhone devices. Editor-in-chief Kerry Wan says the gadget delivers the best wireless Android Auto experience yet.

Also: My favorite Android Auto wireless adapter just got its biggest upgrade ever – and iOS consumers will love it.

According to recent reports, this Bluetooth passport cover was recommended by gadget reviewer Adrian Kingsley-Hughes, and it’s been a great addition to my Bluetooth gadget collection. Ultimately, this isn’t a necessity, but for someone prone to losing things, it’s a potential trip saver. While traveling through several airports this summer, it was easy to keep my passport and a few key debit cards all in one spot and accessible, which made it much more difficult for my passport to accidentally fall out of any bag I used.

Industry observers note that keySmart’s SmartCard Bluetooth tracker is another tried-and-true ZDNET gadget. Another favorite of  Adrian’s, this card simply pops into any wallet like a credit card and adds Bluetooth FindMy technology.

Also: My favorite AirTag wallet alternative is lightweight, super thin, and surprisingly cheap.

Industry observers note that the AirFly Drive works with both CarPlay and Android Auto, and connects to two phones at once. Plus, it’s compact and affordable, costing under $40.

Also: I was anti-wireless CarPlay until I tried TwelveSouth’s new adapter.

It has been revealed that govee’s smart light bulbs connect with Wi-Fi and Bluetooth and work with Google Assistant or Alexa. They instantly transform any room with customization for light color, brightness, and more. Easily control them with voice, too. Several variations are on sale now.
