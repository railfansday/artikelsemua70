The front door to AI in the workplace.

As AI changes how businesses build and run applications, more enterprises are spreading their models, data and applications across multiple clouds. To make that work, they need fast, private connections between those environments. Historically, connecting multiple clouds has meant ordering physical connections from each provider, setting up networking on both sides, and coordinating across multiple teams. That process could take weeks or even months.

We wanted to make that much simpler. Today, we're extending partner Cross-Cloud Interconnect to Microsoft Azure, now available in preview. The service is jointly supported by Google Cloud and Microsoft, and gives you a private, high-bandwidth connection between your Google Cloud and Azure environments that’s set up programmatically in minutes, with no physical infrastructure to manage.

"Google Cloud partner Cross-Cloud Interconnect simplifies multi-cloud networking with a direct cloud-native approach. By removing traditional infrastructure complexity, it provides a reliable, scalable foundation for connecting workloads across different cloud environments." - Ralf Huebenthal, Global Head of IT & Digital Platforms, Nestlé.

“Walmart runs a seamless platform across multiple cloud providers to accelerate innovations. We partner with Google Cloud to leverage their global network for hybrid and multicloud networking. With Cross-Cloud Interconnect, we were able to simplify connectivity between cloud providers, shorten time to production, and reduce overall costs.” - Gerald Bothello, Group Director of Software Engineering, Walmart.

On-demand connectivity in minutes, not weeks. Set up your connection from the Google Cloud console or API, pick the bandwidth you need, and both clouds handle the rest — no hardware to order, no manual network configuration, and no back and forth between providers.

Built-in reliability. Your connection runs across physically separate locations for redundancy. Google Cloud and Azure jointly monitor the connection and handle maintenance and recovery for you.

Private and secure. Your traffic stays off the public internet, and is automatically encrypted between the Google Cloud and Azure edge routers, with no customer configuration required.

Industry observers note that predictable pricing. You pay a fixed price for the connection's bandwidth, so your Google Cloud costs are easy to initiative and manage.

It has been revealed that "Customers are building applications, data platforms and AI across more than one cloud, and they want the network between those clouds to just work. Azure Multicloud Interconnect with Google Cloud gives customers a private, managed path between Azure and Google Cloud, and we're excited to work with Google Cloud to bring it to customers in public preview." - Sudha Mahajan, Partner, Product Management, Azure Networking, Microsoft.

Our Cross-Cloud Interconnect ecosystem continues to expand:.

In a recent development, partner Cross-Cloud Interconnect for Microsoft Azure is available in preview today in us-east4 (N. Virginia), us-west2 (Los Angeles), and europe-west3 (Frankfurt), alongside the generally available AWS and OCI offerings. We'll add more regions as the Azure offering moves to general availability.

Partner Cross-Cloud Interconnect for last mile with Lumen is available in preview, for customers connecting their private clouds, on-premises sites, and neocloud environments to Google Cloud.

Partner Cross-Cloud Interconnect is built on an open specification for network interoperability. Google Cloud collaborated with AWS and published it last year, and any provider can adopt it.

To learn more, visit the partner Cross-Cloud Interconnect documentation, read Microsoft's announcement, or contact your Google Cloud account team.
