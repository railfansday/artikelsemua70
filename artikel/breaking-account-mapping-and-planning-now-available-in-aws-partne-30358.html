Industry observers note that AWS Partners can now map their target accounts against AWS records and find where to collaborate with AWS on joint go-to-market before an opportunity exists. Account Mapping and Planning in AWS Partner Central replaces manual, spreadsheet-based account mapping. Upload a target account list through CSV, API, or CRM integration, and accounts are matched automatically against AWS records, including accounts without an ACE opportunity and in any country.

According to recent reports, partners can use Account Mapping and Planning to enrich matched accounts with AWS Marketplace propensity insights, solution-level purchase likelihood, and eligibility for programs such as the Partner Greenfield Program and the Partner-Led Sales Motion. This helps Partners prioritize high-propensity and program-eligible accounts for go-to-market activity. Eligible Partners can also request joint account planning with AWS sales on accounts marked Qualified, so AWS and Partner teams can align on an account strategy earlier. Account planning is available to Consulting Partners that are ACE-eligible, Premier tier and managed by an AWS Partner Development Manager (PDM), and to ISVs that are ACE-eligible, enrolled in the SaaS Co-sell Benefit program and PDM-managed.

In a recent development, account Mapping and Planning is available under the Sell tab in AWS Partner Central, and account matching and enrichment are open to all ACE-eligible Partners. Partners can work in the Partner Central console, through the API and SDK, through the Partner Central MCP server, or through an integration Partner to connect results to their CRM.

To get started, sign in to AWS Partner Central, go to the Account Mapping page under the Sell tab, and upload a target account list.
