Global Kiwi Labs, the developer behind digital asset colocation network TheHUB, has closed a $4m seed round as it looks to bring the equal-access trading architecture used by major traditional exchanges to digital asset markets.

It has been revealed that valor Capital Group led the round. Adderall Capital, Keyrock and Staking Facilities also invested, alongside a small number of angel backers.

TheHUB’s first site, which is located within the Equinix TY15 data centre in Tokyo and is now live and bringing on exchanges, trading firms and validators. The company will also use the money to add engineers to its team and move into further regions.

Global Kiwi Labs is targeting a structural gap in digital asset trading. Traditional exchanges have worked for decades to stop any participant from paying for a quicker route to the order book, but digital asset markets never developed an equivalent layer.

Recent findings suggest that as a result, the biggest players pay for proximity, while the rest of the market relies on slower and inconsistent links. A firm that spots a price change first can trade against participants still quoting the outdated price.

The company argues this affects more than high-frequency traders. When market makers cannot be confident their quotes will land on time, they widen spreads to offset the risk, which raises costs for every buyer and seller. Removing that uncertainty would allow tighter quoting and better pricing across the market.

TheHUB adapts four elements of traditional exchange infrastructure for digital assets. Each participant’s link to the central switch uses an identical length of fibre, calibrated to within 10 millimetres, so no firm gains a proximity edge to the matching engine.

The company mentioned that as additional exchanges and infrastructure providers join, participants gain access to a wider ecosystem without having to replicate connectivity or infrastructure spending.

Founded in 2025, TheHUB describes itself as the first colocation infrastructure purpose-built for digital markets, positioned as a transaction centre where digital assets and traditional finance meet.

Valor Capital Group partner Bruno Batavia noted, “At Valor, we look for companies building the critical infrastructure that will support the next generation of financial markets. TheHUB is developing an essential technology layer that enables digital asset markets to achieve the performance standards required by global institutional participants. Jamie and his team have demonstrated an ambitious vision combined with exceptional execution, and we’re excited to support them through this next stage of growth.”.

Global Kiwi Labs CEO Jamie Brown noted, Traditional financial markets didn’t reach today’s levels of performance through software alone. They built specialized physical infrastructure around the markets themselves. Digital asset markets are reaching the point where they need the same foundation. We’re building TheHUB so exchanges, traders, and infrastructure providers can access that foundation as a shared network rather than rebuilding it independently.”.
