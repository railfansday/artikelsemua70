TBC Georgia, a leading Georgian financial institution and member of the London-listed TBC Bank Group, has raised $51m from international commercial banks to widen the availability of trade finance for businesses across Georgia.

The capital comes with guarantee backing from the Asian Development Bank (ADB) and the International Finance Corporation (IFC).

Industry observers note that it is channelled through two established schemes: the ADB’s Trade and Supply Chain Finance Program and the IFC’s Global Trade Finance Program. Because both institutions stand behind the facility, commercial lenders can extend capital to the Georgian market with added assurance.

According to recent reports, the resources are aimed at accelerating growth in Georgia’s retail trade sector. Local companies will find it easier to obtain trade finance, which should strengthen their footing as they compete abroad. Firms will be better placed to run their export and import activity efficiently, and to scale operations without increasing their risk exposure.

According to recent reports, for TBC Georgia, the agreement deepens its existing relationships with international development lenders and builds on its client base in this area. The bank says more than 1,000 companies already rely on it for trade finance services, and it views this deal as a further step in reinforcing its leading position in the segment.

TBC Georgia ranks among the country’s foremost financial institutions and operates as part of TBC Bank Group, whose shares are listed in London. The bank offers trade finance and factoring services to Georgian businesses, among other products.

TBC Georgia director of trade finance and factoring Tamar Khizanishvili remarked, “Trade finance is an important tool for business growth and international expansion. Our goal is to make it easier for companies to benefit from these opportunities and support their development, and we are pleased to be partnering with international financial institutions to that end.

“The secured resources expand the financing opportunities available to businesses and enable us to respond more effectively to the diverse needs of the private sector. We are proud that more than 1,000 companies have placed their trust in TBC, with this agreement marking another step towards cementing our market leading position in this segment.”.
