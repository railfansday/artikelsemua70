Held-to-Maturity Accounting: Hidden Losses and Systemic Fragility

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 23 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (137 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

According to recent reports, arXiv:2611.26700v1 Announce Type: newly introduced Abstract: This paper studies how accounting-induced opacity affects systemic risk in the banking system. Motivated by the buildup of unrealized losses during recent monetary tightening episodes, we develop a model in which banks hold correlated interest rate sensitive assets and are connected through an endogenous interbank network. We show that Held-To-Maturity (HTM) accounting amplifies systemic risk by delaying loss recognition, allowing losses to accumulate and become synchronized across institutions. When losses are realized under liquidity stress, the interaction of correlated exposures and network linkages generates nonlinear increases in defaults. These findings suggest that accounting regimes are not neutral from a financial stability perspective, as they influence both the buildup and propagation of systemic risk, particularly in environments with common asset exposures and strong interconnectedness.
