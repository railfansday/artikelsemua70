Framework announced its Wireless Touchpad Keyboard earlier this year with the goal of delivering a better couch keyboarding experience than the Logitech K400. According to The Verge’s Sean Hollister who went hands-on with Framework’s new all-in-one keyboard and touchpad last April, the manufacturer has succeeded in that goal, but it turns out you’ll be paying a bit more for Framework’s than Logitech’s.

Sources indicate that while the K400 couch keyboard is $35, the Framework Wireless Touchpad Keyboard is $99 with a $50 fully refundable deposit required to preorder one starting today. That makes it nearly three times more expensive than Logitech’s, but Framework’s upgrades and improvements could make it worth the premium.

Instead of starting from scratch, Framework uses the same keyboard module found in its Laptop 12 paired with a precision 120Hz touchpad and controller that’s also used in all of the organization’s computers. A Bluetooth connection is the easiest way to pair the keyboard to PCs running Windows, Linux, or macOS, or mobile devices, game consoles, and streaming boxes. You can swiftly switch between four wirelessly connected devices with a button press including one that uses an included USB receiver. If you want more responsiveness, you can also tether it to a device with a USB-C cable.

On a full charge of its replaceable 1,200mAh battery the keyboard will run for well over a year with sporadic use. If you’re instead using it for hours every day on a desk, Framework estimates the battery life will be closer to over a month. For customizability and remapping the keyboard’s open source firmware is based on a fork of ZMK.
