The front door to AI in the workplace.

Google has been recognized as a Leader and positioned furthest in vision in the 2026 Gartner® Magic Quadrant™ for Cloud Operational Database Management Systems (Cloud ODBMS).

This recognition comes as we cross into the era of agentic AI. Leading enterprises are in their journey of deploying autonomous agents to transform core business functions. At the center is data, the fuel for AI. In our view, this recognition from Gartner — particularly our placement furthest for Completeness of Vision — builds on the August 2026 Gartner® report, AI Vendor Race: Google Is the Manufacturer to Beat in Data Platforms for AI. Together, we believe this validates our long-standing belief that the future of operational data is unified, open, and inseparable from the AI layer. In our opinion, this is the blueprint for what we call the Agentic Data Cloud.

It has been revealed that powering a true System of Action through the Agentic Data Cloud requires operational data to be unified, open, and inseparable from the AI tier. We deliver on this across our operational databases by converging transaction processing and analytics in a single engine, while leveraging Google's full-stack AI advantage to integrate Google DeepMind models and vector indexing directly into the core engine over our AI Hypercomputer. Furthermore, we define openness not just through 100% PostgreSQL compatibility and open Apache Iceberg formats, but by providing borderless access to data across other clouds and environments without requiring complete data replication. This eliminates operational friction and costly data movement, providing the low-latency, planet-scale foundation needed to power trusted autonomous agents with confidence.

The Agentic Data Cloud is engineered from the ground up to minimize the complexity and hidden costs of the agentic era. Let's review the core benefits enterprises realize when operating on Google Cloud operational databases, along with our latest innovations to help you thrive in the agentic era.

Our operational database portfolio, anchored by Spanner and AlloyDB, integrates AI natively within the database engine. In AlloyDB, auto vector embeddings and auto vector indexing automatically generate, maintain, and self-tune vector data using simple SQL commands, eliminating complex ETL pipelines. Furthermore, built-in support for Google DeepMind models such as TimesFM enables zero-shot time-series forecasting directly in SQL. With Spanner, organizations can combine full-text, vector, and graph capabilities in a single database to power complex multi-model agentic workflows and context-aware applications with enterprise-grade availability.

In the companion 2026 Gartner® Critical Capabilities for Cloud Operational Database Management Systems report, Google’s operational database portfolio was evaluated as Google (Spanner) and Google (AlloyDB) for the following use cases.

Industry observers note that agentic AI Operations use case: Google was ranked first among all evaluated vendors, with Google (Spanner) ranking #1 and Google (AlloyDB) ranking #2. Capabilities such as natively built AI functions provide developers the ability to bring the power of foundational models within operational databases.

Industry observers note that OLTP Transactions use case: Google (Spanner) placed #2 among all evaluated vendors. Spanner's horizontal scalability with full interoperability between various modalities and no-compromise strong consistency enables differentiated experiences.

In addition, Google (Spanner) placed among the 3 highest-scoring vendors across all 4 evaluated use cases in the report, which we believe demonstrates the breadth of workloads it supports across transactional, analytical, NoSQL, and agentic AI scenarios.

According to recent reports, your organization's ability to take advantage of AI depends directly on your enterprise data strategy. Autonomous agents require a modern data foundation that pairs high-performance transactional processing with in-database intelligence and vector capabilities. Google Cloud continues to set the benchmark for enterprise PostgreSQL, delivering high performance and seamless scalability, 100% PostgreSQL compatibility, and enterprise-grade reliability and security, built for agents with AlloyDB for PostgreSQL.

Industry observers note that A recent component of this data and AI foundation is PostgreSQL for agents in AlloyDB. Autonomous agents require real-time data to reason effectively, but their unpredictable and bursty query patterns can degrade performance if not isolated from operational systems. Purpose-built for this challenge, AlloyDB enables real-time data access with complete compute and I/O isolation. Powered by Google’s Colossus distributed storage, it dynamically provisions sandboxed reader instances in seconds—scaling to thousands of nodes, up to 3 million queries per second, and over 8 million IOPS—and scales back to zero when tasks finish. Paired with direct Iceberg access, agents can seamlessly operate across both live transactional data and analytical lakehouse tables in place with zero ETL.

Recent findings suggest that traditional databases have struggled to keep up with the velocity of AI development, burdened by slow provisioning, CI/CD bottlenecks, and high idle infrastructure costs. AlloyDB Express solves this with an ultra-fast, cost-effective PostgreSQL configuration purpose-built for AI coding agents, rapid prototyping, and ephemeral developer environments. Instances spin up in under two seconds and automatically scale to zero when idle, letting developers and agents iterate rapidly without paying for unused compute. When an application is ready for production, AlloyDB Express provides a seamless, one-click in-place upgrade to AlloyDB—unlocking enterprise-grade scale, high availability, and security while preserving all schemas, data, and connection endpoints.

It has been revealed that spanner's unified multi-model architecture converges relational, graph, vector, and key-value capabilities within a single, planet-scale operational foundation. Graph database structures have swiftly become essential for agentic workloads due to their ability to model complex, interconnected enterprise knowledge and relationships in real time. Spanner Graph enables owners to execute 14 popular graph algorithms — spanning centrality, community detection, similarity, and path finding — directly through the ISO Graph Query Language (GQL). To prevent performance degradation on production workloads, these algorithms run on a fully managed, isolated compute environment while Data Boost serverlessly transfers graph data. This zero-ETL architecture delivers deep structural analytics and high-performance graph processing without impacting live transactional performance.

Spanner’s columnar engine enables lightweight analytics on the most recent updated data in Spanner. Large analytical queries that require joining analytical data in BigQuery with the latest operational data from Spanner are done leveraging Data Boost, which ensures no impact to  Spanner’s transactional workloads. Complementing these capabilities, the recent launch of Spanner queues brings native transactional messaging directly into Spanner, enabling teams to manage asynchronous background work and orchestrate complex, multi-agent workflows without dual-write complexity.

This unified data ecosystem bridges the gap between intelligence and execution, allowing automated insights to immediately trigger real-time actions back into your critical business applications and operational workflows.

To prevent vendor lock-in and help ensure their long-term viability, enterprise AI deployments require a foundation of open standards and hybrid deployment flexibility. Google Cloud operational databases empower teams to run critical workloads across any environment.

At the foundation of this borderless architecture, Google Cloud databases integrate into the lakehouse ecosystem by delivering unified, zero-ETL access to operational and analytical data. Through native support for open table formats like Apache Iceberg, operational databases such as AlloyDB and Spanner bridge real-time transaction processing with lakehouse analytics. This enables autonomous agents to query, reason over, and act upon live enterprise data instantly across hybrid and multi-cloud environments, minimizing data silos while maintaining strict governance, transactional consistency, and planet-scale performance.

It has been revealed that with AlloyDB Omni and Spanner Omni, enterprises can extend Google's advanced operational database capabilities to on-premises environments, multi-cloud deployments, and air-gapped sovereign setups via Google Distributed Cloud (GDC). This helps ensure full operational consistency, compliance, and sovereignty without sacrificing performance or developer velocity.

It has been revealed that our database portfolio has also received broader industry recognition. Recently, Google was also named a Leader in the IDC MarketScape: Worldwide Operational Databases 2026 Vendor Assessment.

Recent findings suggest that “Consider Google AlloyDB when standardizing on PostgreSQL with deeply integrated AI is a priority, particularly for organizations building AI-driven search, retrieval, and agentic applications on live operational data. The platform suits enterprises that want converged transactional and analytical processing in a single engine, enterprise-grade security, and the flexibility to run the same engine across managed cloud, self-managed, and multicloud environments through AlloyDB Omni. Organizations seeking an AI-native operational database backed by Google's ecosystem will find AlloyDB a practical option for balancing modernization, AI readiness, and operational simplicity.”.

Across Spanner and AlloyDB, we believe this recognition reinforces our mission to deliver a true System of Action built to power autonomous enterprise agents at scale.
