Our pick for the best Garmin watch you can buy has also been a bestselling deal this season — both during July Prime Day and October Prime Day — and is back down to the lowest price we’ve seen this year. The Fenix 8 Pro is the flagship model that connects seamlessly to Android and iOS smartphones, as well as golf club sensors, bike sensors, and more. Just about every sport is covered with an updated smartphone experience, so you can customize the watch settings on your phone and sync them over to the watch.

The smartwatch features a vibrant AMOLED display for ultra-readability and an LED flashlight, which reviewer Matt Miller claims “makes the Fenix 8 Pro an essential tool in my collection.” The smartwatch isn’t discounted yet, but there’s good news afoot. Because Garmin recently rolled out the Fenix 9 Pro, this upcoming Black Friday could see a major slash to the price of the Fenix 8 Pro.

The Vivoactive 5 is a great choice for more casual athletes who are looking for a cost-effective Garmin option. It features 11 days of battery life and a vivid AMOLED display. Not only was it one of the top-selling smartwatches for our readers last year, but it is also on sale for its lowest price it’s been all year.

The Forerunner 165 is our pick for the best Garmin watch for casual runners and pickleball players. Our reviewer Matt Miller notes that even at this more affordable price, Garmin’s Forerunner 165 includes a brilliant AMOLED touchscreen display for that elegant Android smartwatch visual experience found in Samsung and Apple smartwatches.

Ready to run with the 47mm Garmin Epix Pro / Garmin/ZDNET.

The Epix Pro has an AMOLED display, and 30 hours of battery life, which feels low compared to the Fenix 8 Pro’s 15 days of battery life. However, the Epix Pro is half as expensive as the Fenix 8 Pro. What you lose in battery you make up for in a slightly more approachable exercise watch. It’s got that Garmin flashlight feature, which Reddit users say comes in more handy than you’d think. The case is titanium, just like the Fenix 8 Pro, so you’ll get a highly durable smartwatch in the Epix Pro. Some users say that it’s one of the best value smartwatches out of Garmin’s lineup.

Garmin introduced its first smart alarm feature with the Vivoactive 6 that wakes you at your lightest point of sleep within 30 minutes of your desired wake time. In addition, it also has more than 80 sports modes, support for wheelchair activities, nap tracking, and more, making the Vivoactive 6 a fantastic GPS sports watch option for the masses. Given the affordable price, it may be the perfect first choice for people looking to try out the Garmin ecosystem.

Also: I tested Garmin’s latest smartwatch, and it has the right balance of style and features.

The Garmin Venu 4, unlike the two Garmin watches above, serves a more casual smartwatch clientele. This would be a great first smart watch, or a capable device for tracking activity, sleep, and daily stress. It’s not as rugged as some of Garmin’s offerings, but it’s also significantly cheaper. The watch offers 10 days of battery life, and it has a more attractive, neutral build that could blend in with dressy clothing.

The Garmin Forerunner 265 is a great running watch for those with smaller wrists. The smartwatch’s price is at the moment at a 365-day low.

We’ve seen some of the lowest prices of the year on Garmin watches during sale periods like June and October Prime Day this year, and we’re expecting to see those same all-time lows, if not lower, as we approach Black Friday. Several retailers, including Walmart and Best Buy, are already running early sales, including on Garmin watches.

At 36% off, the best Black Friday deal on a Garmin watch is for the Garmin Epix Pro. With this Amazon deal, you get $360 off this Garmin Watch, making it the highest discount we’ve seen in this category.
