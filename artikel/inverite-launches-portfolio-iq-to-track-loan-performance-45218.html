📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Inverite, a Canadian risk infrastructure business that converts permissioned financial data into reliable intelligence, has rolled out Portfolio IQ, a tool built to help lenders relate past and current loan performance to the financial data and risk insights gathered when a loan was first issued.

Recent findings suggest that the newly introduced solution pushes the firm’s risk intelligence offering past the point of the initial credit decision. It achieves this by matching the information held on a borrower at origination with the way the loan actually performed over its full term.

At the heart of the launch is Inverite’s upcoming Loan Performance API. Through it, lenders can submit structured information on historical and ongoing repayment results, which can then be traced back to the original financial data, the borrower’s Default IQ score and the lending decision itself. The result is an ongoing feedback loop that equips both lenders and Inverite’s own risk team with the information required to assess how a portfolio performs as time goes on.

Lenders can apply Portfolio IQ to a range of tasks, including validating models after the fact, analysing score bands and cut-off points, monitoring portfolios, reviewing strategy and building tailored risk models.

According to recent reports, inverite CEO Karim Nanji mentioned, “Every lending decision is an opportunity to learn from the outcome. Portfolio IQ connects our predictive financial intelligence with actual loan performance, giving lenders a clearer understanding of how their risk strategies perform over time. It also expands the value of Inverite’s intelligence infrastructure by supporting our customers beyond the initial lending decision.”.
