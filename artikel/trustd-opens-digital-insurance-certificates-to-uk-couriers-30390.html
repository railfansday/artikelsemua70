Trustd opens digital insurance certificates to UK couriers

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Trustd, a digital identity and credentials verification platform serving the transport and logistics sector, has made its verifiable digital insurance certificates available to every insurer and broker working with the UK courier and haulage industry.

The service runs on Truvera, technology developed by Dock Labs, and enables insurers and brokers to issue certificates that reflect whether a courier’s policy remains active.

Recent findings suggest that rather than relying on a static document, the insurer creates a verifiable credential that is stored in the courier’s Trustd profile and can be passed on to any network the courier works with. Operators can confirm that the credential is authentic and current, and they receive a notification in Trustd or in their transport management system should the cover change.

According to recent reports, couriers and hauliers have traditionally demonstrated their insurance with a PDF certificate, and that file stays the same even after a policy has been cancelled. Trustd’s approach is designed to close this gap.

Since launching in September 2025, the service has seen 333 verifiable insurance certificates issued. It has also flagged 13 instances in which policyholders cancelled their cover, or attempted to, after receiving a certificate.

Sources indicate that business Choice Direct (BCD) became the first broker to issue the certificates, which are now relied upon across a verified network of more than 15,000 operators. According to the organization, insurers and brokers benefit from fielding fewer repeat requests for proof of cover, and they face a lower risk of cancelled policies being presented as valid.

Trustd founder and CEO Lyall Cresswell said, “Operators with compliance teams spend hours every month chasing proof of cover. Those without one have no easy way to tell whether a certificate is genuine, expired or altered. A certificate only reveals what cover looked like on the day it was issued. We’re closing that loop. A courier’s cover is now verified once, shared with every network they work with, and checked at any time. Any insurer or broker can join.”.

According to recent reports, dock Labs CEO Nick Lambert noted, “The problem with traditional insurance certificates is that the document stays the same even when the underlying policy changes. Someone can cancel their insurance and continue sharing a PDF that appears perfectly valid. Working with Trustd, we’re changing that. Operators can independently verify that a certificate is genuine and the insurance is still active, without relying on outdated documents or repeated manual checks. This is exactly the kind of problem we built Truvera to solve: making trusted information verifiable and reusable across organisations.”.
