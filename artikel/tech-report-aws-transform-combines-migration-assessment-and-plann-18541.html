Tech Report: AWS Transform combines migration assessment and planning in one experience

🌐 Source: Amazon • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (209 words, 📖 Standard)
💡 Key Takeaways
  • Sources indicate that AWS Transform for migrations now combines migration assessment and migration planning into a single migration job.
  • After you upload your inventory data, you can estimate your AWS costs and build a business case, group your infrastructure into applications and plan migration waves, or do both.
  • According to recent reports, this newest release is available in all AWS Regions where AWS Transform is offered.

Sources indicate that AWS Transform for migrations now combines migration assessment and migration planning into a single migration job. Previously, you ran separate jobs to build a total cost of ownership (TCO) business case and to plan your migration. With this launch, you upload your on-premises inventory once and use it for both assessment and planning, in any order.

After you upload your inventory data, you can estimate your AWS costs and build a business case, group your infrastructure into applications and plan migration waves, or do both. You can rerun either workflow as your data improves. For example, build a business case first and then plan waves from the same inventory, or plan your waves first and then estimate the cost of a single wave. Because assessment and planning share one inventory, your business case and migration plan stay consistent without moving data between jobs. After you finalize your plan and assessment, you can continue in the same job to migrate your networks, deploy landing zones, and migrate servers, storage, and containers.

According to recent reports, this newest release is available in all AWS Regions where AWS Transform is offered. To learn more about migrating with AWS Transform, visit the product page and documentation.
