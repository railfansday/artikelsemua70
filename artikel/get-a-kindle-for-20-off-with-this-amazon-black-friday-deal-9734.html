4If reading more without the hassle of carrying a bunch of paperbacks has been on your mind, now’s a good time to pick up an Amazon Kindle. The 2024 Kindle with 16GB of storage is now 20% cheaper on Amazon, sitting at $119.99 instead of the usual $149.99.

Both Matcha and Black colors are discounted, and you don’t have to be a Prime member to get the discount.

Industry observers note that the 6-inch screen here is about 25% brighter at its maximum setting than the previous version. It features an anti-glare coating, which cuts down on reflections when you’re reading somewhere well-lit, so sessions by the beach or the pool while soaking up some sun won’t have you squinting at the screen. There’s even a dark mode that inverts the colors. This can be nice when you’re finishing up a chapter late at night and a brighter screen feels a bit uncomfortable.

The battery lasts up to six weeks on a single charge, meaning you can go quite a while between charging sessions.

Sources indicate that ZDNET Editor Nina Raemont got her hands on this Kindle when this model first came out and remarked she greatly appreciated the responsiveness. “The page-turning and book-loading speeds were significantly quicker than on Kindle Oasis or Kindle Paperwhite from 2022,” she wrote in her review. “I tested my old Paperwhite and my fresh Kindle and found that the new Kindle turned pages a smidgen quicker than the Paperwhite.”.

Raemont also especially recommends it to anyone buying their first e-reader, since this one covers all the basics without overcomplicating things.

Sources indicate that note that you don’t need a subscription to use your e-reader; just buy the titles you want to read, or download the free options from Amazon. But if you’re a heavy reader, a Kindle subscription might be a better option. For about $12 a month, it opens up a huge library of books to borrow from, so you can keep going without having to pay for each separately.

This 20% discount shaves $30 off the model’s usual $149.99 price. We’ve seen the 2024 Kindle go for less in the past, but this offer still makes getting one easier on your pocket, so we’d rate this deal 3/5.
