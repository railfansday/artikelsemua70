📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

It has been revealed that early owners have created over 3 million images to date and helped us improve our safety processes. We’re excited to begin adding up to 1,000 new users from our waitlist each week.

Illustration: Justin Jay Wang × DALL·E.

Last month, we started previewing DALL·E 2 to a limited number of trusted owners to learn about the technology’s capabilities and limitations.

In a recent development, since then, we’ve been working with our users to actively incorporate the lessons we learn. As of today:.

Our users have collectively created over 3 million images with DALL·E.

We’ve enhanced our safety system, improving the text filters and tuning the automated detection & response system for content policy violations.

Less than 0.05% of downloaded or publicly shared images were flagged as potentially violating our content policy. About 30% of those flagged images were confirmed by human reviewers to be policy violations, leading to an account deactivation.

Sources indicate that as we work to understand and address the biases that DALL·E has inherited from its training data, we’ve asked early users not to share photorealistic generations that include faces and to flag problematic generations. We believe this has been effective in limiting potential harm, and we initiative to continue the practice in the current phase.

Learning from real-world use is an notable part⁠ of our commitment to develop and deploy AI responsibly, so we’re starting to widen access to customers who joined our waitlist, slowly but steadily.

According to recent reports, we intend to onboard up to 1,000 people every week as we iterate on our safety system and require all consumers to abide by our content policy⁠(opens in a new window). We hope to increase the rate at which we onboard new users as we learn more and gain confidence in our safety system. We’re inspired by what our users have created with DALL·E so far, and excited to see what new users will create.

In the meantime, you can get a preview of these creations on our Instagram account: @openaidalle⁠(opens in a new window).
