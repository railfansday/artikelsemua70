Breaking: When Can a Rewiring Control Test LLM Weight Readers? C2 and an Eligibility Protocol

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 26 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (290 words, 📖 Standard)

Sources indicate that arXiv:2611.29120v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Large language models can predict the outputs of small neural networks from raw weights, but success could reflect recognition of weight-space regularities (fingerprints) rather than computation, and function-preserving controls cannot tell these apart. We propose C2, a counterfactual that permutes the columns of a network's final linear readout: every other parameter, and the readout's singular values, norms and entry multiset, are unchanged, and the function can change in an exactly recomputable way; we verify that each evaluated permutation does. C2 separates fingerprinting from computation only under three conditions, which we state as an eligibility protocol: a strategy restricted to C2-invariant summaries must already predict the target; C2 must change a property specific to the rewiring; and the reader's identity must be attested. No natural setting we tested is eligible. On tiny MLPs a frontier reader route followed the rewired function on all 24 networks, but invariant baselines were near chance and its backend could not be attested. In two public CNN zoos the conditions traded off: weight statistics predicted CIFAR-10 accuracy in-distribution, but C2's effect on accuracy was not specific to the swap, while per-image targets were specific but not predictable from invariant summaries. On a constructed population where a planted fingerprint rule works, all conditions hold and C2 separates a rule-following reader from an executor. A self-hosted Qwen3.8-27B followed the rewired function rather than the shortcut, and showed no evidence of using the weights with thinking disabled. A registered replication on fresh networks confirmed both findings (tracking minus stuck 0.875, 95% interval [0.750, 1.000]), and DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-14B also followed the rewiring (tracking minus stuck 0.729 [0.552, 0.885]). The transforms, audits and raw records are available from the authors.
