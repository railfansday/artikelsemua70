HuluOPD: Unifying Heterogeneous Medical Specialists into One Lightweight Model with Unlabeled Prompts

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 26 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (205 words, 📖 Standard)

arXiv:2611.32021v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Specialist medical vision-language models (VLMs) are strong within their specialties but hard to unify: their training data are often private, and independently developed specialists cannot be assumed to share an architecture. Deploying these large models separately is costly. We present HuluOPD, an on-policy distillation recipe that unifies heterogeneous specialists into one lightweight VLM using only unlabeled prompts, without requiring reference answers or the specialists' training data. The lightweight model answers each prompt, and the specialist of the prompt's modality supervises its response; a continuation-aligned cross-tokenizer loss makes this possible even when they differ in architecture and tokenizer. We apply HuluOPD to five specialists, for ultrasound, pathology, optical coherence tomography, fundus photography, and dental imaging. To distill and evaluate them, we build HuluOPDCorpus, 914k unlabeled prompts, and HuluOPDBench, 47k held-out test items, from over 90 public datasets, and score all models under one protocol on 132k test items, including third-party benchmarks. A single model with 10% of the five specialists' combined parameters retains 94% of their question-answering performance, and one with 2% retains 89%. To our knowledge, this is the first demonstration of unifying heterogeneous medical specialist VLMs without requiring their training data or a shared architecture.
