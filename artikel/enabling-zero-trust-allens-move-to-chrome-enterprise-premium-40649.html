In a recent development, vice President – IT & Cybersecurity, ALLEN Career Institute Pvt. Ltd.

Add advanced security protections on top of streamlined management with Chrome Enterprise Premium.

Editor’s note: Founded in 1988 in Kota, Rajasthan, ALLEN is one of India’s leading coaching institutions, serving over 400,000 students across 350+ classroom campuses in 78 cities. Here, the IT and Cybersecurity Leadership Team shares how they standardized on Chrome Enterprise Premium to secure sensitive data and enable zero trust across over 5,000 consumers.

Sources indicate that with a workforce that lives in the browser across a SaaS and multi-cloud estate, securing our digital environment is paramount. Our 5,000+ users include faculty, operations, engineering, contractors, and a large Bring Your Own Device (BYOD) population.

Operating at this scale, our top priority is protecting student personally identifiable information (PII) flowing through web applications. Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA), protecting this data is critical to regulatory compliance and maintaining brand trust. We needed security within the browser.

The challenge of a fragmented browser estate.

We previously operated a mixed-browser environment, but managing multiple browsers made consistent policy enforcement and visibility challenging. We explored several options to standardize; however, our team didn’t want consumers to have to learn a fresh tool, and many of the options had high overhead costs.

Testing and rolling out Chrome Enterprise Premium.

We implemented a phased Chrome Enterprise Premium pilot over a few weeks. Our team first validated the management capabilities in the Google Admin console by testing policy, deployment, extension controls, and reporting. Once we felt confident that Chrome Enterprise Premium provided our desired level of visibility, we rolled it out to several hundred customers across various environments, including BYOD, Windows, and macOS. We deployed Safe Browsing and URL filtering for immediate threat protection, DLP rules to restrict copy/paste, downloads, and uploads on sensitive SaaS apps, and context-aware access to restrict application entry based on device posture.

The proof of concept succeeded across all criteria including consistent policy enforcement, measurable reduction in risky-site and risky-download events, minimal user friction, and a centralized dashboard to give our teams visibility into managed user activity.

It has been revealed that the real decision moment came when we realized we could achieve a true Zero Trust posture. With Chrome Enterprise Premium as our backbone, access decisions now happen dynamically based on identity, device posture, and context. A teacher on a school-managed laptop enjoys seamless, full access. On a personal phone, that same educator can review materials without compromising security—downloads and clipboard sharing remain locked down. It delivers airtight data protection without disrupting daily instruction.

According to recent reports, with Chrome Enterprise Premium, we replaced virtual desktops (VDIs) and fragmented security tools with built-in browser protection. Securing the browser itself means we no longer need to install heavy security software on every computer. This makes it easy to apply security policies to personal devices used by contractors and employees. Whether our team uses Windows, macOS, ChromeOS, iOS, or Android, we manage updates, policies, and audit reports from a single Google Admin console.

Industry observers note that we use Chrome Enterprise’s built-in features, such as Safe Browsing sandboxing and site isolation, and auto-updates to keep end people secure while they work on the web. These benefits plus Chrome Enterprise Premium prove that security does not have to slow down business. Employees work without security interruptions, and our IT team gets a single, clear audit trail across all devices. This helps us maintain high efficiency and supports our regulatory compliance efforts.
