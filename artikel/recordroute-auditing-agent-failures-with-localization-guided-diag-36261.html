RecordRoute: Auditing Agent Failures with Localization-Guided Diagnosis

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 26 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (162 words, 📖 Standard)

Industry observers note that arXiv:2611.33022v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Auditing failed agent executions requires identifying the responsible actor, the earliest decisive step, and the error mode. Representative supervised methods either focus on step localization or predict multiple attribution targets from shared trajectory representations. These methods do not use the localization result to select the text for mode diagnosis. To address this limitation, we propose RecordRoute, which couples comparative localization with local diagnosis. Specifically, it represents each record with word and character features and learns to rank records through comparisons within each trajectory. The selected record supplies the actor and step, while its text and metadata drive mode prediction. On controlled-injection Who&When Pro, RecordRoute outperforms StepFinder and ASCon on all four official metrics. With actor and step predictions fixed, local diagnosis reaches 77.47% pooled joint accuracy versus 69.28% for full-trace diagnosis. On natural failures from TraceElephant and CatchBench POST, it outperforms these baselines and three open LLMs in localization.
