Managing Director, Global Partner Marketing.

The front door to AI in the workplace.

At Google Cloud, our commitment to agentic transformation extends to everyone, including the partners who build and grow alongside us.

Today, we are excited to introduce fresh Gemini-powered co-marketing capabilities directly within everyday go-to-market workflows for our partners. These new features enable partners to produce customized, brand-aligned joint assets in minutes, scaling campaign execution and driving pipeline more effectively.

According to recent reports, as technology evolves and customer expectations shift, marketers are no longer just producing campaigns. We’re building continuous, hyper-personalized, data-driven demand engines, and we’re doing it across an increasingly fragmented buyer journey.

For our partner network, delivering on this expectation means solving a dual challenge: differentiating core offerings while articulating a unified vision with Google Cloud.

Bridging that gap at enterprise scale requires more than incremental efficiency — it requires agentic agility to jointly create, adapt, and distribute stories together that resonate.

The updated Partner Marketing Studio introduces a suite of agentic tools designed to accelerate campaign execution, optimize performance, and maximize impact for partners of all sizes.

Agentic search: Partners can now use a simple chat feature. Search for what you need, rather than navigating a content repository.

Brand alignment: Never worry about brand compliance again, with a brand reviewer agent that helps ensure your content upholds Google Cloud’s partner brand guidelines.

ISV asset creation: Gemini generated, high-fidelity marketing assets to drive demand to marketplace listings.

Social automation: A upcoming social editor with the ability to create posts across multiple networks simultaneously, add account tags, and refine messaging per channel with AI.

Customer case studies: An agent designed to build best-in-class customer success stories to highlight joint customer wins.

Gemini-Assisted Translation: Accelerate global marketing programs with agentic translations across 130 languages.

Natural language analytics: Gain end-to-end visibility connecting marketing execution directly to tangible business outcomes.

Recent findings suggest that "Partner Marketing Studio has become an extension of our partner marketing team. With built-in Gemini capabilities and ready-to-run joint campaigns, we can tailor high-impact marketing assets across industries, regions, and personas in minutes. It allows us to do more with less while accelerating pipeline for our joint solutions" - Liana Shuma, Global Head of Technology Partnerships, Pigment.

While the platform features powerful latest tools, our core focus remains unchanged: helping you build your brand authority and generate pipeline demand.

It has been revealed that you will continue to find a curated content library structured specifically for you, highlighting top-performing campaigns aligned with Google Cloud strategic priority plays. Partners maintain full access to Google-produced localized campaign content and ready-made assets that can be customized to suit specific go-to-market objectives.

Furthermore, high-value partner services remain readily accessible within the platform:.

Applying to host customer events directly inside a Google office.

Requesting a Google Cloud speaker for your hosted events.

Attending dedicated office-hours sessions with Google Cloud marketing experts.

Requesting social amplification for campaigns.

According to recent reports, “Google offices are not only a great draw for attendees, but by handling all event logistics, Google Cloud saved us significant time, money, and allowed us to focus entirely on delivering high-impact content and driving audience engagement” - Jenny Bejarano. GTM Specialist, Tiger Analytics.

“We appreciate Google Cloud’s support with speakers for our events. This collaboration strengthens our credibility and value proposition with prospects” - Tommaso Vaccarella, Co-Founder & General Manager, Connected Stories.

Via Google Cloud Partner Network: Navigate to Resources > Marketing > Partner Marketing Studio.

Via direct browser link: Visit www.partnermarketingstudio.com directly.

It has been revealed that these updates are part of Google Cloud’s larger commitment to transforming how we empower and enable partners with Gemini. In addition to Partner Marketing Studio, we’re leveraging Gemini throughout the partner experience, including a revamped partner marketing funds experience to streamline activity planning, simplify pipeline reporting, and proactively validate required documentation before submission.

In a recent development, we’ve seen Google Cloud experience tremendous growth in recent years, and a major part of that success has been the incredible work done by our partner ecosystem. Now, as we’re bringing the power of Gemini directly into your go-to-market workflows, we’re excited to take our work together to a whole upcoming level.

With AI at our side, and as one of our strongest offerings, it will be easier than ever to create, connect, and grow together.
