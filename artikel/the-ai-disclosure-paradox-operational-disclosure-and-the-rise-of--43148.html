The AI Disclosure Paradox: Operational Disclosure and the Rise of Corporate AI Narratives

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 27 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (144 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

arXiv:2611.33201v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: AI-related corporate disclosure can expand while becoming less concentrated on current use. Across U.S. SEC filings from 2017 to 2025, AI-related passage intensity increased 6.3-fold between 2017-2022 and 2023-2025, whereas corrected operational-disclosure intensity increased 3.3-fold. The operational share fell from 25.76% to 13.45%; approximately 89% of the net increase in passage intensity fell outside the operational category. The decline persists within sectors and filing types and in 10-K-only analysis. These estimates combine frozen language-model predictions for 26,279 AI-related passages with an audit of 1,000 annotated passages. The findings distinguish growth in organizational attention from growth in implementation-specific claims: more operational disclosure can coexist with a substantially lower operational share. Operational disclosure was thus diluted, not displaced, by nonoperational disclosure. Reading corporate technology narratives therefore requires attention to both disclosure intensity and the organizational state described.
