Latest Update: Higgsfield takes AI video from 1 minute to over 10

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Higgsfield brings creators longer videos with GPT‑6 Astra, precise visuals with GPT‑Image‑2.5, and a path to voice filmmaking with GPT‑Live.

According to recent reports, higgsfield AI⁠(opens in a new window) is on a mission to make professional video and image creation accessible to anyone with an idea. Before GPT‑6 Astra, those videos topped out at around one minute. Now, says CEO and co-founder Alex Mashrabov, consumers can create videos over 10 minutes long.

Longer videos are just one way Higgsfield AI helps smaller creative teams take on more ambitious work. According to Alex, with models like GPT‑6 Astra and GPT‑Image‑2.5, it’s now possible for a single creator to execute high-end fashion campaigns from start to finish.

Higgsfield AI’s creative agents take on repetitive production tasks, such as creating multiple variations of a shot. With GPT‑6 Astra, those agents can also call external tools to handle complex, manual work in 3D modeling. Alex claims that capability helps address one of creative AI’s hardest problems: maintaining consistency across longer videos.

Recent findings suggest that being able to create longer instructional videos also matters to Alex personally. He supports rural schools in Kazakhstan and describes an early effort using GPT‑6 Astra’s visual understanding to identify topics students may be struggling with and create videos tailored to what they need to learn.

According to recent reports, for small businesses creating promotional content, having readable text and precise infographics can be just as notable as the image itself. GPT‑Image‑2.5 makes that kind of content possible for businesses without large production budgets, Alex states.

“GPT‑Image‑2.5 is state of the art and the first model of its kind that can reliably render the text and infographics on top of the imagery,” he says.

It has been revealed that higgsfield AI builds its product through close collaboration between around 200 creatives and 100 engineers. Codex gives the creative team “the power of a really strong software engineer,” shares Alex.

Creatives use Codex to turn their ideas into software prototypes. Previously, bringing those prototypes into production required heavy involvement from front-end and back-end engineers. Now, Alex says GPT‑6 Astra reduces the work needed to bring those ideas into the product.

With less friction between prototyping and production, Alex notes Higgsfield AI can develop and ship better products faster.

Next, Higgsfield AI wants creators to be able to describe an idea and see it take shape as they talk. Alex calls that vision “voice to movie,” with AI understanding a creator’s spoken intention and rendering their vision in real time.

He sees OpenAI’s advances in planning, tool calling, image generation, and voice bringing that experience closer. He describes its voice model as “best in class for voice understanding and just for regular communication.”.

For Higgsfield AI, the ambition is to make creating a film feel more like describing an idea, with AI handling more of the work in between.
