As someone who covers consumer tech for a living, I get new smartphones fairly often. But most consumers don’t upgrade at the same pace. With each passing year, their phones can start slowing down. If that’s you, there are a few quick ways to speed up your old Android phone today before you hand over money for a new one.

Industry observers note that my top tips are easy to implement for both tech-savvy consumers and the average Joe, with no rooting required. They include clearing storage in a couple of ways, checking for updates, and tweaking your animation speed. More on that below.

In a recent development, phones can become cluttered with a lot of unused apps over time. You might have an old QR scanner app you no longer need or a photo editing app you’ve since replaced. It’s best to uninstall any apps you’re not using, both to clear space and for security reasons.

Also: The best anti-AI smartphones in 2026.

Sources indicate that decluttering your Android phone can make it faster because it won’t need to allocate resources to apps you are no longer using. Uninstalling such APKs will lead to better resource management and thus, a faster phone. You can go to Settings > Storage > Apps to see the list of installed apps and uninstall the ones you no longer need. On the app screen, you can also long-press on such forgotten apps and go to App Info > Uninstall.

In a recent development, old Android phones can also accumulate a lot of files and long-forgotten photos and screenshots you don’t need anymore. After deleting unused apps, aim to free up space by reviewing the files and photos on your Android phone.

Also: How to clear your Android phone cache.

Industry observers note that you can go to your phone’s Files app on Pixel or My Files on Galaxy to check what’s saved. The Downloads folder often contains the most junk, including long-forgotten PDF files, photos, etc. There are two more spaces you should check to clear the storage.

Sources indicate that clear the app cache. Cache is bits of data that an app saves so it can operate more quickly the next time you open it. I recommend clearing the cache for less-used apps with no saved login information. You can go to Settings > Storage > App > Clear Cache to save more space.

It has been revealed that make sure your Android phone is up to date on the OS front. Keeping it up to date will make it snappier and ensure it uses the latest security protocols. You can go to Settings > General > System Update to check and install the latest available software version.

Once you’ve removed apps you don’t need and updated your old Android phone to the latest OS version, go to the Google Play Store and update your remaining apps. It is important to use the latest versions of these apps to access the latest features, improve performance, and enhance security.

In a recent development, you can enable or disable a few settings to make your phone feel faster, too. One of those is the animation speed via Developer options. This is slightly more complicated than the rest of the settings mentioned above, but it can make your phone feel significantly faster.

First, you need to enable Developer options by going to Settings > About phone > Tap on Software information > and then tap your Build number seven times. This should enable Developer options, allowing you to access different animation scale settings.

Sources indicate that now, go to the Drawing section within Developer options and scroll down until you find the Window animation scale. Tap on it and set the animation to 0.5x, so apps and window pop-ups run faster.

It has been revealed that you can also adjust the Transition animation scale to minimize the speed of moving between home screen panels and within apps. Plus, minimize the Animator duration scale to make opening and closing apps, unlocking the phone, and other general UI animations snappier.

Sources indicate that changing these settings will give your old Android phone a new life. Once adjusted, it should feel faster, with added zip in day-to-day tasks. If you want to give it a refresh, I recommend messing around with your home screen, changing the wallpaper, and adjusting more settings to help it last longer.
