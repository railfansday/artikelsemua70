Breaking: Addendum to GPT-5 System Card: Sensitive conversations

🌐 Source: Openai • 📅 27 Oct 2025 • ⏱️ 1 min read (165 words, 📖 Standard)

When we launched GPT‑5, we noted⁠ in the system card that we were working to establish better benchmarks and to continue to strengthen model safety in areas related to mental and emotional distress. On October 3, we deployed an enhancement⁠(opens in a newly introduced window) that reflected those efforts, improving ChatGPT’s default model to better recognize and support people in moments of distress. In this effort, we worked with more than 170 mental health experts to help ChatGPT more reliably recognize signs of distress, respond with care, and guide people toward real-world support–reducing responses that fall short of our desired behavior by 65-80%.

Recent findings suggest that we are publishing a related blog post⁠ that gives more information about this work, and this addendum to the GPT‑5 system card to share baseline safety evaluations. These evaluations compare the August 15 version⁠(opens in a upcoming window) of ChatGPT’s default model, also known as GPT‑5 Instant, to the updated one launched October 3.
