Helping OpenAI grow and adapt as AI changes how work gets done.

We’re excited to welcome Arvind KC to OpenAI as our Chief People Officer.

Sources indicate that KC brings a rare combination of engineering depth and people leadership. Over the course of his career, he has held senior roles at Roblox, Google, Palantir Technologies, and Meta, helping build both products and the organizations behind them at meaningful scale. He understands how high-performing technical teams operate and how strong, practical systems can help people do their best work without slowing them down.

KC's role will be to help OpenAI grow in a way that supports the people doing the work, from the foundations of hiring, onboarding, and development, to the systems and policies that make it easier to collaborate, move rapidly, and sustain high performance.

As AI reshapes how work gets done, our Chief People Officer will be central to how we navigate that transition responsibly. We believe we have both an opportunity and an obligation, to our own incredible talent and to society, to provide a model for how companies can navigate the transition to more AI-enabled work that expands what people can do with AI and invest in their growth. This work spans how roles evolve, how newly introduced skills are developed, and how education and re-skilling can keep pace with technological change. Over time, we strategy to share what we learn with our customers and partners, and hopefully help the broader economy move from theory to practice as AI increasingly becomes part of everyday work.
