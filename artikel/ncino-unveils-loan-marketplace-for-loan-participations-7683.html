nCino unveils Loan Marketplace for loan participations

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

nCino, the agentic AI banking platform, has made its Loan Marketplace for participations generally available to US financial institutions.

Banks and credit unions that already use nCino Portfolio Analytics can access the upcoming product straight away within their existing nCino instance.

The marketplace extends the functionality of Portfolio Analytics, a tool more than 1,000 financial institutions currently rely on for loan-level performance insight, individualised forward-looking risk modelling, balance sheet management and compliance with the Current Expected Credit Losses standard.

Industry observers note that since each institution’s loan information is already standardised within its Portfolio Analytics environment, there is no need to recreate a loan tape in a spreadsheet or separate application. Sellers can assemble pools directly from their own portfolio data, while prospective buyers can examine a pool’s analytics, along with risk and performance details for each individual loan, before submitting an offer.

The result is a single destination where institutions can sell, purchase and oversee participations, covering everything from pre-commitment pool analysis to post-closing remittance calculation and validation.

It has been revealed that on the selling side, the platform offers guided pool building based on eligibility criteria and retention rules set by the seller. It also provides pool analytics spanning concentration, credit risk, gross yield and pricing sensitivity, as well as role-based approval controls that record who created and who signed off each listing.

Buyers, meanwhile, can review pools using loan-level risk and performance analytics, export a list of the loans involved and work through a due diligence process in which expressions of interest, letters of intent and supporting documentation are exchanged inside the marketplace.

Industry observers note that for ongoing management, a guided monthly reconciliation highlights any discrepancies and demands seller review and approval before funds are released. Owners also gain a consistent view of performance, delinquency, charge-offs, maturity and concentration for pools traded via the marketplace, as well as for sold pools brought in from other sources.

Participations have become an notable lever for balance sheet management, with institutions often mapping out purchases and sales months in advance to hit portfolio goals such as loan-to-share or loan-to-deposit ratios when fresh lending falls short. They also help firms tackle concentration risk by broadening their portfolios beyond the loans they can originate themselves. At 31 October 2026, nCino’s Portfolio Analytics customers held over $21bn in loan participations and trade substantial volumes annually. However, the supporting tools have lagged behind.

Sources indicate that according to nCino, brokers, listing boards and existing participation marketplaces typically do not hold the underlying portfolio data. As a result, buyers frequently price pools without proper visibility into the loans they contain, and post-sale servicing often depends on spreadsheets sent by email in varying formats. By linking the marketplace to each institution’s own data in Portfolio Analytics, nCino aims to introduce more transparency, automation and efficiency to a market historically driven by personal contacts, phone calls and spreadsheets.

nCino serves more than 2,700 customers globally, ranging from community banks, credit unions and independent mortgage banks to some of the world’s largest financial organisations. Its agentic platform is designed specifically for financial services and other regulated sectors, pairing AI agents with human staff to help institutions remove inefficiencies, improve decision-making and achieve better results for their customers.

Sources indicate that nCino CEO Sean Desmond remarked, “Participation trading keeps growing across the industry. Because we already hold each institution’s loan-level performance data through Portfolio Analytics, sellers and buyers can analyze a pool in detail before they commit. That data foundation is what sets nCino Loan Marketplace apart, and it gives our customers a better path to diversified, efficient and healthier balance sheets.”.

Recent findings suggest that amplify Credit Union chief lending officer JC San Pedro stated, “What stands out about the Marketplace is the combination of data and reach. We get consistent, loan-level detail on every pool, and we’re connected to a network of over one thousand financial institutions. That means we can find the right counterparties faster and evaluate opportunities with real transparency.”.
