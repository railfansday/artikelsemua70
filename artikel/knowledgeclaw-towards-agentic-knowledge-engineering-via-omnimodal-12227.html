KnowledgeClaw: Towards Agentic Knowledge Engineering via Omnimodal Hypergraphs

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 20 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (156 words, 📖 Standard)

arXiv:2611.24294v1 Announce Type: fresh Abstract: Knowledge engineering (KE) turns raw files into a reasoning-ready knowledge base, a task now within reach of LLM agents. KE has progressed from chunk and graph retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) to memory agents, agentic KE and multimodal KE. However, a live knowledge base needs omnimodality, a closed-loop lifecycle and multi-granular application at once, yet existing systems meet at most two and no benchmark measures all three. We formalize Omnimodal Knowledge Engineering (OmniKE) and release OmniKE-Bench, six tasks with 1,829 model-free items over 3,960 real files in five modalities. We further propose KnowledgeClaw, an agentic system that builds an omnimodal typed hypergraph, keeps it current with a closed-loop lifecycle agent and routes each query to the granularity it needs. Against 30 baselines, it ranks first on all six tasks with an overall OKE of 55.9, +14.6 over the strongest baseline. Our software and data are publicly available at https://github.com/LHRLAB/KnowledgeClaw.
