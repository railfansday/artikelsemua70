UCB exploration via Q-ensembles

🌐 Source: Openai • 📅 05 Jun 2017 • ⏱️ 1 min read (62 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

In a recent development, we show how an ensemble of Q*-functions can be leveraged for more effective exploration in deep reinforcement learning. We build on well established algorithms from the bandit setting, and adapt them to the Q-learning setting. We propose an exploration strategy based on upper-confidence bounds (UCB). Our experiments show significant gains on the Atari benchmark.
